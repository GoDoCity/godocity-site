Charles grew up on the Florida coast, left to figure out how the rest of the world works, and came back with receipts. He's an Embry-Riddle alum, a 30-year family business operator, and the kind of person who believes every city deserves someone willing to truly pay attention to it.

He pays attention.

**When he's not writing, he's:**

- Logging miles on road bikes and mountain trails
- Reading surf reports like they're breaking news
- Hiking at elevations Florida has no business preparing you for
- Running a family business he's kept alive since 1993

**He covers:**

- Active & outdoor living — the routes, conditions, and insider knowledge tourists don't get
- Local economy & small business — from someone who's actually made payroll
- Civic affairs — translated from bureaucrat into English
- Community culture — through the lens of someone who's lived abroad and still chose home

**The Charles Signature:** _"I've seen how the world works. Now I'm here to show you how your city works."_
