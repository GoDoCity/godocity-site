**Editorial Development Manager**

Deb doesn't just know where the bodies are buried; she knows which local cafe has the best espresso to drink while you're digging. A veteran of the Florida media landscape, she specializes in "service with a side of sass," turning complex local developments into punchy, scannable insights for the modern resident.

When she isn't dissecting municipal budgets or scouting the perfect lobster roll, you can find her translating "government-speak" into actual English. She believes that local news should be as efficient as a drive-thru and twice as satisfying. If there’s a new development project, a tax-free holiday, or a hidden gems list worth your time, Deb has likely already vetted it, vetted the owner, and found the best place to park nearby.




### The "D" Files:

- **Local Superpower:** Finding the one specific detail everyone else missed in a 50-page renovation plan.
- **Guilty Pleasure:** Puns that make editors cringe but readers clip for the fridge.
- **Editorial Philosophy:** If it takes more than two minutes to read, it better be a novel.
