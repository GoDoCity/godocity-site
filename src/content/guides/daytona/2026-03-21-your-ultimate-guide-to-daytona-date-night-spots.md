## The Sunset Seekers

There is something about the Halifax River at dusk that just sets the mood. If you're looking for a view to go with your drinks, these are our top picks:

- **Our Deck Seafood Yacht Club**: Located in Port Orange, this is where you go for a "casual-cool" vibe. Sit outside, watch the boats, and catch the orange glow over the water.
- **Caribbean Jack's**: A Daytona staple. It offers a massive deck, live music, and front-row seats to the Intracoastal.
- **The Casements (Pro-Tip)**: Before dinner, take a romantic stroll through the gardens of John D. Rockefeller's former winter home in Ormond. It's the best free view in the county.

## The Foodie Favorites

If the goal is a meal you'll be talking about for a week, these three spots deliver on both atmosphere and plate presentation:

- **The Cellar**: This is the "Gold Standard" for romance. Located in the historic home of Warren G. Harding, this upscale Italian spot is intimate, quiet, and world-class.
- **63 Sovereign**: For the couple that wants a modern, "Big City" vibe. This Ormond gastropub has incredible seasonal cocktails and a chic, moody interior.
- **Rose Villa**: A historic Victorian house on Granada that serves Southern-inspired fare. The wrap-around porch is perfect for a breezy evening.

## The After-Hours

Don't let the night end just because the plates are cleared. Keep the vibe going here:

- **The Sky Lounge at Streamline Hotel**: Head to the rooftop where NASCAR was founded. You get 360-degree views of the Atlantic and the city lights.
- **Ormond Garage**: If you want something a bit more high-energy, this craft brewery pays homage to Daytona's racing roots and usually has a great local crowd.

## GoDo Pro-Tips

- **The Parking Hack**: When hitting Granada Blvd in Ormond, skip the street hunt. Park in the large public lot behind the shops on the South side of the street — it's a 2-minute walk and zero stress.
- **The Golden Hour Drive**: If you have time before dinner, drive the **Ormond Loop**. The canopy of oaks just before sunset is the perfect "opening act" for a great night.

## The Vibe Check

Daytona's date night scene is all about "Elevated Casual." You don't need a tuxedo for The Cellar, but a nice button-down or sundress fits the mood perfectly. Most spots on this list are walkable once you park, so wear comfortable but stylish shoes!
