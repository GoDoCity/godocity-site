**\## The Route: Where to Start**

The most iconic way to experience the Loop is to start at the intersection of \*\*High Bridge Road and A1A\*\*. From there, you head West across the bridge. This section offers an incredible view of the salt marshes and the Halifax River. If you’re lucky, you’ll catch a glimpse of a manatee or a leaping mullet near the shoreline.



**\## The Canopy: The "Tunnel of Oaks"**

Once you turn South onto \*\*Old Dixie Highway\*\*, you enter the most photographed part of the drive. The "Tunnel of Oaks" features ancient, moss-draped trees that completely arch over the road. 

**\* \*\*Pro-Tip\****: This section is a low-speed zone. It’s perfect for windows-down cruising, but keep an eye out for cyclists and runners—this is their favorite morning playground.



**\## Historic Stops: James Ormond Park**

Nestled along the route is \*\*James Ormond Park\*\*, the site of an old plantation and the resting place of its namesake. It’s a quiet, shaded spot perfect for a mid-drive stretch.

**\* \*\*The "Sugar Mill" Connection\****: Just down the road, you can visit the ruins of the Bulow Plantation, which offers a deeper look into the region's agricultural past before the 1830s.



**\## Nature & Wildlife: Bulow Creek State Park**

Home to the \*\*Fairchild Oak\*\*, one of the largest live oaks in the Southern United States, this park is a mandatory stop. The tree is over 400 years old and provides a massive, sprawling canopy that is a popular spot for wedding photos and quiet reflection.

**\* \*\*Birding\****: Keep your binoculars ready for Ospreys, Great Blue Herons, and the occasional Bald Eagle nesting in the pine trees.



**\## The Beachside Finish**

As the Loop winds back toward the Atlantic, you’ll eventually hit \*\*North Ocean Shore Blvd\*\*. This completes the circle. We recommend ending your trip by pulling into a beach approach to watch the waves. The transition from the deep, dark oak forest to the bright orange sands of Ormond is the best "reveal" in Florida.



**\## GoDo Loop-Tips**

**\* \*\*Timing the Light\****: The best light is the "Golden Hour" (about 60 minutes before sunset). The sunlight filtering through the Spanish moss is unbeatable.

**\* \*\*Fuel & Snacks\****: There are no gas stations on the Loop itself. Make sure your tank is ready and grab a coffee in downtown Ormond before you head North.
