## Sunset Seekers

The best views in Volusia County come with a golden hour you won't forget. These waterfront spots nail the vibe — whether you're looking for craft cocktails with a dock view or a leisurely stroll before dinner.

### The Cellar

Tucked beneath street level on Volusia Avenue, **The Cellar** is Daytona's most atmospheric date night destination. Stone walls, candlelit tables, and a wine list that actually surprises you. The rack of lamb is a perennial favorite, and the service has an old-school formality that feels intentional, not stuffy. Make a reservation — it fills up fast on weekends.

- **Address:** 220 Magnolia Ave, Daytona Beach, FL 32114
- **Vibe:** Romantic, upscale, unhurried
- **Best for:** Anniversaries, first impressions, special occasions

## Pro Tips

A few things the locals know that the tourists don't.

**Book early on race weekends.** Every good restaurant in a 20-mile radius fills up weeks in advance during Bike Week, the Daytona 500, and Rolex 24. If your date night falls anywhere near those windows, make a reservation the moment you know your plans.

**The patio season is real.** Daytona's winters are mild enough that outdoor seating is usable most of the year. Ask for patio when you call — the indoor experience at many spots is fine, but the outdoor tables are often the reason locals choose a place.

**Tuesday and Wednesday are the sweet spots.** Weekend crowds are real. Mid-week visits mean better service, shorter waits, and a quieter atmosphere — especially at spots like The Cellar where the room itself is part of the experience.

**Trust the specials.** At seasonal-menu spots, the off-menu items are usually what the kitchen is most excited about. Ask your server what's new — it's almost never a mistake.

## Parking Hack

Daytona's downtown and beachside areas have solid free and low-cost parking if you know where to look.

**Downtown (Beach Street corridor):** The city lots along Orange Avenue and the garages near City Hall are free after 5 PM on weeknights and all day on weekends. Walk two blocks and you're in the middle of everything.

**Seabreeze Boulevard / beachside:** Street parking on the side streets off Seabreeze opens up by 6 PM. Avoid the beachfront meters — they run late and get expensive. Head one block inland and the spots are usually free and untimed.

**Ormond Beach (Granada Boulevard area):** Street parking is plentiful and free along Granada and the surrounding blocks. The area isn't dense enough to have parking issues on a normal evening.

**Pro move:** Arrive 15 minutes early, park once, and walk. The dining and drinking destinations in any of these corridors are close enough together that you won't need to move the car between dinner and after-hours.
