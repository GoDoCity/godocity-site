Whether you’re a lifelong metalhead or just looking for the ultimate Florida weekend, Welcome to Rockville is back and bigger than ever. With headliners like **Guns N’ Roses, Foo Fighters, Bring Me The Horizon,** and **My Chemical Romance,** this year’s lineup is a powerhouse that promises to shake the Speedway’s infield. It’s not just about the music, though; expect carnival rides, art installations, and a massive selection of local and regional food vendors.

**Pro Tip:** **With 160+ bands on the schedule,** the festival footprint can be overwhelming. Download the official app early to map out your "must-see" sets and set reminders. Also, hydrate! The Florida sun is no joke, even in May—bring a refillable water bottle to use at the designated stations throughout the Speedway.

If you’re coming from out of town, pack comfortable shoes—you will be doing a lot of walking across the Speedway’s legendary asphalt. Don’t miss the late-night sets; the atmosphere as the sun sets over the track is truly unmatched. See you in the pit!
