Race Week is back. The 68th running of the Daytona 500 drops the green flag on Sunday, February 16th — and if you've never experienced it from inside the track, this is the year to go.

## The Schedule

The week kicks off with practice sessions on Wednesday and Thursday. Qualifying runs Friday afternoon. The Duel at Daytona, the twin 150-mile qualifying races, runs Thursday evening under lights.

The 500 itself goes green at **2:30pm ET on Sunday, February 16th.**

## Getting In

General admission for Sunday is still available through the official Daytona International Speedway site. Infield passes sell out fastest.

**Charles King's section pick:** Sections 168–172 on the backstretch give you a clear sightline to the trioval, a bit of shade past noon, and easy exit access. Been sitting in 170 for eleven years. The backstretch crowd is friendlier and the racing comes to *you*.

## Parking

The official lots run $40–$60 and fill by 10am on race day. Save money by parking at One Daytona and taking the pedestrian bridge — spots run $20–$25 and you're inside the gates in twelve minutes.

## Road Closures

International Speedway Boulevard closes to through traffic from 8am on race day. Expect closures on:

* **Bill France Blvd** — closed from 7am
* **Williamson Blvd** — significant delays from 9am
* **I-95 on-ramps at LPGA and ISB** — expect metering

## What to Watch For

This year's field has a legitimate three-way battle shaping up between the Ford Penske cars, the Hendrick Chevrolets, and a Gibbs Toyota that tested faster than anyone publicly admitted. The real race starts around lap 130.
