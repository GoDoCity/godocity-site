Downtown Daytona Beach has undergone a dramatic transformation along the Halifax River. The 22-acre Riverfront Esplanade has revitalized the city's urban core into a serene, walkable waterfront destination.

Designed as a premier gathering space, the park features lush botanical gardens, shaded walking paths, swings overlooking the Intracoastal, and expansive event lawns surrounding the city’s waterfront performance venue. A standout feature is the 330-foot Riparian Garden Fountain, crafted to mimic natural river tidal pools alongside interactive splash pads for families. Managed by the Brown Riverfront Esplanade Foundation, the park hosts community events, fitness classes, and weekend gatherings. Whether you are taking a morning jog or catching a sunset over the river, the Esplanade proves that Daytona's urban heart is thriving.

## 📝 Park Highlights

- **Location:** Beach Street along the Halifax River
- **Features:** 22 acres of gardens, walking trails, and water features
- **Admission:** Free and open to the public daily

**Pro Tip:** Park along Beach Street and grab a local coffee before exploring the north end lawns—it's the best spot for morning dolphin watching!
