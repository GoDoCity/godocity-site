The New Smyrna Beach Jazz & Blues Festival takes over Riverside Park with headlining acts and local talent performing on two stages across the weekend. Enjoy craft beer from local breweries, food from area restaurants, and art from local vendors against the backdrop of the Indian River Lagoon.
