Now in its **53rd year**, the Daytona Turkey Run is a 100% nonprofit event proudly presented by a partnership of the Daytona Beach Racing & Recreational Facilities District and the Daytona Beach Street Rods. Together, these two organizations work to support and grow the old-car hobby by hosting these events twice each year in March and November. All proceeds from the Turkey Run, currently over Twenty Million Dollars have been donated to local and national charities. Helping strengthen communities while celebrating America’s automotive heritage.
