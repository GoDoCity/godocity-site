The annual Daytona Beach Arts Festival brings together over 100 juried artists, live stage performances, and local food vendors along the scenic Halifax Riverfront. Browse fine art, photography, sculpture, and handmade goods while enjoying live music from local and regional acts.

Free parking available at the Peabody Auditorium and City Island lots.
