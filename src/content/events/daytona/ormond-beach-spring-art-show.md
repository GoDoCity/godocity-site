The Ormond Beach Spring Art Show returns to Andy Romano Beachfront Park with over 80 juried fine artists. Browse original work from painters, sculptors, jewelers, photographers, and ceramicists with the Atlantic Ocean as the backdrop.

Admission is free. Parking available on Oceanshore Blvd and at nearby lots.
