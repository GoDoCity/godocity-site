title: 'The Sweet Way to Dodge Mosquitoes: A DIY Vanilla Spray That Actually Works 🦟'
intro: |-
  Mosquito season in Florida is no joke, and while heavy-duty chemical sprays are highly effective, many of us want a safe, natural alternative for casual backyard hangouts and patio dinners. Pure vanilla extract acts as a natural pest control method by temporarily masking the carbon dioxide, breath odors, and general skin scents that hungry mosquitoes use to find you.

  While vanilla alone only buys you about thirty to forty-five minutes of protection and will not ward off ticks or wasps, it makes an incredible base. To maximize your defense, you can easily combine your vanilla spray with other natural deterrents like lemongrass, lavender, or lemon eucalyptus essential oils.
materials: |-
  15 mL (1 tablespoon) pure vanilla extract (avoid imitation vanilla, as the sugars attract bugs)
  240 mL (1 cup) clean water
  Empty spray bottle
  Optional: 15 drops of lemongrass, lavender, or lemon eucalyptus essential oil
steps: |-
  Step 1: Prep the Base
  Pour 240 milliliters of clean water directly into your empty spray bottle.

  Step 2: Add the Vanilla
  Measure out 15 milliliters of pure vanilla extract and add it to the water. If you want to boost the spray's effectiveness, add up to 15 drops of lemongrass, lavender, or lemon eucalyptus essential oil at this stage.

  Step 3: Shake and Apply
  Cap the bottle and shake it vigorously to blend the ingredients. Generously mist the mixture onto your skin before heading outside. Remember to reapply every 30 to 45 minutes for continuous coverage.

  Step 4: Dress Smartly
  To maximize your protection, avoid wearing dark colors, reds, or oranges. Light, neutral colors or pastels will help you stay invisible to passing pests.
