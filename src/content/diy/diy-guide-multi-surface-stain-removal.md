title: "DIY Guide: Multi-Surface Stain Removal"
intro: >-
  Tackling stubborn stains doesn’t require expensive specialty chemicals. By
  using common household staples, you can effectively lift discoloration from
  various surfaces ranging from kitchenware and bathroom fixtures to delicate
  fabrics and wooden furniture.


  This guide focuses on inexpensive, easy-to-find ingredients that provide powerful cleaning results. Whether you are dealing with mineral buildup in the bathroom or protein-based stains on clothing, these methods offer a practical approach to maintaining a spotless home environment.
materials: >-
  Liquids: 3% Hydrogen Peroxide, White Vinegar, Rubbing Alcohol, and Dawn dish
  soap.


  Powders/Solids: Baking soda, Alka-Seltzer tablets, and regular (non-gel) toothpaste.


  Tools: Toilet brush, microfiber cloths, sponges, paper towels, and an old toothbrush or spoon for scrubbing.
steps: >-
  Diagnose the stain type to select the right agent, such as vinegar for hard
  water or peroxide for organic stains like blood.


  Establish a cleaning paste by mixing baking soda with water or hydrogen peroxide for tougher marks on dishes and sinks.


  Coat the affected area thoroughly with the chosen solution and allow it to sit for at least 10 minutes to break down the residue.


  Agitate the surface using a brush, cloth, or sponge to lift the loosened particles from the material.


  Purify the item by rinsing with water, wiping clean, or laundering as usual to remove any remaining cleaning paste or lifted grime.
