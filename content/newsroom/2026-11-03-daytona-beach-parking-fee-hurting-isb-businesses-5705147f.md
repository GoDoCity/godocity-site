## Why it matters

The $20 daily county beach parking fee directly affects where tourists stop, eat and shop along a signature Daytona corridor, and local business owners say it's threatening their survival. Readers care about both the money angle and the fate of beloved beachside spots. The county's pending review means practical change may be coming.

## What happened

Beachfront businesses along International Speedway Boulevard's beach ramp told the Volusia County Council the new $20 daily parking fee is driving away customers and cutting revenue, with some considering reduced hours or closure. Non-residents get ticketed quickly for nonpayment (tickets $30-$120), while county residents park free. County staff is reviewing short-term parking options and is expected to bring options back to the Council within weeks.
