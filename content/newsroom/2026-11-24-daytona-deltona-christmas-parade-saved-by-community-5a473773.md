## Why it matters

The Deltona Christmas Parade, held annually since 2010, was abruptly canceled amid steep budget cuts tied to Amendment 3, drawing national attention before community partners stepped up to save it. It's a feel-good community-triumph story that also spotlights a serious fiscal crisis affecting many Volusia residents and all city events.

## What happened

On Nov. 21, City Manager Dale 'Doc' Dougherty announced the Dec. 12 Deltona Christmas Parade was canceled due to budget cuts from the state's Amendment 3. After national media attention, Dougherty announced late Monday that several community organizations stepped in to fund the parade, reviving it. The city still faces major cuts — roughly $12 million next year, $5 million the following year, and over $23 million out of a $230 million budget — with nearly all other city events canceled.

## Key facts

- Parade scheduled for Dec. 12, annual since 2010
- Initially canceled Nov. 21 due to Amendment 3 budget cuts
- Reinstated late Monday via community organization funding

- Nearly all other city events canceled except two self-supporting 5k runs
