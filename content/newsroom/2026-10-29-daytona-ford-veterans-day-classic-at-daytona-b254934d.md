## Why it matters

A star-studded Ford racing and concert event lands at Daytona International Speedway on Nov. 11, bringing NASCAR champions, major country acts and celebrity guests to town. It's a buzzy, TV-broadcast spectacle that locals can attend in person, and a new tourism draw for the area.

## What happened

Ford Motor Company is staging the 'Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic' at Daytona International Speedway on November 11. Each U.S. military branch is represented by two drivers racing Ford Mustang Dark Horse Rs on the 3.56-mile road course, with a 15-minute qualifying and 30-minute sprint race. The event features tailgating, celebrity guests (Bryan Cranston, Braun Strowman) and live music headlined by Zac Brown Band, with Jo Dee Messina, Russell Dickerson, Brittney Spencer and Warren Zeiders. It airs on USA Network 8-10 p.m. ET.

## Key facts

- Event is Nov. 11 at Daytona International Speedway
- Run on the 3.56-mile road course (Rolex 24 layout)

- Zac Brown Band headlines the music lineup

- Drivers include Austin Cindric, Joey Logano, Ryan Blaney, Brad Keselowski
