## What happened

Denis Keeran, 51, a former school guardian at New Smyrna Beach High School, was arrested a second time Tuesday on 26 counts of possessing child sexual abuse material after investigators found more than 1,600 suspected images. The probe began Oct. 7 when a resource officer saw him photographing female students; his initial arrest on Oct. 10 involved bestiality videos. He is held on $780,000 bond and no longer works for Volusia County Schools.
