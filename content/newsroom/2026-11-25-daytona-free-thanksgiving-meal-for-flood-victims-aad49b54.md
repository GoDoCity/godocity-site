## Why it matters

Recent flooding hit Daytona Beach residents hard, and a free community Thanksgiving meal is both a timely practical resource and a feel-good community moment. It's exactly the kind of uplifting, local, same-day event readers want to know about and share.

## What happened

Daytona Beach Mayor Derrik L. Henry invited residents affected by recent flooding to a free community Thanksgiving dinner at the Cherry Cultural & Educational Center at 1 p.m. Thursday. Meals will be served to 300 residents first-come, first-served while supplies last, prepared by Bethune-Cookman University's Wildcat Dining team. Sponsors include Gary Yeomans Ford, CW Roberts Contracting and Glenn and Connie Ritchey.
