## Why it matters

Residents hit by last week's flooding get a one-stop location for housing, financial and social-service help. This is critical, practical public-impact news for Daytona Beach and wider Volusia residents recovering from the disaster.

## What happened

FDEM approved a state-led Multi-Agency Resource Center at the Dickerson Community Center, 308 S. Dr. MLK Jr. Blvd., opening 1-7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 27, then daily 7 a.m.-7 p.m. through Dec. 11. It connects flood-impacted residents to housing, financial, social-service and other recovery help; in-person only with ID, proof of residence and insurance documentation required.

## Key facts

- MARC opens 1-7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 27 at Dickerson Community Center
- Daily hours 7 a.m.-7 p.m. through Dec. 11, including weekends
- Serves Daytona Beach and other flood-impacted Volusia residents
- In-person only; bring ID, proof of residence, insurance documentation
- FDEM funds operations; city provides facility
- Not currently offering federal disaster assistance
