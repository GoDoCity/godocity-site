## Why it matters

A citywide change of garbage haulers affects every household and business in New Smyrna Beach. Residents need to know about new totes, new trucks, cart-size options, and what to expect starting Oct. 1. Practical, broad-impact service news even if not exciting.

## What happened

New Smyrna Beach is transitioning residential and commercial solid waste collection from Waste Pro to FCC Environmental Services, with service beginning Oct. 1, 2026. FCC is delivering new totes and 533 commercial containers, staffing up with new drivers doing practice route runs, and adding GPS/collection-verification tech. About 147 residents have requested smaller carts.

## Key facts

- FCC Environmental Services takes over from Waste Pro on Oct. 1, 2026
- 13 of 17 collection vehicles already onsite; rest arriving soon
- 533 commercial front-load containers being delivered
- About 147 residents have requested smaller carts
- FCC vehicles doing practice route runs the week before launch
- Public Works contact: 386-424-2212
