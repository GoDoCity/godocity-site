## Why it matters

This was a shocking, high-profile random attack on a teenager at the Daytona Beach Pier during Daytona 500 weekend, one of the area's busiest tourism events. The sentencing resolves a case that drew wide community attention, and lingering questions about whether police acted fast enough earlier that day give it continued public-safety relevance.

## What happened

Jermaine Long was sentenced Tuesday in a Volusia County courtroom to 45 years in prison under a plea deal covering eight charges, after randomly slashing then-13-year-old Sullivan Clarke's neck near the Daytona Beach Pier during Daytona 500 weekend in February 2026. Long, a registered sex offender recently released from jail, apologized in court. The victim's family questioned why police did not arrest Long earlier the same day after a reported sledgehammer attack at a gas station.

## Key facts

- Jermaine Long sentenced to 45 years under a plea agreement covering eight charges
- Victim Sullivan Clarke, now 14, was slashed near the Daytona Beach Pier during Daytona 500 weekend in February
- Long is a registered sex offender recently released from jail after charges were dropped in a separate case
- Long had been trespassed—but not arrested—after a reported sledgehammer attack at a gas station hours earlier
