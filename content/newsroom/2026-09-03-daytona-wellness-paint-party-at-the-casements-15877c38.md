## Why it matters

The Casements is a beloved Ormond Beach landmark, and a recurring hands-on art class gives readers a low-key, creative local activity to try. It's the kind of accessible community event newsletter readers can act on.

## What happened

Ormond Beach announced a series of Wellness Paint Party classes at The Casements led by Gina Bickish. Sessions run monthly on Sept. 18, Oct. 16, Nov. 13 and Dec. 11 from 10:45 a.m. to 1:15 p.m. at $45 per class with all supplies included.

## Key facts

- Held at The Casements in Ormond Beach
- Led by instructor Gina Bickish
- Dates: Sept 18, Oct 16, Nov 13, Dec 11
- Time 10:45 a.m.–1:15 p.m., $45 per class
- All painting supplies included; register by calling (386) 956-6891
