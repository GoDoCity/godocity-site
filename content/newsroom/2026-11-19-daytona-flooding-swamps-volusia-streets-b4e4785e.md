## Why it matters

Heavy rain flooded streets across multiple Daytona-area communities, with residents evacuating homes and more rainfall forecast. This is critical public-impact news affecting where people live and drive, and readers need to know flood-prone spots and sandbag locations.

## What happened

On Wednesday night, heavy rain flooded streets across Volusia County, including Lantern Drive in South Daytona's Lantern Park subdivision (estimated a foot or more of water), Moonstone Court and Nixon Lane in Port Orange, and areas near Spruce Creek Road/Oak Street. Some residents moved cars and a couple evacuated to stay with family. Near Edgewater, a downed tree took out power lines, leaving a resident without power since 3:30 p.m. More rain is expected in coming days.
