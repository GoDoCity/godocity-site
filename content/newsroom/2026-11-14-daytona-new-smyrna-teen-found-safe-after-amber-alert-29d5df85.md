## What happened

A 16-year-old New Smyrna Beach girl, Fayren Morris, was reported missing Thursday and believed abducted, prompting an AMBER Alert naming 34-year-old Ra'Kim Edwards as a suspect. Police issued updates through the evening and confirmed at 9:40 p.m. that Morris was found safe. As of Friday morning, Edwards had not been arrested in connection and the circumstances, including his involvement, remain under investigation.
