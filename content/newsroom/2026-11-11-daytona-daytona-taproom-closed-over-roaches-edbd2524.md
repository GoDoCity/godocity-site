## Why it matters

Daytona Taproom sits on Seabreeze Boulevard, a high-traffic dining and nightlife strip, so a health-inspection shutdown is exactly the kind of local news residents share and discuss. Diners want to know where they're eating, and restaurant inspection news reliably drives engagement. The eatery has already reopened after meeting standards, giving the story a clean resolution.

## What happened

State inspectors ordered Daytona Taproom at 310 Seabreeze Blvd. temporarily closed on Nov. 6 after finding over 50 dead roaches and more than a dozen live roaches near the cookline amid grease accumulation. It met inspection standards and was allowed to reopen the next day, Nov. 7. It was one of six Central Florida eateries shut down that week.
