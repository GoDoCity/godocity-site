## Why it matters

A beloved 24-year-old surf competition and charity event is just up the coast and draws spectators and surfers from the Daytona area. It's a feel-good weekend outing with pro aerial demos and a charitable mission for local student scholarships.

## What happened

The 24th Annual Tommy Tant Memorial Surf Classic runs October 23-25 south of the Flagler Beach Pier, featuring pro and amateur divisions, the return of the Tow-At pro aerial demo with Flagler Beach surfers Ryan Huckabee and Robbie McCormick, and a new permanent home at Craeft Surf Studio run by Will and Clarissa Tant. Proceeds support local student scholarships through the Fellows Program.

## Key facts

- 24th annual event, Oct 23-25, south of Flagler Beach Pier
- Tow-At aerial demo Saturday Oct 24 with Huckabee and McCormick
- Kickoff party Friday Oct 23, 7 p.m. at Craeft Surf Studio, 404 S Ocean Shore Blvd
- Historically raised over $200,000 for community causes
- 2026 proceeds fund student scholarships via Fellows Program
- Memorial paddle-out and awards ceremony Sunday
