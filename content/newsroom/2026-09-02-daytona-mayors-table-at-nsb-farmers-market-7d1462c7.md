## Why it matters

Residents can talk directly with Mayor Fred Cleveland and a rotating senior city official at the popular Saturday farmers market. It's an easy, low-barrier way for locals to raise concerns and connect with city leadership.

## What happened

Mayor Fred Cleveland hosts a table at the New Smyrna Beach Farmers Market in front of City Hall (210 Sams Ave.) every first Saturday from 7 a.m. to noon. Each month a different senior city official joins him; on Saturday, Sept. 5, it will be Finance Director Jo Anne Drury.

## Key facts

- Mayor Fred Cleveland hosts a table every first Saturday
- Location: NSB Farmers Market, City Hall, 210 Sams Ave.
- Hours 7 a.m. to noon
- Sept. 5 guest: Finance Director Jo Anne Drury
- A different senior official joins each month
