## Why it matters

Parents and residents near these two schools will see new sidewalks, curb ramps and safer crossings, directly affecting daily school walks. It's a tangible neighborhood improvement funded largely by state Safe Routes to School dollars. Construction is already underway and affects local foot and vehicle traffic.

## What happened

Daytona Beach began construction Oct. 12 on a roughly 0.3-mile pedestrian safety project along Cedar and Keech streets near Campbell Middle School and Turie T. Small Elementary. The work adds 5-to-8-foot sidewalks, reconstructed curbs and gutters, intersection bump-outs and ADA-compliant ramps, with completion expected in early 2027. It builds on earlier trail and intersection work on Russell Drive and Loomis Avenue, funded primarily through FDOT's Safe Routes to School program.

## Key facts

- 0.3-mile project started construction Oct. 12, 2026
- Covers Cedar Street (Jean to Keech) and Keech Street (South to Cedar)
- Includes 5-8 ft sidewalks, bump-outs, ADA ramps
- Serves Campbell Middle and Turie T. Small Elementary
- Funded primarily by FDOT Safe Routes to School program
- Completion anticipated early 2027
