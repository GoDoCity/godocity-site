## Why it matters

The Ormond Beach Songwriter Festival is a marquee local music event, and the Unplugged day at The Casements offers free family-friendly fun on the Halifax riverfront. Readers looking for a weekend outing would want to know about live music, food trucks and a kids' zone in one spot.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced The Casements historic landmark would close temporarily to prepare for the Ormond Beach Songwriter Festival Unplugged. The event day features live music from 10 AM to 10 PM, food trucks, a kids' zone and more, with the home open for viewing. The Casements returns to normal hours and tours on Monday, September 28.

## Key facts

- The Casements will close temporarily to prep for the festival
- Unplugged event runs Saturday 10 AM to 10 PM
- Features live music, food trucks and a kids' zone
- The historic home will be open for viewing
- Normal hours and tours resume Monday, September 28
