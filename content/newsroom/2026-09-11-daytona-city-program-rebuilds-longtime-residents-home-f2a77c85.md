## Why it matters

Housing affordability and helping longtime residents stay in their neighborhoods is a live concern across Daytona Beach. This shows a tangible outcome of a city program many residents may not know exists, and offers a heartwarming human-interest angle.

## What happened

Barbara Lester, a Daytona Beach resident since 1992, received keys to a newly reconstructed home valued at $255,905 after an approximately 18-month rebuild. The work was completed through the City's Home Rehabilitation and Reconstruction Program, administered by Redevelopment and Neighborhood Services and funded by SHIP, CDBG and HOME programs.

## Key facts

- Barbara Lester has lived in Daytona Beach since 1992, moving from Fort Worth, Texas
- New home valued at $255,905, built by JJ Pro Management LLC
- Roughly 18-month reconstruction process
- Program administered by Redevelopment and Neighborhood Services Department
- Funded through SHIP, CDBG and HOME programs
