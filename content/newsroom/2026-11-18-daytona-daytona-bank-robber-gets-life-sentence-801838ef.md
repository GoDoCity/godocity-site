## What happened

46-year-old Scott Riley was convicted of robbery with a mask and sentenced last week to life in prison for an April 8, 2026 robbery of a bank on Seabreeze Boulevard in Daytona Beach. He was arrested the same day with $9,556 still on him. The life term stems from his status as a prison releasee reoffender and habitual violent felony offender with prior offenses including sexual battery.
