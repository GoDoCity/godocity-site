## Why it matters

The city is putting money directly toward nonprofits helping homeless and disadvantaged residents, a tangible community-support move. Local nonprofits and the people they serve stand to benefit, and the newsletter can help spread the word before the deadline.

## What happened

New Smyrna Beach announced five $10,000 Community Aid grants for local nonprofits serving homeless and disadvantaged individuals and families within city limits. Applications are due by 5 p.m. Friday, Oct. 30, with proposals presented to an evaluation committee at 9 a.m. Monday, Nov. 9 at the Hathaway Commission Chambers.

## Key facts

- Five grants of $10,000 each available
- For nonprofits serving homeless/disadvantaged people within city limits
- Application deadline 5 p.m. Friday, Oct. 30
- Proposals presented 9 a.m. Monday, Nov. 9 at 214 Sams Ave.
- Contact: Grants Manager Larry Wheatcraft, 386-410-2616
