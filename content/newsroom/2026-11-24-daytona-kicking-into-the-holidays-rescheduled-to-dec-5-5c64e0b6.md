## Why it matters

It's a free, family-friendly community event at a beloved local landmark that combines fun with holiday giving. Readers planning weekend outings will want the updated date, and it supports local families through toy and food drives.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach rescheduled its Kicking Into the Holidays event to Saturday, Dec. 5, 10 a.m.–1 p.m. at Jackie Robinson Ballpark due to expected weather. The free event features a kickball tournament between city departments, kids' activities, concessions, and a donation drive for toys (Early Learning Coalition) and nonperishable food (Halifax Urban Ministries).

## Key facts

- Rescheduled to Saturday, Dec. 5, 10 a.m.–1 p.m. due to weather
- Held at Jackie Robinson Ballpark, 105 E. Orange Ave.
- Free kickball tournament between city departments with kids' activities
- Donation drive: toys for Early Learning Coalition, food for Halifax Urban Ministries
- Hosted by Daytona Tortugas; sponsors include East Coast Signs & Trophies and Academy Sports
