## Why it matters

Local families with elementary-age kids are always hunting for affordable, educational things to do, and a city-run nature program is an easy weekend tip. It's small but on-brand for a parent-focused reader.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced a 'Fascinating Foxes' educational program on Friday, November 13, designed for children in grades 1-5. Registration is required, with both morning and afternoon sessions available.

- Morning and afternoon sessions available
- Hosted by the City of Ormond Beach
