## What happened

Daytona Beach German Shepherd Rescue is holding its 2nd Annual Pet Party & Fundraiser at The Casements North Lawn on Saturday, September 12, 2026, from 9 AM to 2 PM. The free event features K9 demonstrations, a disc dog demo team, pet vendors, food trucks, raffles and giveaways.
