## Why it matters

A recurring free outdoor activity gives area residents and nature lovers a low-cost way to explore Central Park's trails with an expert guide. It's the kind of accessible community event that fits GoDoDaytona's go-and-do angle.

## What happened

The Ormond Beach Environmental Discovery Center announced Fall Bird Walks led by Halifax River Audubon president and Florida Master Naturalist Joan Tague. The casual guided walks run Wednesday mornings 8-10AM starting Sept. 30 and continuing Oct. 7, 14, 21, 28 and Nov. 4, meeting at the EDC at 601 Division Ave.

## Key facts

- Led by Joan Tague, president of Halifax River Audubon and Florida Master Naturalist
- Wednesdays 8-10AM starting Sept. 30 through Nov. 4
- Meet at the Environmental Discovery Center, 601 Division Ave, Ormond Beach
- Walks explore Central Park trails for nesting and migrating birds
