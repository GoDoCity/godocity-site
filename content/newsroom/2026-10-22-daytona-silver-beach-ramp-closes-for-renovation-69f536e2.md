## Why it matters

Beach access ramps are heavily used by Daytona residents and visitors, so a months-long closure affects how people get to and park on the beach. The upgrades signal county investment in coastal facilities, including storm resilience improvements.

## What happened

Volusia County is closing the Silver Beach Avenue beach access ramp in Daytona Beach for a major renovation beginning after October 19, 2026. Work includes reconstructing the concrete ramp, reinforcing its structure, updating lighting and electrical systems, adding storm-protected family restrooms, and new landscaping, sidewalks and curbs. The ramp is expected to stay closed until early 2027.

## Key facts

- Silver Beach Avenue ramp in Daytona Beach closes after Oct 19, 2026
- Expected to reopen early 2027
- Concrete ramp reconstructed, structure reinforced, lighting/electrical updated
- Family restrooms added farther from ramp bottom for storm protection
- New sidewalks, curbs and landscaping planned
- Project led by Volusia County government
