## Why it matters

The Casements is a beloved Ormond Beach landmark, and a hands-on cooking class is exactly the kind of do-something local event GoDoDaytona readers enjoy. It gives residents a fun, tangible activity to plan for and share.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced a 'My Big Fat Greek Cooking Class' at The Casements on Thursday, October 22, 2026, from 11:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. Participants will learn to prepare a Greek vegetarian menu including hummus, tzatziki, Greek salad, homemade pita, vegetarian moussaka, spanakopita and baklava, then enjoy the meal together.

## Key facts

- Event: My Big Fat Greek Cooking Class
- Date/time: Thursday, October 22, 2026, 11:30 a.m.–3 p.m.
- Location: The Casements, Ormond Beach
- Vegetarian Greek menu including moussaka, spanakopita, baklava
- Registration required via city link
