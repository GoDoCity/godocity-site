## Why it matters

A presidential visit to the Ocean Center on Friday evening will shut down much of the Daytona Beach peninsula, triggering road closures, traffic jams, transit disruptions and airport delays. This directly affects where thousands of residents and travelers can drive, park and catch flights on the same day. It's high-impact practical news regardless of politics.

## What happened

Volusia County, the Sheriff's Office and Daytona Beach International Airport issued traffic warnings ahead of President Trump's visit to the Ocean Center on Friday, Oct. 30. Sheriff Mike Chitwood said the peninsula would go into 'lockdown' with street and road closures and traffic jams. The airport warned of delays and urged early arrival; VoTran, VoAccess and VoRide riders were told to expect delays and that the Beachside Transit Facility would be closed. Airspace restrictions and a drone ban were also announced. The event was set for 7 p.m. with doors at 3:30 p.m.; specific closure locations were still being finalized.
