## Why it matters

Two young brothers drowned in a neighborhood retention pond, and the city is now weighing whether to require fencing around stormwater ponds in developments citywide. This touches public safety, who pays for new requirements, and could change rules for developers, apartment complexes and homeowners across Daytona Beach.

## What happened

City commissioners were scheduled to discuss pond safety at a Wednesday meeting after 3-year-old Kashh Williams and 5-year-old Elijah Hawkins drowned in a retention pond near the Reserve at Clyde Morris Landings earlier this month. No ordinance vote was scheduled. Commissioner Ken Strickland said safety measures should apply to all bodies of water and that commissioners must discuss who would pay for fencing.

## Key facts

- Brothers Kashh Williams, 3, and Elijah Hawkins, 5, drowned in a retention pond near the Reserve at Clyde Morris Landings

- City does not generally require fences around stormwater ponds in developments
