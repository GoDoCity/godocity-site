## Why it matters

A new convenience store with gas pumps signals development along Ormond Beach's U.S. 1 corridor, which falls in a protected greenbelt gateway area. Readers care about what's being built near them and how the city balances growth with its preservation overlay rules.

## What happened

Circle K Stores, with engineering firm Kimley-Horn and property owner RCM Properties, applied for a buffer variance to build a Type B convenience store with gas pumps on N. U.S. Highway 1. The land development code requires a 36-foot landscaped greenbelt buffer, but the applicant seeks a reduction to 33 feet (27 feet planted depth) citing grade changes, a retaining wall, right-of-way limits, tree preservation and a planned sidewalk. The Board of Adjustments and Appeals will hear the request Nov. 4, 2026.

## Key facts

- Applicant: Circle K Stores, Inc. with Kimley-Horn; owner RCM Properties, LLC
- Proposed: Type B convenience store with gas pumps on N. U.S. Highway 1
- Site is in B-8 Commercial zoning and the Greenbelt/Gateway Preservation Overlay
- Seeking a 9-foot variance to the required 36-foot greenbelt buffer
- Public hearing Nov. 4, 2026, 6 PM at Ormond Beach City Hall
