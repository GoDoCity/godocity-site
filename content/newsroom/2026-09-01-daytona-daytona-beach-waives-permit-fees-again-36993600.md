## Why it matters

This puts real money back in the pockets of homeowners and businesses planning renovations, roof replacements, new AC units, fences and more. With the last waiver saving $3.5 million across 2,000+ projects, residents planning any improvement could save meaningfully by timing their permits now.

## What happened

Daytona Beach is waiving all permit and inspection fees for both commercial and residential projects through Jan. 31, 2027. Eligible work ranges from new construction and interior renovations to roof replacements, electrical/fire suppression upgrades, and improvements like fences, doors, windows, driveways and AC units. It is the city's sixth fee waiver since 2020.

## Key facts

- Fees waived through Jan. 31, 2027 for commercial and residential projects
- Covers new construction, renovations, roofs, electrical/fire systems, fences, windows, driveways, AC units
- Prior waiver (Sept 2024–March 2025) waived $3.5M across 2,000+ projects
- Sixth fee waiver since 2020
- Apply online at daytonabeach.ims16.com/ims or call 386-671-8140
