## Why it matters

A seasonal giving opportunity that lets West Volusia residents help local families during the holidays. Actionable items like this give readers something positive to do before Christmas.

## What happened

West Volusia Habitat for Humanity is collecting new toys and bicycles for a holiday toy drive. Items will go to Habitat homeowners and the Spring Hill Resource Center, with donations accepted at the Habitat ReStore in DeLand.

## Key facts

- Donations are new toys and bicycles
- Drop-off at Habitat ReStore, 604 S. Spring Garden Ave., DeLand
- Items benefit Habitat homeowners and Spring Hill Resource Center
