## Why it matters

Rising river flooding is a genuine public-safety and property concern for Volusia residents, with electrical boxes near water and homes threatened. Readers near the St. Johns corridor need to know the river is at major flood stage and expected to crest early next week.

## What happened

Floodwaters from the St. Johns River are rising in Volusia and Lake counties. Residents in Astor and near DeLand report water entering garages, submerged docks, and streets flooding, with electrical boxes nearly touching water. Lake County officials said the river reached major flood stage; Volusia officials expect it to crest early to mid-next week. No relocation assistance had been requested as of Tuesday night.
