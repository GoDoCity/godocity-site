## What happened

Epifanio Gaminde, 48, was arrested Monday and charged with first-degree murder in the January 2024 disappearance of his wife, Maria Gaminde, a University High School paraprofessional and mother of three. Investigators say he faked her being abroad using a niece in the Philippines to send texts, emails and credit-card spending, while Maria never left the U.S. The sheriff's crime scene unit searched the couple's DeBary home, focusing on a fake backyard fountain; her body has not been found but prosecutors say they will proceed.
