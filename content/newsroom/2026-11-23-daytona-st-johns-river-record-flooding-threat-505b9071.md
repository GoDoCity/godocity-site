## Why it matters

This is critical public-impact news: major to record flooding is forecast for river communities including DeLand, Astor and Hontoon Island, threatening homes, roads and marinas. Readers near the river need to prepare now, and sandbag sites and road hazards are directly actionable. It qualifies under the critical-public-impact exception.

## What happened

Volusia County and the National Weather Service warned that the St. Johns River is expected to continue rising to major or record flood stages over the coming week, even as rain-driven flooding recedes elsewhere. Because the north-flowing river keeps draining runoff from Central Florida, communities near Astor, DeLand, Hontoon Island, Enterprise, Lake Harney Woods and Stone Island face prolonged, significant impacts. The county urged residents to prepare, opened three sandbag sites, and warned against driving through flooded roads.
