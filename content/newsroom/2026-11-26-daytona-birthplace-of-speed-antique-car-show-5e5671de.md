## Why it matters

The Birthplace of Speed show celebrates Ormond Beach's heritage as the cradle of American auto racing and draws car enthusiasts and families. It's a free, local weekend event readers can attend right after Thanksgiving.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced the return of the Birthplace of Speed Antique Car Show on Saturday, November 28, 2026, at Fortunato Park.
