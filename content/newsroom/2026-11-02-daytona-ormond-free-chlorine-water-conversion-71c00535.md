## Why it matters

Residents may notice a change in their tap water's taste and odor, which prompts questions and concern even though the city says water remains safe. City-wide hydrant flushing can also cause temporary discoloration. It's practical public-impact info worth a short heads-up.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach began a scheduled drinking-water system maintenance, temporarily converting to free chlorine (a stronger disinfectant) and flushing hydrants city-wide. The conversion runs through Nov. 23. Officials say water stays safe to drink but may have a slightly different taste and odor.

## Key facts

- Conversion to free chlorine runs through Nov. 23, 2026
- City-wide hydrant flushing accompanies the switch
- Water remains safe to drink; possible taste/odor change
- Public Works contact: (386) 610-0700
