## Why it matters

Residents still recovering from last week's flooding get a free holiday meal and community gathering, a timely uplifting story tied to a recent local disaster. It's practically useful (where, when, how many meals) and showcases local businesses stepping up. Timing is urgent since the event is tomorrow.

## What happened

Daytona Beach Mayor Derrick Henry invited residents affected by recent flooding to a free Thanksgiving community dinner at 1 p.m. Thursday, Nov. 26, at the Cherry Cultural & Educational Center on George W. Engram Blvd. Meals are free for 300 residents, first-come first-served, with turkey, dressing, sides and pies prepared and donated by Wildcat Dining/SodexoMagic. Additional sponsors include Gary Yeomans Ford, CW Roberts Contracting and Glenn and Connie Ritchey.

## Key facts

- Free dinner 1 p.m. Thursday, Nov. 26 at Cherry Cultural & Educational Center, 925 George W. Engram Blvd.
- Meals for 300 residents, first-come first-served while supplies last

- Meals prepared and donated by Wildcat Dining, Powered by SodexoMagic
