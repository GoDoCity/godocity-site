## Why it matters

Major flooding with active water rescues is a critical public-safety event affecting dozens of Edgewater homes. Residents across southern Volusia need to know about dangerous flood conditions, road impacts and where to find help.

## What happened

First responders in Edgewater went door to door and deployed boats to rescue families trapped by rising floodwater, with roughly 20 homes in one neighborhood reporting water inside. Rescues included a mother and young son and a family with an infant recovering from a health issue. One family said water began pouring in around 4 a.m.

## Key facts
