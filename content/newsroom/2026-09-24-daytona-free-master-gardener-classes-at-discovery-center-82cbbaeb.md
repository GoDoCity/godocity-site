## Why it matters

Free, hands-on gardening classes give Ormond Beach residents a recurring, low-cost activity and practical Florida-specific tips they can use at home. It's the kind of community event a newsletter can point readers to month after month.

## What happened

The Ormond Beach Environmental Discovery Center is hosting free UF/IFAS Master Gardener classes on the first Wednesday of each month from 10 a.m. to noon at 601 Division Ave. Topics run from growing strawberries and nuisance wildlife to orchids, soil, seed starting and a spring plant swap, spanning September 2026 through May 2027.

## Key facts

- Free classes first Wednesday each month, 10am-12pm
- Held at Environmental Discovery Center, 601 Division Ave, Ormond Beach
- Presented by UF/IFAS Master Gardeners
- Topics span September 2026 through May 2027
- Includes a May 5 plant swap
