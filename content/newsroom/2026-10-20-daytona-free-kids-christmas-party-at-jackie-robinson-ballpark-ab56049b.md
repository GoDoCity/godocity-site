## Why it matters

A free family holiday event at the iconic Jackie Robinson Ballpark gives Daytona Beach families an easy, no-cost outing during the busy holiday season. It's part of a larger holiday weekend lineup that readers can plan around.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced a free Children's Christmas Party from 6 to 8 p.m. Friday, Dec. 11, at Jackie Robinson Ballpark. Aimed at kids ages 3 to 12, it will feature hot chocolate, face painting, music, line dancing, games and concessions. It's presented by Zone 6 Commissioner Paula R. Reed and the Daytona Tortugas, and is part of a holiday weekend that also includes the Dashing Through the Decades Parade (Dec. 12) and Midtown Sundayz (Dec. 13).

## Key facts

- 6 to 8 p.m. Friday, Dec. 11 at Jackie Robinson Ballpark, 105 E. Orange Ave.
- Free; aimed at children ages 3 to 12
- Features hot chocolate, face painting, music, line dancing, games, concessions
- Presented by Commissioner Paula R. Reed and the Daytona Tortugas
- Part of a holiday weekend with a Dec. 12 parade and Dec. 13 Midtown Sundayz
