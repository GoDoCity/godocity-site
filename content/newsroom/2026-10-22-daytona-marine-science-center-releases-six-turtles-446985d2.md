## Why it matters

The Marine Science Center is a beloved local institution, and a public turtle release is exactly the kind of feel-good, shareable story Daytona-area readers enjoy. It highlights local conservation work and gives families a reason to visit and attend future releases.

## What happened

On Wednesday, October 14, 2026, the Marine Science Center in Ponce Inlet released six rehabilitated juvenile green sea turtles at the Beach Street Ramp in a public event, along with a rehabilitated juvenile brown pelican. The six named turtles were among hundreds the center cares for annually.

## Key facts

- Six juvenile green sea turtles released Oct 14, 2026 at Beach Street Ramp, Ponce Inlet
- Turtles named Frogish, Mangrove Snapper, Highland Cow, Nurse Shark, Armadillo, Silkie Chicken
- A rehabilitated juvenile brown pelican was also released
- Center has taken in 243 sea turtles, 250 hatchlings/washbacks, 215 other reptiles in 2026
- Since 2002, center has cared for 27,000+ turtles and 19,000+ seabirds
