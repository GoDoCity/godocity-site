## Why it matters

The Flagler Avenue boardwalk and its parking lot are a popular beach-access point for NSB locals and visitors. A multi-day closure affects where people park and access the beach, which is practical news readers can plan around.

## What happened

New Smyrna Beach announced the Flagler Boardwalk will close Monday, Oct. 26 to replace pavilion and restroom roofing and regrade the parking lot. The north half of the lot reopens by the Oct. 30 weekend, the south half on Nov. 3, with the full project completing by Nov. 6.

## Key facts

- Closure begins Monday, Oct. 26
- North half of lot reopens by Oct. 30 weekend
- South half reopens Tuesday, Nov. 3
- Project expected to finish Friday, Nov. 6
- Work includes pavilion/restroom roofing and parking lot regrading
- Public Works contact: 386-424-2212
