## Why it matters

A recurring city event gives residents a low-cost way to declutter, hunt for bargains and connect with neighbors. It's the kind of local, do-something item readers can act on, though its appeal is fairly modest.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach is hosting Community Yard Sales at the Schnebly Recreation Center (1101 N. Atlantic Ave.) from 8 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. Residents can reserve indoor or outdoor vendor spaces, with indoor limited to two per person and outdoor unlimited. Registration closes at 4 p.m. the Friday before each sale, via DaytonaBeach.gov/Activities.

## Key facts

- Held at Schnebly Recreation Center, 1101 N. Atlantic Ave.
- Hours 8 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.
- Indoor spaces limited to two per person with a table provided; outdoor unlimited but bring your own table
- Registration closes 4 p.m. the Friday before each sale
- Register at DaytonaBeach.gov/Activities
