## Why it matters

Taste of Ormond is Ormond MainStreet's biggest annual fundraiser and a popular local food-and-drink event, giving readers unlimited tastings from area restaurants at a scenic riverside venue. It's an actionable event with a fun theme that supports historic Ormond Beach preservation and local businesses.

## What happened

Ormond MainStreet announced the 13th Annual Taste of Ormond, set for Sunday, October 18, 2026, from 4 to 7 p.m. at Rockefeller Gardens. The Roaring '20s-themed event features unlimited restaurant tastings, live music, a silent auction, raffles, and an optional Mixology Experience add-on. Proceeds support Ormond MainStreet's community events, beautification, public art and local business efforts.

## Key facts

- 13th Annual Taste of Ormond, Sunday Oct. 18, 2026, 4-7 p.m. (VIP entry 3 p.m.)
- Held at Rockefeller Gardens, 26 Riverside Drive, Ormond Beach
- Roaring '20s theme; attire encouraged but not required
- General admission $100, VIP $125; tables of 10 $900/$1,150; $25 Mixology add-on
- Largest annual fundraiser for Ormond MainStreet
