## Why it matters

A hands-on Greek cooking class is a fun, local seasonal activity readers can sign up for and share. It's the kind of upbeat 'do something this weekend' item the newsletter thrives on.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced a Greek holiday dessert cooking class with Chef Elaine Pitenis on Thursday, December 10, 2026, from 11:30 AM to 3:00 PM, featuring traditional sweets like baklava, kourambiethes, loukoumades, galaktoboureko, koulourakia, and finikia, plus Greek coffee.

## Key facts

- Date: Thursday, December 10, 2026, 11:30 AM–3:00 PM
- Led by Chef Elaine Pitenis
- Desserts include baklava, kourambiethes, loukoumades, galaktoboureko, koulourakia, finikia
- Includes authentic Greek coffee
- Hosted/announced by City of Ormond Beach
