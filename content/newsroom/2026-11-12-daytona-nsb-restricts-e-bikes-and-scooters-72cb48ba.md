## Why it matters

New e-bike and scooter rules with real fines affect residents, families and the many visitors who ride micromobility devices around New Smyrna Beach. The $150 penalty and helmet rule for teens are tangible changes people will discuss and need to know about.

## What happened

At their first meeting after inauguration, New Smyrna Beach commissioners voted 5-0 to approve an ordinance restricting electric bikes, scooters, unicycles and similar devices. The ordinance establishes a $150 fine for riding in city parks, streets, sidewalks, paths and other areas deemed hazardous to pedestrians, and a $25 fine for riders ages 16-17 not wearing helmets. The move followed resident concerns and an Oct. 1 public forum hosted by Police Chief Chris Kirk. The meeting also included the swearing-in of Mayor Fred Cleveland and commissioners Curtis Hodges and Jennifer Devine, and appointment of Brian Ashley as vice mayor.
