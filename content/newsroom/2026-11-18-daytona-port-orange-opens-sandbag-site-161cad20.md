## Why it matters

A sandbag distribution site signals a looming weather threat and offers residents a free, practical resource to protect their homes. Port Orange residents need to know the location, hours and eligibility right away. This is time-sensitive public-impact information.

## What happened

The City of Port Orange opened its sandbag distribution site Wednesday, November 18, from 10 a.m. until dark on Bruner Road. The site is for Port Orange residents only, who must bring valid ID. City staff pre-fill and load bags using new equipment, with designated entrance and exit routes.

## Key facts

- Site open Wednesday, Nov. 18, 10 a.m. until dark on Bruner Road
- Port Orange residents only; valid ID required
- Staff pre-fill and load bags — no heavy lifting
- Enter via Bruner Road from Clyde Morris Blvd; exit right only onto Madeline Ave
