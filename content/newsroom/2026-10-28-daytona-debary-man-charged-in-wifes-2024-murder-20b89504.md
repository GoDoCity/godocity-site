## Why it matters

This is a highly unusual murder case with an international investigation spanning Japan and the Philippines, not routine blotter material. A nearly three-year cover-up involving faked messages makes it a story Volusia residents will discuss widely.

## What happened

Volusia Sheriff's Office announced Tuesday the arrest of 48-year-old Epifanio IX Ramos Gaminde of DeBary, charged with first-degree murder in the 2024 disappearance of his wife, Maria Gaminde, last contacted by family in January 2024. Investigators say he impersonated her in messages to conceal her death, prompting an international investigation. Maria's body has not been recovered. He was booked without bond Monday and faces an ICE detainer.
