## Why it matters

River Bend Golf Course has sat vacant since 2020, and nearby residents want it reopened. With three letters of intent and a 3,000-signature petition now on the table, the city is nearing a decision on the future of this airport-owned land 	a change many residents care about.

## What happened

Ormond Beach commissioners agreed to hold a workshop on the future of the city-owned, airport-adjacent River Bend Golf Course, vacant since 2020. Three letters of intent were submitted 	two to reopen it as a golf course and one to expand the adjacent airport business park. A petition with more than 3,000 signatures calling for reopening was presented Tuesday night. Because the land is part of airport property, the FAA has a role, and the city is awaiting FAA input before setting a workshop date.

## Key facts
