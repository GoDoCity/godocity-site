## Why it matters

A beloved, affordable family holiday tradition at Ormond Beach's historic Casements that sells out fast. Readers with young kids will want the ticket details and timing so they don't miss out.

## What happened

Tickets for Ormond Beach's Breakfast with Santa go on sale Saturday, November 7, 2026, at 8:00 AM, in person only at The Casements. Tickets are $7 per person (free for ages 2 and under), with a limit of 10 per family. Advance tickets are required and typically sell out quickly.

## Key facts

- Tickets on sale Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026 at 8:00 AM
- In person only at The Casements
- $7 per person; ages 2 and under free
- Limit 10 tickets per family; advance tickets required
- First-come, first-served while supplies last
