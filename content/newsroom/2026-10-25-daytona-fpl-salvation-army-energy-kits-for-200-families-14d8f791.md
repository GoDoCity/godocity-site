## Why it matters

With utility bills a real concern for many households, a free program that helps families cut energy use and offers a $200 bill credit is practical news. It ties a major utility and a well-known charity to concrete help for local residents.

## What happened

FPL and The Salvation Army distributed 200 energy-saving kits to pre-selected Volusia families at the Daytona Beach Salvation Army on Friday, Oct. 16. Kits included weatherization items like door seals, a fridge thermometer, dryer balls, an LED nightlight and a smart power switch. FPL also shared bill-management tips and its Saving Steps program, which offers eligible customers a one-time $200 bill credit.

## Key facts

- 200 pre-selected Volusia families received kits at Daytona Beach Salvation Army
- Event held Friday, Oct. 16 at 1555 LPG Blvd., ran until noon
- Kits not a walk-up giveaway; recipients drawn from food-pantry users
- FPL Saving Steps offers eligible customers a one-time $200 bill credit
- First time Salvation Army has done energy kits
