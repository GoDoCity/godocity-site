## Why it matters

Residents and businesses hit by recent heavy rainfall and flooding have a direct, practical way to document damage that could support state and federal disaster aid requests. This is actionable public-impact information that readers can use and share.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach urged residents and businesses affected by recent heavy rain and flooding to submit damage reports through Volusia County's Crisis Track online portal. The data helps the county document community-wide damage and support requests for potential state and federal disaster assistance. Officials noted a report is not an insurance claim or FEMA application and does not guarantee financial aid.

## Key facts

- City is directing residents to Volusia County's Crisis Track online reporting portal
- Reports help document community-wide damage and support state/federal aid requests
- Residents urged to report even if they've contacted insurance
- A report is not an insurance claim, FEMA application, or guarantee of financial assistance
