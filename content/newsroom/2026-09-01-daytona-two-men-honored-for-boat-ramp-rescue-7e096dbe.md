## Why it matters

A dramatic local rescue turned civic-award story is exactly the kind of uplifting human-interest content readers love to share. It celebrates ordinary residents doing something extraordinary, and it ties to a recognizable New Smyrna Beach landmark, the North Causeway boat ramps.

## What happened

At the Aug. 25, 2026 City Commission meeting, Cody Swavely and Robert Baggs received Good Smyrnian Awards for rescuing a motorist whose sedan submerged at the North Causeway boat ramps on Aug. 4. The car reportedly drove down the ramp at about 25 mph and sank 20 feet past the floating dock. The driver was underwater roughly 60 seconds before Swavely opened the door and Baggs helped pull them out. Mayor Fred Cleveland read a proclamation praising their courage.
