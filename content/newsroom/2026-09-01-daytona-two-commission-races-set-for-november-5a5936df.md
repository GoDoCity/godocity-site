## Why it matters

City Commission decisions shape development, taxes and services across Daytona Beach, and voters will decide two of three races in November. The results affect who represents residents in Zones 1 and 3, and a new commissioner has already won Zone 5.

## What happened

After the primary, Zone 1 pits incumbent Monica Paris against John Navarra, and Zone 3 pits incumbent Quanita May against Steve Miller (Miller led the primary 41.89% to May's 37.64%; Christos Mavronas got 20.47%). In Zone 5, Willie Williams won outright with 60.36%, replacing retiring Dannette Henry. New commissioners take office Nov. 18.

## Key facts

- Zone 1: incumbent Monica Paris vs. John Navarra
- Zone 3: incumbent Quanita May vs. Steve Miller
- Zone 5: Willie Williams won outright with 60.36%
- Voter registration deadline Oct. 5; vote-by-mail request by Oct. 22
- Early voting Oct. 19-31 at two Daytona Beach sites
- New commissioners sworn in Nov. 18
