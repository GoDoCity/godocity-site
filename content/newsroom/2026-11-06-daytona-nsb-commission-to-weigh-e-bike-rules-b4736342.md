## Why it matters

E-bike rules affect beachgoers, residents and visitors who ride on sidewalks, trails and the beach in a popular coastal town. New restrictions could change where and how people ride, making this a practical change worth flagging. It also comes alongside a new commission's inauguration.

## What happened

The New Smyrna Beach City Commission scheduled its nineteenth regular meeting for Tuesday, Nov. 10, with an inauguration ceremony at 5 p.m. and the meeting at 5:30 p.m. The agenda is expected to include consideration of e-bike rules, and public comment is open.

## Key facts

- Meeting set for Tuesday, Nov. 10 at 5:30 p.m., with inauguration at 5 p.m.
- Held at James W. Hathaway Commission Chambers, 214 Sams Ave.
- Public comment allowed, up to three minutes per speaker
- Headline flags e-bike rules on the agenda
