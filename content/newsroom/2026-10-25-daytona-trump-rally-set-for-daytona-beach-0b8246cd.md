## Why it matters

A sitting president holding a rally in Daytona Beach is a major local event that will draw big crowds, heavy traffic, road closures and security operations affecting residents and businesses. Regardless of politics, readers will want to know when, where and how it affects getting around town.

## What happened

The Republican National Committee says President Trump will hold a rally in Daytona Beach on Friday, Oct. 30, ahead of the 2026 midterm election. As of reporting, the White House had not officially confirmed the rally, and no specific venue or speaker list had been announced. Tickets (up to four per person) are being requested through the GOP website. It would be Trump's second Florida rally in a week after a Fort Myers event on Oct. 23.
