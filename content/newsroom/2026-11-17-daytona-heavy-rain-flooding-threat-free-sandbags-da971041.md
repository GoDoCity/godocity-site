## Why it matters

Heavy rain and potential flooding is a direct safety-and-property concern for residents in low-lying Volusia neighborhoods, and free sandbag locations are immediately actionable info. Daytona Beach itself is offering free sandbags, giving a strong local hook.

## What happened

As heavy rain threatened Volusia County, cities prepared drainage systems and offered free sandbags. Daytona Beach provided free sandbags at Bethune Point Park on Nov. 17-18, and Edgewater offered them at Rotary Park and its Public Works facility. Edgewater crews lowered retention ponds, checked ditches and staged pumps; the mayor noted post-flood improvements including a vac truck. New Smyrna Beach residents near Ball Bay were already seeing high water.

## Key facts

- Daytona Beach free sandbags: noon-5 p.m. Tue Nov. 17 and 8 a.m.-5 p.m. Wed Nov. 18 at Bethune Point Park, 11 Bellevue Ave.
- Edgewater offering sandbags at Rotary Park and Public Works Operations Facility
- New Smyrna Beach's Ball Bay area already seeing high water
- Edgewater lowering retention ponds, staging pumps, added vac truck since last flood
- Tides can worsen flooding, mayor warned
