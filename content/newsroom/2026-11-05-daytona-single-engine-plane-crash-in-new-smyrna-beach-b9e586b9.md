## What happened

On Wednesday afternoon, a single-engine aircraft crashed off Turnbull Bay Road in New Smyrna Beach. Firefighters extricated the pilot and provided emergency care on scene before the pilot, declared a trauma alert, was airlifted to a hospital. The cause has not been released.
