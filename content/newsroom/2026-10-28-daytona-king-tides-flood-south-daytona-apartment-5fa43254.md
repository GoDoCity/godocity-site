## Why it matters

King tides are a recurring and worsening coastal flooding issue in low-lying Volusia communities, and this story shows real property damage hitting local families. Readers in flood-prone areas will want to know when king tides are happening and how bad they can get.

## What happened

A South Daytona family said king tides flooded their apartment three times in 24 hours, seeping through doors and damaging electrical wiring, a computer and a PlayStation 5. The family, who moved in three months ago, spent hours cleaning with a borrowed shop vac and set up a GoFundMe as king tides continued.

## Key facts

- Family's South Daytona apartment flooded three times in 24 hours
- Water damaged electrical conduits, a computer and a PlayStation 5
- Family moved in three months ago; cleanup took about five hours
- King tides were ongoing with more expected in following days
- A GoFundMe was set up to help the family
