## Why it matters

A precautionary boil water notice directly affects residents' daily safety and water use in a core coverage community. Even short service interruptions matter to people who need to know their tap water is safe.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach interrupted water service on Wednesday, Oct. 21, 2026, from 8am to 11am to install a fire hydrant at Spanish Waters Drive and Ocean Shore Boulevard. Affected customers were placed on a precautionary boil water notice.
