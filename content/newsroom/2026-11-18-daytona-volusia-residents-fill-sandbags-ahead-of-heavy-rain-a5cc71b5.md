## Why it matters

Days of heavy rain with potential coastal flooding directly affects residents' homes and safety, and sandbag locations are practical, actionable information readers need now. This meets the critical public-impact exception even though it isn't 'fun.'

## What happened

Volusia County homeowners lined up at sandbag distribution sites in Edgewater and New Smyrna Beach on Tuesday ahead of forecasted heavy rain. Inland areas could see up to 6 inches, with a worst-case coastal scenario of 8-12 inches (under 10% chance). Edgewater distributed nearly 1,000 sandbags to 100+ homes and deployed pumps and a vac truck. Multiple sandbag sites remain open Wednesday.

## Key facts

- Sandbag sites open in Edgewater (Rotary Park) and New Smyrna Beach sports complex
- Edgewater distributed nearly 1,000 sandbags to at least 100 homes
- Inland Volusia could see up to 6 inches of rain
- Worst-case coastal scenario: 8-12 inches, less than 10% chance
- Sites remain open Wednesday
- Edgewater deployed pumps and a new vac truck
