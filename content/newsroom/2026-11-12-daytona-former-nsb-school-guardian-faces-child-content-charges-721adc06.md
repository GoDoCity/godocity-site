## Why it matters

A man who served as a guardian at New Smyrna Beach High School, entrusted with protecting students, now faces dozens of child sexual abuse material charges — a story of serious public-safety and institutional-trust concern for local parents. The connection to a specific high school and the scale of the alleged material elevate it above routine crime blotter.

## What happened

Denis Keeran, 51, a former school guardian at New Smyrna Beach High School, was arrested a second time this week on 26 counts of possessing child sexual abuse material. Edgewater police seized over 30 devices from his home and found a cache of more than 1,600 images. His first arrest came October 10 on four counts involving bestiality videos. He remains in custody on $780,000 bond. Investigators say scrutiny began when he was seen photographing female students on campus.
