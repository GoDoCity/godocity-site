## Why it matters

A free, all-day music festival with 70+ acts across 10 stages at the historic Casements is exactly the kind of can't-miss local event Daytona-area readers want to plan around. It draws crowds, spotlights local music, and gives families a full day of activities.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced the Ormond Beach Songwriter Festival Unplugged at The Casements on Saturday, September 26, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. It features more than 70 acts on 10 stages inside and on the North Lawn, plus artist and trade-show booths, a youth art contest, food vendors, a beer-and-wine garden, and kids' crafts. It runs rain or shine.
