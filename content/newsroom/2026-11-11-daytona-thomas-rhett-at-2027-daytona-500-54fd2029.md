## Why it matters

The Daytona 500 is the area's marquee event, and a big country headliner adds buzz for the huge local and visiting crowd. Readers love knowing who's playing and when tickets are available. It's a feel-good, shareable announcement tied to the city's biggest sporting weekend.

## What happened

Daytona International Speedway announced that country star Thomas Rhett will perform the pre-race concert ahead of the 69th Daytona 500 on Sunday, February 21, 2027. The concert takes place inside the speedway before the race. Tickets for the race are on sale.

## Key facts

- Thomas Rhett to play pre-race concert at 2027 Daytona 500
- Race is Sunday, Feb. 21, 2027 — the 69th running
- Announced by Speedway, quote from track president Frank Kelleher
- Rhett is a multi-award-winning country artist, 2020 ACM Entertainer of the Year
- Tickets for the Daytona 500 are on sale
