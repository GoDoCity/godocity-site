## Why it matters

The St. Johns River is expected to crest Monday or Tuesday, with roadway flooding a real possibility in the Stone Island/Enterprise area. This is a genuine public-safety and property concern that residents in southwest Volusia will want to track, and the county has already opened sandbag sites.

## What happened

Volusia County officials warned that heavy rain from Brevard County is moving north and could raise the St. Johns River enough to flood area roads. The Stone Island area near Enterprise is the top concern, with the river expected to crest around Monday or Tuesday. The county opened sandbag distribution in Osteen and Astor and says residents will get days of advance notice if conditions worsen.

## Key facts

- River expected to crest Monday or Tuesday
- Stone Island near Enterprise is the main concern
- Flooding driven by heavy Brevard rain flowing north
- Sandbag sites open in Osteen (180 N. SR 415) and Astor (23023 SR 40)
- County says residents will get days of advance warning
