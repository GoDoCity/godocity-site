## Why it matters

A feel-good human-interest story about a community rallying after a popular Halloween decoration was vandalized and stolen downtown. It celebrates local business owners pitching in for a neighbor, exactly the uplifting, shareable content readers enjoy. It ties to a recognizable Downtown DeLand spot.

## What happened

On the night of Oct. 29, Bee Realty Corp.'s popular skeleton Halloween decoration was dismembered and stolen from its Downtown DeLand office, along with the security cameras. Owner Bee Powell shared her disappointment on Facebook. In response, local residents Vikkie Keller (Seven Oaks Farm) and Carly Burke (Pretty Porch DeLand) redecorated the porch with pumpkins and fall decor, and Josh Hays (New York Ave marketing agency) paid $400 for a replacement skeleton.
