## Why it matters

A new federal Opportunity Zone designation could draw business investment and redevelopment to New Smyrna Beach's north mainland starting in 2027. Readers who live, work or own property near Islesboro and Turnbull Bay may see new development activity as a result.

## What happened

The city announced that an area of NSB's north mainland, including Islesboro and Turnbull Bay Country Club Estates, was selected for a federal Qualified Opportunity Zone taking effect Jan. 1, 2027. The tract is classified rural, offering enhanced tax benefits to qualified investors. The city plans to market the designation to recruit businesses and advance redevelopment projects.

## Key facts

- Designation takes effect Jan. 1, 2027
- Covers north mainland including Islesboro and Turnbull Bay Country Club Estates
- Tract classified as rural, unlocking enhanced OZ benefits
- Uses federal tax incentives to attract long-term capital
- Economic Development Director Samantha Bergeron is leading recruitment efforts
