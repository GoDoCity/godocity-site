## Why it matters

An officer-involved shooting inside a Volusia County police station is a rare and serious event that residents will be talking about. It raises questions about police response, public safety, and the FDLE investigation. This is critical public-impact news, not routine blotter.

## What happened

Police say 71-year-old Linda Jean Croteau walked into the Port Orange Police Department lobby around 8:30 a.m. Thursday, fired one shot, then walked outside where officers repeatedly told her to drop the gun before shooting her. She died Friday at the hospital. Surveillance video was released, and the FDLE will investigate as standard protocol.
