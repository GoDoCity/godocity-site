## Why it matters

E-bikes and scooters are increasingly common across Volusia, and new speed limits, helmet rules and steep fines would directly affect riders, parents of young riders, and pedestrians. The coordinated approach with Orange City and the Sheriff's Office signals this could spread to neighboring communities.

## What happened

DeBary city leaders were set to vote on the first reading of Ordinance No. 09-2026, which would set e-bike speed limits (5 mph in active school zones, 10 mph on sidewalks, 15 mph on state trails), require helmets for riders under 16, ban electric motorcycles from sidewalks and trails, and impose fines of $100/$250/$500 for repeat violations within 12 months, plus impoundment provisions.

## Key facts

- Ordinance No. 09-2026 first reading vote scheduled for the night of Nov. 18, 2026
- Speed limits: 5 mph active school zones, 10 mph sidewalks, 15 mph state trails
- Helmets required for riders under 16
- Fines: $100 first, $250 second, $500 third within 12 months; impoundment provisions
- Developed over six months with Volusia Sheriff, Orange City and Orange City PD
- Takes effect immediately after a second reading if passed
