## Why it matters

The Belvedere Terminals fuel storage facility fight has been a major regional controversy, and Ormond Beach residents were directly involved in beating back the proposal in 2025. This update shows the project's continued collapse across the area's municipalities, which matters to Volusia residents who opposed it.

## What happened

The Bunnell City Commission voted unanimously on Monday to reject $10 million in state subsidies for a proposed Belvedere Terminals 300,000-gallon fuel storage facility along the Florida East Coast Railway. Bunnell becomes the third local government to reject the proposal, following Ormond Beach/Volusia County and Palm Coast in 2025. Without state funding, the company has little path forward.

## Key facts

- Bunnell Commission voted unanimously Monday to reject $10M state subsidy
- Belvedere Terminals sought to build a 300,000-gallon fuel facility along FEC Railway
- Ormond Beach/Volusia County rejected the subsidy in March 2025
- Palm Coast rejected it in April 2025; Flagler County in May 2025
- Without state funding, the project has little path forward
