## Why it matters

The same industrial parcels that fueled the Belvedere fuel-terminal fight are back before the city, now for a concrete mixing plant near a 620-home neighborhood. Residents are mobilizing over air quality, traffic and property impacts, making this a live local controversy headed to the Planning Board. Land-use fights like this shape what gets built in residents' backyards and draw strong community engagement.

## What happened

Three industrial parcels totaling about 52 acres along Hull Road and Hull Trail are before Ormond Beach for a land-use change to light industrial and utilities. Halifax Paving wants to build a concrete mixing plant on its property. Ormond Lakes neighbors oppose it, citing air quality, truck traffic and lack of a traffic signal at Hull Road. The proposals go before the city's Planning Board on Thursday (Nov. 12).
