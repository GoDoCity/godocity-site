## Why it matters

City Commission decisions shape development, spending, and services across Daytona Beach, and both Zone 1 and Zone 3 races were decided by fewer than 100 votes. Residents will want to know who represents them for the next four years and that a 30-year political comeback just happened in Zone 3.

## What happened

In the November 3, 2026 general election, incumbent Monica Paris won re-election in Zone 1 over John Navarra 51.46% to 48.54% (a 96-vote margin). In Zone 3, challenger Steve Miller unseated two-term incumbent Quanita May 51.60% to 48.40%, returning to the commission more than 30 years after his first stint in the mid-1990s. Zone 5 was decided in the August primary when Willie F. Williams won outright with 60.26%.

## Key facts

- Zone 1: Monica Paris re-elected 1,688 to 1,592 (96-vote margin)
- Zone 3: Steve Miller beat incumbent Quanita May 1,583 to 1,485
- Miller previously served on the commission in the mid-1990s
- Zone 5: Willie F. Williams won outright in August primary with 60.26%
- May had beaten Miller in 2022 with 55.40%
