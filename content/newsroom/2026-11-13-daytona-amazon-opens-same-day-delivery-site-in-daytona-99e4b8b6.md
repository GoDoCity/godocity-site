## Why it matters

A new Amazon facility means 100+ full-time jobs at $20/hour plus benefits, plus faster delivery for local shoppers heading into the holidays. It's a tangible economic win and a practical change in how quickly residents can get orders. This is Amazon's sixth Volusia County facility, signaling continued regional investment.

## What happened

Amazon opened a Same-Day Delivery facility in Daytona Beach ahead of the holidays. The site combines fulfillment, sorting and delivery to cut shipping to as little as five hours, with Prime members able to pay extra for one- to three-hour windows. It brings 100+ full-time roles at $20/hour plus benefits and is Amazon's sixth facility in Volusia County.
