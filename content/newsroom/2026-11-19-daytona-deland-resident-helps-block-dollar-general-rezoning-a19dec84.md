## Why it matters

A resident showing up and swaying a 3-2 commission vote is a classic civic-engagement win readers love to share. It also signals ongoing tension over commercial creep into DeLand's quiet neighborhoods, a recurring local issue.

## What happened

Dollar General sought to rezone a small parcel near Margaret Street in southwest DeLand to allow a new store. Resident Adrian Mattos, who lives nearby, spoke against it at two commission meetings. On Nov. 16, the first reading of the rezoning ordinance failed 3-2.

## Key facts

- Dollar General wanted a parcel near Margaret Street, SW DeLand, rezoned
- Property is majority zoned Highway Commercial; subject parcel is urban low intensity
- First reading of rezoning ordinance failed 3-2 on Nov. 16
- Resident Adrian Mattos spoke against it at two meetings
- Site currently holds a cell tower and little else
