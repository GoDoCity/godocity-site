## Why it matters

Residents who rely on reclaimed water for irrigation need to stop using it until further notice, a direct and practical impact on homeowners. While not fun, it's a real public-utility notice affecting daily routines in Edgewater.

## What happened

The City of Edgewater temporarily suspended reclaimed water availability after heavy rainfall overwhelmed demands on its wastewater treatment system. Residents using reclaimed water for irrigation were told to refrain until service resumes. Potable drinking water is unaffected.

## Key facts

- Reclaimed water service suspended until further notice
- Caused by heavy rainfall straining the wastewater treatment system
- Residents told to stop using reclaimed systems for irrigation
- Potable drinking water is not affected
- City will update when service can safely resume
