## What happened

Actor and NASCAR driver Frankie Muniz was involved in a crash Wednesday during practice for the Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic at Daytona International Speedway. He was taken to a local hospital, evaluated, and released, saying he was sore but protected by his Mustang. A spectator described it as one of the hardest hits he'd seen, with crews cutting off the car's roof to extricate Muniz.
