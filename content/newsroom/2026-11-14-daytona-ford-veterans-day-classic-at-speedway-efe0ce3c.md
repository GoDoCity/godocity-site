## Why it matters

A star-studded NASCAR exhibition with big-name drivers and major musical acts drew crowds to Daytona International Speedway, spotlighting the track as a year-round entertainment destination beyond its marquee races. Local fans would enjoy the celebrity lineup and the veteran-honoring theme.

## What happened

Ford Racing held its first Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic on Wednesday at Daytona International Speedway, a 30-minute road-course dash with 12 Ford drivers representing the six military branches. Austin Cindric won for the Air Force, edging Marine Corps rep Robert Noaker. The event also featured performances by Zac Brown Band, Nelly, and others, plus celebrity appearances. Frankie Muniz and Ryan Blaney withdrew due to injuries.

## Key facts

- First-ever Ford Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic, held Wednesday Nov. 11

- Musical acts included Zac Brown Band, Nelly, Russell Dickerson, Jo Dee Messina, Warren Zeiders
- Hosted by Bobby Bones for USA Network; Bryan Cranston and Adam Scherr attended
