## Why it matters

Nova Road is a major Port Orange thoroughfare, and nightly lane closures on it and at the busy Dunlawton intersection will affect many drivers' commutes. Practical heads-up news residents can act on.

## What happened

FDOT began paving Nova Road between Ridgewood Avenue and Herbert Street on Monday, September 28, 2026. The project involves nightly single-lane closures on Nova Road and Spruce Creek Road (8 p.m.–6 a.m.) and single/double-lane closures at Dunlawton Avenue at Nova Road (8:30 p.m.–6:30 a.m.).

## Key facts

- Paving began Monday, September 28, 2026
- Stretch: Nova Road from Ridgewood Avenue to Herbert Street
- Nightly closures on Nova/Spruce Creek Road 8 p.m.–6 a.m.
- Dunlawton at Nova single/double-lane closures 8:30 p.m.–6:30 a.m.
- FDOT project 450643-1 (S.R. 5A from U.S. 1 to Herbert Street)
