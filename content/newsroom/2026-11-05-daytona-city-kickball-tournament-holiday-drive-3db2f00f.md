## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly community event at the historic Jackie Robinson Ballpark that lets residents cheer on their local firefighters, police and public works crews while helping families in need. It's the kind of feel-good, shareable local happening that fits a 'things to do' slot and ties into holiday giving.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced 'Kicking Into the Holidays,' a kickball tournament and toy/food drive set for Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Jackie Robinson Ballpark. Teams from the Fire, Police, Utilities and Public Works departments will compete, with family activities and concessions available. Attendees are encouraged to bring new unwrapped toys for the Early Learning Coalition or nonperishable food for Halifax Urban Ministries.

## Key facts

- Event date: Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, 10 a.m.–1 p.m.
- Location: Jackie Robinson Ballpark
- Teams from Fire, Police, Utilities and Public Works departments compete
- Donations: toys for Early Learning Coalition, food for Halifax Urban Ministries
- Partnership with Daytona Tortugas; trophy donated by East Coast Signs & Trophies
