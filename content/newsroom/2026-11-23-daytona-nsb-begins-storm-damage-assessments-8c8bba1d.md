## Why it matters

Flooding significantly affected NSB neighborhoods, and residents in flood-prone areas need to know door-to-door and drive-by damage inspections are starting this week. This has direct practical impact on affected homeowners and reflects a real public-impact weather event in the coverage area.

## What happened

After several days of heavy rainfall and flooding, New Smyrna Beach reported most roadways have reopened and announced that city assessment teams will begin documenting storm damage. Drive-by inspections in the hardest-hit areas start Monday, Nov. 23, with door-to-door assessments in designated Special Flood Hazard Areas on Tuesday. The city thanked first responders, Public Works, and utility crews and provided a Development Services contact number for residents.

## Key facts

- Heavy rainfall caused significant flooding in parts of New Smyrna Beach
- Most roadways have reopened; some areas still affected
- Drive-by damage inspections begin Monday, Nov. 23
- Door-to-door assessments in Special Flood Hazard Areas on Tuesday
- Residents can contact Development Services at 386-410-2800
