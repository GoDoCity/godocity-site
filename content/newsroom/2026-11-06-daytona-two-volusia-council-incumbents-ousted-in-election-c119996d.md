## Why it matters

The Volusia County Council makes decisions on growth, development, flooding and spending that affect everyone across the Daytona area. Three new members joining in January signals a potential shift toward limiting sprawl and environmental protection, which could reshape local policy for years.

## What happened

In Tuesday's general election, two of three County Council incumbents lost. Wendy Anderson unseated Don Dempsey in District 1 (59.24%), Danny Robins held District 3 over Bryon White, J. David Sosa edged out incumbent David Santiago in District 5 by about 500 votes, and Nancy J. Miller won the open At-Large seat over Mike Poniatowski. The council will have three new members in January.

## Key facts

- Two of three incumbents seeking re-election lost
- Wendy Anderson beat Don Dempsey in District 1, 59.24% to 40.76%
- Danny Robins (i) held District 3 over Bryon White, 53.14%
- J. David Sosa unseated David Santiago in District 5 by ~500 votes
- Nancy J. Miller won At-Large with 118,007 votes, most of any local candidate
- Three new members join the council in January
