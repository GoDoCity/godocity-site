## Why it matters

Biketoberfest is one of Daytona Beach's signature tourism events, drawing an estimated 125,000 riders and $16 million for local businesses. The recent Main Street shootout and the safety response are top-of-mind for residents, visitors and downtown merchants. This blends a major economic driver with a real public-safety angle.

## What happened

The 34th annual Biketoberfest drew big crowds to Main Street just days after a Monday overnight shootout near Froggy's Saloon left two people in critical condition. A 26-year-old Port Orange man, Steven Ott, was arrested and charged with attempted murder. Police said the incident was isolated and unrelated to the event, and have increased staffing. Visitors interviewed said they generally feel safe, though some cited concerns about heavy drinking.
