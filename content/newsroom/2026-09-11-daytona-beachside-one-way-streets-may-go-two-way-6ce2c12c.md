## Why it matters

Converting beachside streets back to two-way traffic would change how residents, visitors and businesses navigate the core beachside area. With roughly 50 residents and business owners weighing in and most in favor, this touches everyday driving, business access and speeding concerns in a heavily trafficked part of the city.

## What happened

Mayor Derrick Henry hosted a community meeting at Peabody Auditorium where about 50 beachside residents and business owners largely supported exploring converting several one-way streets to two-way. City staff found Earl Street could be converted without major reconstruction, Wild Olive may work under conditions, and Oleander poses challenges due to narrow pavement. North Coates and Hollywood Avenue will also be reviewed. Seabreeze and Oakridge boulevards would require FDOT approval, studies and funding since they are state roads.
