## Why it matters

A pedestrian was killed and the driver fled, leaving an active public-safety search on a major Daytona Beach artery. Residents may have information, and the community-interest/safety angle on a busy road clears the blotter threshold.

## What happened

On Saturday, Nov. 7 around 4:30 a.m., a woman was found in the road at the 1500 block of LPGA Boulevard after being struck by a vehicle. She was taken to a hospital and pronounced dead. DBPD, which announced the search Tuesday, believes the vehicle is a sedan with front-end and windshield damage last seen speeding westbound on LPGA Boulevard.
