## Why it matters

License plate reader cameras touch privacy, policing and civil liberties concerns that resonate widely across Volusia communities. The decision affects what surveillance tech exists on county roads where residents drive daily, and pits Sheriff Chitwood against privacy advocates. This is a countywide policy change that readers will discuss.

## What happened

On Tuesday, October 6, 2026, the Volusia County Council approved a 12-month moratorium on license plate reader cameras, including Flock Safety devices, on county rights of way. The moratorium halts new permits and gives existing permittees 30 days to remove cameras. The county will use the year to evaluate state-level changes. The move follows FDOT's August decision disallowing LPR cameras on state roads, and Sheriff Mike Chitwood's agreement to suspend VSO usage while lobbying for a moratorium rather than an outright ban.
