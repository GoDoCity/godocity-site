## Why it matters

A fatal crash has closed a major I-95 interchange ramp in Daytona Beach during the morning commute, forcing drivers to detour. Road closures on a key corridor affect many local commuters and visitors.

## What happened

Just after 4:30 a.m. Monday, a crash involving two cars and a pedestrian occurred on the I-95 northbound exit ramp to U.S. 92 in Daytona Beach. The pedestrian was pronounced dead at the scene. The ramp was closed, with northbound drivers detoured to SR-400. FHP is investigating.

## Key facts

- I-95 northbound exit ramp to U.S. 92 closed
- Detour: exit at SR-400 northbound
