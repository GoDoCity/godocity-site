## Why it matters

Biketoberfest is one of Daytona Beach's signature events, pumping an estimated $16 million into the local economy and filling Main Street and Dr. Mary McLeod Bethune Boulevard. Residents and businesses feel the impact directly, from packed bars and shops to heavier traffic and road barricades. It's a quintessential Daytona story that draws strong community interest.

## What happened

The 34th annual Biketoberfest, a four-day motorcycle rally, took place in Daytona Beach, drawing roughly 125,000 riders and an estimated $16 million in economic impact. Police set up barricades and added patrols at Main Street and Dr. Mary McLeod Bethune Boulevard to manage traffic and pedestrian safety. The event unfolded amid ongoing debate over public safety, open carry, and proposed increases to vendor permit fees that merchants fear could push vendors out of the city.
