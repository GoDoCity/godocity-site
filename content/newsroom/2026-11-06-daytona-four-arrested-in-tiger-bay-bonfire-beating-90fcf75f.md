## Why it matters

A disabled teen was beaten for 30-40 minutes at a Volusia County bonfire party and the attack was filmed and posted online, drawing sharp public comment from Sheriff Chitwood. The case has generated significant community outrage and is a high-profile public-safety story countywide, with more arrests possible.

## What happened

The Volusia Sheriff's Office arrested two more men, Logan Levesque and Casey Knight, on Wednesday in connection with an Oct. 23, 2026 attack on a disabled teen at a bonfire party in Tiger Bay State Forest, bringing total arrests to four. Earlier, Cody Stripling and Sebastian Bowers were arrested over the weekend. Charges include aggravated abuse of a disabled adult, aggravated battery and battery by strangulation; Knight is also accused of filming and posting the beating. The victim was reportedly attacked for 30-40 minutes, leaving him with multiple bruises.
