## Why it matters

The North Causeway and 3rd Avenue corridors are major gateways and commercial areas in New Smyrna Beach, so redevelopment planning could reshape where residents and visitors drive, shop and gather. Workshops give residents direct influence over the vision before concepts are locked in.

## What happened

New Smyrna Beach is holding two community workshops on Nov. 16 and Nov. 18 (5:30-7:30 p.m.) at the Brannon Center to gather public input on future plans for the North Causeway and 3rd Avenue corridors. An interactive online map lets residents mark areas for improvement.

## Key facts

- Two workshops: Mon. Nov. 16 and Wed. Nov. 18, 5:30-7:30 p.m.
- Held at Brannon Center, 105 S. Riverside Dr.
- Focus is North Causeway and 3rd Avenue corridors
- Interactive project website for input: inspire-engagement.com
