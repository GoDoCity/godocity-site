## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly outdoor event is exactly the kind of thing local parents look for on a weekend. It's a low-cost, easy win for families and highlights Ormond Beach's riverside parks.

## What happened

Ormond Beach Parks and Recreation is hosting a free catch-and-release kids fishing tournament, 'Reel in the Fun,' on Saturday, October 3, 2026 at Bailey Riverbridge Gardens, 1 North Beach Street. Check-in starts at 8 AM with fishing beginning at 9 AM. Registration is required.

## Key facts

- Free catch-and-release kids fishing tournament
- Saturday, October 3, 2026 at Bailey Riverbridge Gardens, 1 North Beach Street
- Check-in 8 AM, fishing begins 9 AM
- Registration required; info at (386) 610-0790
- Hosted by Ormond Beach Parks and Recreation
