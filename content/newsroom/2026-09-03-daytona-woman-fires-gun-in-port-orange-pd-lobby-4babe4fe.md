## What happened

On Thursday morning, a woman identified as Linda Jean Croteau walked into the Port Orange Police Department lobby armed with a gun and fired at least one round before walking out, according to Chief Manuel Marino. Officers confronted and shot her; she was taken to the hospital in critical condition. The motive and any relationship to the department were unknown. The PD lobby will remain closed until Tuesday.
