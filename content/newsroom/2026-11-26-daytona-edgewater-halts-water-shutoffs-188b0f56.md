## Why it matters

A pause on water shutoffs is real money and real relief for struggling residents, and it signals a broader weather emergency affecting Edgewater. Readers behind on bills would want to know the grace period and deadlines.

## What happened

The City of Edgewater announced on November 23, 2026 that it is temporarily suspending water service disconnections. The move comes amid a cluster of weather-related emergency notices, including a declared state of emergency, heavy rainfall, reclaimed water suspension, water conservation urging, and refuse collection delays.

## Key facts

- Edgewater suspended water service disconnects as of Nov. 23, 2026
- City declared a state of emergency Nov. 19, 2026
- Reclaimed water service suspended due to heavy rainfall Nov. 20
- Residents urged to conserve water amid heavy rainfall
- Refuse collection delayed due to weather
