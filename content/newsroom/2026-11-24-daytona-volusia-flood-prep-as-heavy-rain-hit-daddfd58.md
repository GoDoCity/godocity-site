## Why it matters

Flooding is a real safety-and-money issue for Daytona-area residents, especially in southern Volusia communities still scarred by Hurricane Ian. Thousands of sandbags were distributed and schools closed, directly affecting families. St. Johns River flooding impacts could linger for weeks, keeping this relevant even after the rain stopped.

## What happened

A mid-November heavy rain event (3-6 inches, locally up to 8-12) prompted a Flood Watch across Volusia County through Nov. 20. Edgewater, Oak Hill and southern New Smyrna Beach faced the greatest risk due to 75-85% soil saturation. Edgewater deployed portable stormwater pumps and set up sandbag stations; Port Orange distributed 6,000 sandbags and Edgewater 10,000. Volusia County Schools closed Nov. 19-20 as a precaution. Officials warned the St. Johns River near Lake Harney could approach major flood stage over that weekend, with river impacts potentially lasting weeks.
