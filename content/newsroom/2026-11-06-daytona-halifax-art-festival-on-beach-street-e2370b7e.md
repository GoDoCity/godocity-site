## Why it matters

The Halifax Art Festival is a beloved free event drawing 200+ artists to downtown Daytona's riverfront, making it a can't-miss weekend plan for residents. It brings crowds, culture, food and music to Beach Street — exactly the kind of uplifting local happening GoDoDaytona readers want to attend and share.

## What happened

The Halifax Art Festival returned to Beach Street in downtown Daytona Beach on Nov. 7-8, 2026, transforming the riverfront Esplanade into a walkable outdoor gallery. Organizers say it features juried work from more than 200 artists, including about 150 fine art exhibitors competing for cash awards, plus student art, live music and festival food. Admission is free.

## Key facts

- Held Saturday Nov. 7 (10 a.m.-5 p.m.) and Sunday Nov. 8 (10 a.m.-4 p.m.)
- Located on Beach Street between Bay Street and Orange Avenue along the Riverfront Esplanade
- More than 200 artists, including ~150 fine art exhibitors competing for cash awards
- Free admission to the public
- Includes student art exhibits, live music and festival food
