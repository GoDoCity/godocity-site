## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly evening of local food trucks is exactly the kind of outing GoDoDaytona readers like to put on the calendar. It spotlights several local vendors and gives residents a low-cost night out at the Florida Tennis Center.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced Food Truck Friday returns Friday, Nov. 13, from 5 to 8 p.m. at the Florida Tennis Center, 1 Deuce Court. Confirmed trucks include Fizzy Flamingo, Tropisoul, JT's Wiener Wagon, Side of the Road Jerk Chicken and Hangry Eats.

## Key facts

- Date: Friday, Nov. 13, 5-8 p.m.
- Location: Florida Tennis Center, 1 Deuce Court
- Confirmed trucks: Fizzy Flamingo, Tropisoul, JT's Wiener Wagon, Side of the Road Jerk Chicken, Hangry Eats
- Hosted by the City of Daytona Beach
