## Why it matters

Homelessness and the Ridgewood corridor are ongoing concerns for Daytona Beach residents and businesses. A coordinated outreach effort by police, fire and community partners shows a positive, solutions-focused side of the city's response. Last year's walk connected more than 100 people to assistance.

## What happened

On Friday, Nov. 6, the Daytona Beach Fire and Police Departments partnered with local organizations for the Drug Abuse Response Team (DART) Street Walk, traveling more than six miles along the Ridgewood Avenue corridor to connect people experiencing homelessness with services. A resource fair was also held at 316 North St.
