## Why it matters

The Bandshell summer concert series and Saturday fireworks are a beloved local tradition, and readers are curious about how they stay free. This breakdown reveals the finances behind a favorite pastime and shows how sponsorships and concessions offset city costs.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach released a breakdown of what it costs to produce the free Friday Summer Concert Series and Saturday Friends of the Bandshell concerts. Friday concerts run about $125,300 a year (roughly $10,400 per show) but generate about $132,000 in offsetting revenue. Saturday concerts cost about $99,340 plus a $50,000 annual city contribution to Friends of the Bandshell (through 2027), while generating about $164,190. Costs cover police, fire, EMTs, security, cleaning, restrooms, staffing and artist fees ($2,700-$4,000 per show).

## Key facts

- Friday Summer Concert Series: ~$125,300/year, ~$10,400 per concert; ~$132,000 revenue
- Saturday concerts: ~$99,340 direct costs plus $50,000 city contribution to Friends of the Bandshell
- Saturday concerts generate ~$164,190; artist fees $2,700-$4,000 per show
- 2023 multi-year agreement funds Friends of the Bandshell $50,000 annually through 2027
- Saturday shows include weekly fireworks display, late May through September
