## Why it matters

The Casements Christmas Gala is one of Ormond Beach's most cherished holiday traditions, drawing families to the historic Rockefeller winter home. It's a perfect can't-miss seasonal event for readers planning their holiday weekends.

## What happened

The Casements Guild announced the 48th Annual Casements Christmas Gala, running December 4-6, 2026, at The Casements in Ormond Beach. The event kicks off Friday at 6 PM with a Christmas Tree Lighting and Santa's arrival in Rockefeller Gardens, followed by a weekend of entertainment, food trucks, train rides, handmade crafts, a Tree Gallery, and a Gift Shoppe. Saturday and Sunday run 12-5 PM.

## Key facts

- 48th annual event presented by The Casements Guild
- December 4-6, 2026 at The Casements, 25 Riverside Drive
- Tree lighting and Santa's arrival Friday 6 PM in Rockefeller Gardens
- Saturday & Sunday hours 12-5 PM
