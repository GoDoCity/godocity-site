## Why it matters

The Casements is a beloved historic Ormond Beach landmark, and a recurring hands-on art class gives locals a relaxing, affordable activity to try. It's the kind of do-something-this-weekend item that fits GoDoDaytona's lifestyle focus.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced a series of guided wellness paint classes at The Casements led by instructor Gina Bickish. Sessions run 10:45 a.m.–1:15 p.m. on September 18, October 16, November 13, and December 11, cost $45 per class with supplies included, and require registration by phone.

## Key facts

- Held at The Casements in Ormond Beach
- Instructor Gina Bickish leads step-by-step painting
- Dates: Sept 18, Oct 16, Nov 13, Dec 11
- Time 10:45 a.m.–1:15 p.m., $45 per class
- All supplies included; register at (386) 956-6891
