## Why it matters

Coastal flooding is threatening homes and businesses and closing roads along the Halifax River, a direct public-safety and property concern for residents and drivers. A coastal flood warning is in effect through Friday, making this timely and practically useful.

## What happened

On Wednesday, unusually high king tides combined with persistent onshore winds caused the Halifax River to overflow into Daytona Beach streets near Fairview Avenue and Ballough Road, flooding roads, sidewalks and reaching businesses like The Fishin Hole. City crews are monitoring pump stations and clearing storm drains along Beach Street and Palmetto Avenue. A coastal flood warning remains in effect through 2 a.m. Friday.
