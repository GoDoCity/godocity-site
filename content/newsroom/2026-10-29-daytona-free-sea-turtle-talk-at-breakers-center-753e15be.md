## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly educational event tied to Daytona's beloved coastline and sea turtle conservation gives readers something fun and local to do. Free admission plus provided parking lowers the barrier to attend.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced a free sea turtle presentation at 10:30 a.m. Tuesday, Nov. 17, at the Breakers Environmental Learning Center, 13 S. Ocean Ave. Lynn Black of the Volusia Turtle Patrol will discuss sea turtles, their habitats and conservation. Admission is free, parking permits provided, and registration is online.

## Key facts

- 10:30 a.m. Tuesday, Nov. 17 at Breakers Environmental Learning Center, 13 S. Ocean Ave.
- Presented by Lynn Black of the Volusia Turtle Patrol
- Admission free; parking permits provided
- Registration at DaytonaBeach.gov/Activities
