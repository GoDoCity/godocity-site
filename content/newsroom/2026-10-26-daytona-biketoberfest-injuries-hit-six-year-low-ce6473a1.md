## Why it matters

Biketoberfest is a signature Daytona Beach event drawing hundreds of thousands, so injury data is a meaningful measure of how the weekend went for the community and hospital. The good-news angle — fewer serious injuries and no deaths — is a positive marker residents will note.

## What happened

Halifax Health Medical Center reported that 19 patients were treated for Biketoberfest-related injuries following the event, which ended October 18, 2026. That was the lowest total in the six years of data, with 13 trauma activations/admissions and no deaths. Most injuries were motorcycle crashes, and most patients were out-of-state riders.

## Key facts

- 19 patients treated; 13 trauma activations/admissions
- Lowest injury total in six years of Biketoberfest tracking
- No patient deaths on arrival or in care
- 16 cases were motorcycle accidents; 10 of 15 riders wore helmets
- Median patient age 58; 18 men, one woman
- Only 3 patients were Volusia residents; 12 were from out of state
