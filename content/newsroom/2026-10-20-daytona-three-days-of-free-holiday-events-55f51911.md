## Why it matters

These are free, family-friendly events in the heart of Daytona Beach during the holiday season — exactly the kind of community celebration residents enjoy and share. The Christmas parade also ties into the city's 150th anniversary, adding a festive milestone hook.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced three free holiday events Dec. 11-13: a Children's Christmas Party at Jackie Robinson Ballpark on Friday, the Dashing Through the Decades Christmas parade down Beach Street on Saturday, and a special holiday edition of Midtown Sundayz at Daisy Stocking Park on Sunday.

## Key facts

- Three free events run Dec. 11-13, 2026
- Children's Christmas Party: Fri Dec. 11, 6-8 p.m., Jackie Robinson Ballpark, ages 3-12
- Dashing Through the Decades parade: Sat Dec. 12, 6 p.m., Beach Street route
- Midtown Sundayz holiday edition: Sun Dec. 13, 3-8 p.m., Daisy Stocking Park, with toy giveaway
- Parade ties into city's 150th anniversary celebration
