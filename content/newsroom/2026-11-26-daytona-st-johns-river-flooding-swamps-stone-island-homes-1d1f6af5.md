## Why it matters

Residents in Stone Island are dealing with floodwaters inside homes and sewage backups as the St. Johns River rises, a real public-impact story affecting Volusia County families. The county has opened a relief station, and officials warn flooding could persist for weeks. This meets the critical public-impact exception despite its grim tone.

## What happened

Floodwaters pushed into Stone Island homes as the St. Johns River rose toward a Wednesday crest. Residents reported water inside homes, sewage backing up into showers and toilets, and even alligators in the area. Volusia County set up a relief station with showers, restrooms and laundry. Emergency Management Director Clint Mecham said the area could remain in minor to moderate flood stage for an extended period and that pumping is unlikely to be a solution.
