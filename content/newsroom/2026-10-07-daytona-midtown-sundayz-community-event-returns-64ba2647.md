## Why it matters

A recurring, family-friendly community event in the heart of Midtown gives Daytona Beach residents a free, easygoing way to gather, dance and connect. These are exactly the local happenings readers want to know about to fill their weekends.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced the return of Midtown Sundayz, a free monthly community event at Daisy Stocking Park featuring DJ entertainment, food, board games and family activities, held the second Sunday of each month.

## Key facts

- Held at Daisy Stocking Park in Midtown Daytona Beach
- Takes place the second Sunday of each month
- Features DJ entertainment, food, and board games
- Free, family-friendly, all ages
- Attendees encouraged to bring lawn chairs
