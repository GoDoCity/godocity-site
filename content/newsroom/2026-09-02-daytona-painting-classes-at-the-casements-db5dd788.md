## Why it matters

The Casements is a beloved Ormond Beach landmark and a low-cost recreational class there gives residents a relaxed local activity to try. It's the kind of small, do-something item newsletter readers can act on.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced a series of guided painting classes at The Casements led by instructor Gina Bickish. Classes run monthly on Sept. 18, Oct. 16, Nov. 13 and Dec. 11 from 10:45 a.m. to 1:15 p.m., cost $45 each with all supplies included, and are open to beginners and experienced painters.

## Key facts

- Held at The Casements in Ormond Beach
- Instructor Gina Bickish leads step-by-step projects
- Dates: Sept. 18, Oct. 16, Nov. 13, Dec. 11
- Time 10:45 a.m.–1:15 p.m.; $45 per class, supplies included
- Register by calling (386) 956-6891
