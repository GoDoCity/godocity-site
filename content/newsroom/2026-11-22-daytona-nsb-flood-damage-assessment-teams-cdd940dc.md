## Why it matters

Flooding hit NSB hard enough this week to trigger city and FEMA-level damage assessments, which affects affected homeowners' insurance and recovery options. Residents in flood-prone areas like Corbin Park will want to know when teams arrive and what documentation is needed.

## What happened

The City of New Smyrna Beach announced that starting Monday, assessment teams will document water damage from this week's heavy rainfall. Drive-by inspections begin in hardest-hit areas including Corbin Park, followed Tuesday by door-to-door visits in Special Flood Hazard Areas requiring detailed FEMA documentation.

## Key facts

- Assessment teams start Monday with drive-by inspections of hardest-hit areas
- Corbin Park named among identified water-damaged areas
- Tuesday door-to-door in Special Flood Hazard Areas with FEMA documentation
- County assessment form completed for each affected property
- Contact Development Services at 386-410-2800
