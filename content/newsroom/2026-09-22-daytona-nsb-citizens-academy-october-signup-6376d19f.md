## Why it matters

Residents get an insider look at how their city government operates, with facility tours and access to department heads. It's a low-cost civic-engagement opportunity that appeals to newcomers and longtime residents alike.

## What happened

The City of New Smyrna Beach opened registration for its 2026 Citizens Academy, a five-evening program starting at 5:30 p.m. on select October evenings featuring presentations from department heads and facility tours, culminating in a graduation ceremony with the City Commission on Tuesday, Oct. 27.

## Key facts

- Five October evenings, sessions start at 5:30 p.m.
- Includes department-head presentations and facility tours
- Graduation ceremony Tuesday, Oct. 27 with City Commission
- Contact PIO Phillip Veski at 386-410-2614 or pveski@cityofnsb.com
