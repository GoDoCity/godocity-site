## Why it matters

A beloved holiday tradition was abruptly canceled then saved within 48 hours, a community win that locals will talk about. It also spotlights the real fiscal impact of Amendment 3, which Deltona says will force $23 million in cuts by 2030 and threaten other city services.

## What happened

Deltona's city manager canceled the Dec. 12 Christmas parade on Saturday, citing budget pressures from the recently passed Amendment 3, which expands homestead tax exemptions and slashes property-tax revenue. After community groups offered time and resources, City Manager Doc Dougherty reversed the decision Monday evening and announced the parade would proceed. Dougherty says the city must cut $12 million in 2027 and more in later years, and that other city-organized events are effectively canceled except two self-supporting 5K runs.

## Key facts

- Parade originally scheduled for Saturday, December 12
- Canceled Saturday, reinstated Monday evening after community outreach
- City Manager Doc Dougherty cites Amendment 3 budget cuts
- Deltona projects $12M in cuts for 2027, $5M in 2028, over $23M by 2030
- All other city-run events 'effectively canceled' except two self-supporting 5K runs
- Amendment 3 passed statewide with 61.98% on Nov. 3
