## Why it matters

The fire chief leads a $21.3 million department with seven stations and 131 positions serving the whole city. Residents get a rare chance to meet and question the candidates before the city manager picks the next leader. One finalist is the current interim chief and a 25-year department veteran.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced a public meet-and-greet from 6 to 7:30 p.m. Thursday, Nov. 5, at Fire Station No. 1, where residents can meet the three finalists for Fire Chief: Michael DeStefano of Jupiter Fire Rescue, interim chief Christopher Hughes, and veteran metro fire chief Dennis Rubin. City Manager Deric Feacher is expected to name a final pick by month's end.

## Key facts

- Public meet-and-greet 6-7:30 p.m. Thursday, Nov. 5 at Fire Station No. 1, 420 S. Ridgewood Ave.
- Finalists: Michael DeStefano, Christopher Hughes (interim chief), Dennis Rubin
- Hughes has nearly 25 years with the Daytona Beach Fire Department
- City Manager Deric Feacher to announce final selection by month's end
- Department has $21.3M budget, 7 stations, 131 budgeted positions
