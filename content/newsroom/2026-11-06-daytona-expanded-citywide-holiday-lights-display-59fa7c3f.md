## Why it matters

The holiday season is a prime time for residents and visitors to get out and enjoy the city, and these expanded displays give families destinations to visit from Beach Street to the Boardwalk. The coastal-themed touches like glowing fish, seagrass and kissing fish are distinctly Daytona. It's a feel-good seasonal story with clear 'go do' appeal.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced an expanded holiday lighting program for this season, adding decorations and illuminated displays across multiple landmarks and corridors including Beach Street, Mary McLeod Bethune Boulevard, Main Street, the Boardwalk, Breakers Environmental Learning Center, City Hall, Magnolia Avenue, Seabreeze Boulevard and the Tarragona Arch. Highlights include a 25-foot tree, illuminated snowflakes, coastal-themed displays with glowing fish and seagrass, kissing fish, a photo frame and walk-through arch. The City Commission and CRA approved an annual investment of up to $222,662.18 for rentals, installation, maintenance and removal.

## Key facts

- Decorations span Beach Street, MMB Blvd, Main Street, Boardwalk, City Hall, Magnolia Ave, Seabreeze Blvd, Tarragona Arch
- 25-foot Christmas tree and 40 illuminated snowflakes at Tarragona Way
- Coastal-themed displays: glowing fish, seagrass, kissing fish, illuminated sailfish
- Main Street Arch and bridge entrances decorated
- City Commission/CRA approved up to $222,662.18 annually
- Displays begin this month (November 2026)
