## What happened

5-year-old Elijah Hawkins and 3-year-old Kashh Williams were found deceased in a pond near their home at 1361 N Clyde Morris Boulevard on Sunday, October 11, 2026, after being reported missing. DBPD said the boys had been seen walking on foot in the area. A memorial of balloons, candles and stuffed animals was placed near the pond. Police are investigating the circumstances.
