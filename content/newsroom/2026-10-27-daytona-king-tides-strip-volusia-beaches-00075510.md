## Why it matters

King tides are dramatically eroding Volusia beaches and exposing seawalls, raising questions about whether a recent taxpayer-funded sand project worked. Beach erosion directly affects coastal property, tourism and public safety across the area. Residents are visibly frustrated about where their tax money went.

## What happened

Powerful king tides reshaped Volusia County's coastline, stripping sand and exposing seawalls, especially at 27th Beachfront Park and along New Smyrna Beach. The county recently completed a 550,000-cubic-yard sand placement project over four-plus miles of shoreline, which residents say has already washed away. County officials say the sand may have shifted offshore where it still absorbs wave energy, and that a full damage assessment won't be possible until the tidal event ends mid- to late week.
