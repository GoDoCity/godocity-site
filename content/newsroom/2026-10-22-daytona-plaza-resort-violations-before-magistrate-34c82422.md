## Why it matters

The Plaza Resort is a historic Daytona Beach landmark that has sat empty since 2022, and its stalled redevelopment affects 600-plus condo-hotel owners and a closed Seabreeze Boulevard beach access point. The city holding the owner accountable is a high-interest local saga about a prominent property. Readers care about whether this beachfront eyesore will revive.

## What happened

At a Tuesday hearing, Special Magistrate David Vukelja pressed Plaza Resort owner Jonathan Abraham Eid over code violations and health/safety concerns dating to an August noncompliance declaration. Eid said some exterior work is done but interior work awaits engineers and there is still no definitive construction plan. No new fines were issued. More than 600 unit owners remain in financial limbo, paying dues without access to units. The next hearing is Dec. 8, when the magistrate wants permits and a concrete plan. A related Seabreeze Boulevard beach access point remains closed for repairs.
