## Why it matters

This is critical public-impact news: schools countywide canceled Friday, an Edgewater state of emergency, roads closing and water entering homes. Up to a foot of rain threatens low-lying areas and the St. Johns River, directly affecting where residents can drive and whether kids are in school. Sandbag availability and safety guidance are immediately useful.

## What happened

A prolonged stretch of heavy rain prompted Volusia and Flagler schools to cancel classes and activities Friday, Nov. 20. Edgewater declared a state of emergency with road closures and reports of water entering homes. Forecasters projected up to 12 inches of rain in spots, with most areas seeing 3-6 inches through Friday evening. Volusia is distributing sandbags at 13 locations; Lake Harney and St. Johns River areas face sustained flooding. A system near the Bahamas could develop into Tropical Storm Kyle.
