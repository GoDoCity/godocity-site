## Why it matters

Raw sewage spilling into streets and yards is a genuine public-health concern affecting South Palmetto Avenue residents. It raises questions about aging infrastructure and whether the city will fix a problem residents say dates back to Hurricanes Ian and Nicole. This is real public-impact news, not fun filler.

## What happened

Residents of a South Daytona neighborhood on South Palmetto Avenue say heavy rain caused a manhole cover to lift, spilling sewage and wastewater into streets and yards. Resident Timothy Lynch said hundreds of thousands of gallons were pumped out over five days, leaving sewage sludge and debris. He says the recurring problem dates to Hurricanes Ian and Nicole and is calling on city leaders for a solution.
