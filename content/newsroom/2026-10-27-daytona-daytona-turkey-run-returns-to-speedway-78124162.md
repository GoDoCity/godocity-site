## Why it matters

The Turkey Run is one of the area's biggest annual draws, pulling thousands of classic-car fans and tourists to the Speedway over Thanksgiving weekend. It's a quintessential Daytona event that readers plan their holiday weekend around and a boost for local business.

## What happened

The 53rd annual Daytona Turkey Run, billed as the largest combined classic car show and swap meet in the country, will run Wednesday, Nov. 25 through Sunday, Nov. 29 at Daytona International Speedway, featuring thousands of classic cars, a swap meet, arts and crafts, food and music.

## Key facts

- 53rd annual Daytona Turkey Run, Nov. 25-29 at Daytona International Speedway
- Billed as the largest combined classic car show and swap meet in the U.S.
- Hours 8 a.m.-4 p.m. Thu-Sat, 8 a.m.-2 p.m. Sun
- Admission $21 per person, per day
- Tickets and info at turkeyrun.com
