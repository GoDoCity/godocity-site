## Why it matters

More than 300 families reported property damage across Volusia from last week's flooding, with Edgewater, Oak Hill and parts of New Smyrna Beach, Port Orange and Daytona Beach hit hardest. This is critical public-impact news affecting where residents live and real money in their pockets. Road closures and recovery resources are actionable for readers.

## What happened

Floodwaters receded across parts of Volusia County after heavy rainfall last week, leaving families to race against mold and further damage. County officials reported 300+ self-reported property-damage instances, roughly 30 water rescues, 17 displaced households placed in temporary housing, and no deaths or injuries. Edgewater's Florida Shores neighborhood saw about 100 affected homes; some Georgetowne roads remained impassable.

## Key facts
