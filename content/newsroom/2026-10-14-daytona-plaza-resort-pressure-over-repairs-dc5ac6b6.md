## Why it matters

The Plaza Resort is one of Daytona Beach's most recognizable oceanfront landmarks and has sat closed and deteriorating since 2022. Readers care about the fate of a prominent beachfront property, and a recent TikTok break-in by 13 people made its vacancy a safety and eyesore concern. The city's push for a firm construction timeline signals possible movement on a long-stalled story.

## What happened

Daytona Beach officials, through a special magistrate, are pressuring the owners of the long-closed Plaza Resort to fix code violations and stabilize the hurricane-damaged building. Owners say they've addressed exterior issues like broken windows and peeling paint but still need roof and structural repairs; they say engineers are drawing up plans and permits. The city wants a firm start and completion schedule. The case returns to the special magistrate Dec. 8.
