## Why it matters

This directly affects every household in New Smyrna Beach with a new hauler, new totes, changed pickup schedules, and the end of curbside recycling. Service and money changes like this are exactly what residents talk about and need to plan for. The recycling discontinuation is likely to spark community debate.

## What happened

Effective Oct. 1, 2026, New Smyrna Beach will switch from Waste Pro to FCC Environmental Services. Households receive a navy blue 96-gallon tote starting Aug. 31, with a 64-gallon option after 90 days and a $290 bear-proof variant available. New schedule includes two household waste days and one yard waste day weekly, with bulk pickup up to four cubic yards. Curbside recycling will be discontinued due to contamination concerns, with voluntary drop-off sites being explored.
