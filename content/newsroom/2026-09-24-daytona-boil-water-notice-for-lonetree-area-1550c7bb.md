## Why it matters

Boil water notices are direct public-safety and public-health information residents on the affected street need immediately. Even a small-area notice qualifies under the critical-public-impact exception because it affects what people can safely drink and cook with.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach issued a boil water notice for the Lonetree Look area. The published alert contained only boilerplate public-records language and no operational details in the scraped body.

## Key facts

- Boil water notice issued by City of Ormond Beach
- Affected area listed as Lonetree Look
- Notice posted Sept. 23, 2026
