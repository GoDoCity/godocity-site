## Why it matters

E-bikes and micromobility devices are increasingly common on NSB streets and beachside, so new rules affect many riders, families and visitors. The city emphasizes it isn't a ban but a push for clear, enforceable safety rules. The ordinance returns for a second reading Nov. 10, giving readers a chance to weigh in.

## What happened

New Smyrna Beach held a public forum where Police Chief Chris Kirk presented proposed e-bike and micromobility ordinance changes to an estimated 30-40 attendees. Feedback will be considered before the City Commission takes it up for second reading on Nov. 10. Officials stressed the goal is safety and enforceability, not banning e-bikes.
