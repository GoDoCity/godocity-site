## Why it matters

A fatal hit-and-run on a major Daytona Beach thoroughfare is a genuine public-safety matter, and police are asking residents for help identifying the driver and vehicle. The suspect vehicle remains at large, which could concern drivers and residents along LPGA Boulevard.

## What happened

A 43-year-old woman was found in the roadway around 4:30 a.m. Saturday in the 1500 block of LPGA Boulevard and later died of her injuries. Police say a dark-colored passenger car or small crossover struck her, turned around, passed the dying victim and fled westbound at high speed. Detectives are seeking the public's help identifying the vehicle, believed to have front-end and windshield damage.
