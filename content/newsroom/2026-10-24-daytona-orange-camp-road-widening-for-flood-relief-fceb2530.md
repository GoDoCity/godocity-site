## Why it matters

Orange Camp Road is a major DeLand-area thoroughfare that has flooded for years and snarls daily traffic. A widening-and-drainage project affects how residents drive, where water goes during storms, and reflects fast growth around DeLand.

## What happened

Volusia County is advancing a road and drainage project on Orange Camp Road from Woodland Boulevard to Kirkaldry Court, including added lanes, retention ponds and a possible roundabout. The county already bought and demolished several homes along the corridor. Council was asked at Tuesday's meeting to select an engineering firm for design; construction remains years off.

## Key facts

- Project runs Woodland Boulevard to Kirkaldry Court, just outside DeLand city limits
- Includes lane expansion, retention ponds and a possible roundabout
- County already purchased and demolished homes along the corridor
- Council was asked Tuesday to select an engineering design firm
- Identified as a top countywide road priority
- Construction is still some time away pending contract approval
