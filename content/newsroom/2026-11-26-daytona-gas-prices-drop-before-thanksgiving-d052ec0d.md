## Why it matters

Thanksgiving is a major travel holiday and gas prices hit every driver's wallet. A 40-cent drop since early November is practical, pocketbook news readers can use, with specific local cheap-gas locations in Daytona Beach and Volusia.

## What happened

AAA data showed Florida's average gas price fell to $3.42 as of late Tuesday, down over 40 cents since early November. Volusia County averaged $3.48, with the cheapest public pumps including Costco, Sam's Club, and a cash-only Shell on LPGA Boulevard at $3.24 in Daytona Beach. Prices remain higher than last Thanksgiving's $3.09.
