## Why it matters

Families with young kids are a core local audience, and new playgrounds are the kind of shareable, feel-good improvement parents talk about. The story also previews two more distinctive playgrounds coming this fall, giving readers reasons to visit multiple parks.

## What happened

New Smyrna Beach opened a new two-tower playground on padded turf at Esther Street Beachfront Park, two blocks north of Flagler Avenue at 551 Esther St., replacing an older monochromatic playground on wood mulch. Two more projects are underway: a 30-foot baseball-themed net tower with sensory and inclusive amenities at the NSB Sports Complex, and a 20-foot climbing dome with swings and toddler play tower at Detwiler Park in Central Beach this fall.

## Key facts

- New two-tower playground now open at Esther Street Beachfront Park, 551 Esther St.
- Replaces an older monochromatic playground on wood mulch, now on padded turf
- 30-foot baseball-themed net tower with inclusive/sensory amenities under construction at NSB Sports Complex
- 20-foot climbing dome plus toddler tower coming to Detwiler Park this fall
