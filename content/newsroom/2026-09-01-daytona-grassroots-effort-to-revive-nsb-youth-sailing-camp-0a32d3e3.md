## Why it matters

A community-driven fundraising push to revive a beloved youth sailing camp is exactly the kind of local win readers enjoy and can rally behind. Parents and boating enthusiasts would care about a new recreational option for kids, and the effort is nearly funded, making it timely.

## What happened

Smyrna Yacht Club member Brian Flora has been urging city leaders to revive New Smyrna Beach's youth sailing camp, which once taught teens sailing on the Indian River. Flora told the mayor and commissioners on July 28 that a grassroots effort has raised $90,000 of the $110,000 needed for boats, instructors and curriculum. He said the city only needs to provide fencing, a tent and portable toilets at Callalisa Park to launch six weeks of sailing programs.

## Key facts

- Grassroots effort has raised $90,000 of $110,000 goal
- Original camp served ages 12-18 on the Indian River
