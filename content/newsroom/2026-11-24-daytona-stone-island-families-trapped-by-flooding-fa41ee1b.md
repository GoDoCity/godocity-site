## Why it matters

This is a critical public-impact story: the St. Johns River has reached historic levels, trapping Volusia County families in their homes with waist-deep water and officials warning flooding could persist through the week. Residents need to know about evacuation efforts, temporary facilities, and ongoing risk. It directly affects where people in western Volusia live and whether they can safely stay in their homes.

## What happened

Five days after heavy rain inundated Central Florida, families in the Stone Island community near Deltona remain surrounded by rising floodwaters from the swollen St. Johns River. Some homes are cut off, residents are using kayaks to navigate, and alligators have been spotted near doorsteps. One family, including a son who uses a wheelchair, was rescued by raft after being trapped five days. The city set up temporary restroom and shower facilities, and officials expect water to keep rising through the week.
