## Why it matters

## What happened

New Smyrna Beach's Casey Sparks ran seven marathons on seven continents in seven days, returning home over the weekend. He has raised about $20,000 for No Longer Fatherless, a nonprofit mentoring fatherless boys in Volusia and Flagler counties, and is making a final push ahead of the organization's annual gala on Saturday. He won race six in Cartagena, Colombia, and called Antarctica the hardest leg.
