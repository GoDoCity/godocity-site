## Why it matters

A local human-interest win: a DeLand husband-and-wife writing team earned a statewide award for their Florida marina-set mystery novel. It's the kind of feel-good, shareable local-pride story that resonates with readers and book lovers, drawing on authentic Florida waterfront flavor.

## What happened

DeLand authors Renée and Robert 'Bob' Garrison won a silver medal for adult cover design in the 2026 Florida Authors and Publishers Association President's Book Awards for their novel The Harbormaster. The mystery, inspired by Bob's years working as a harbormaster, follows an amateur investigator at a small Florida marina after a boat explosion and a death.

## Key facts

- Authors Renée and Robert 'Bob' Garrison live in DeLand
- The Harbormaster won silver for cover design in the 2026 Florida Authors and Publishers Association President's Book Awards
- Cover designed by Nancy R. Koucky
- Novel inspired by Bob's real career as a harbormaster
- Available in paperback and hardcover via online booksellers and local bookstore request
