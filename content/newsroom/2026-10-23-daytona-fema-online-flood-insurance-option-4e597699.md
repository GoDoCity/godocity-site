## Why it matters

Flood-prone Daytona Beach homeowners deal with hurricanes and flooding regularly, and most standard homeowners policies don't cover flood damage. A new direct-online NFIP purchasing option plus a 30-day waiting-period reminder is genuinely useful money-and-safety info for local residents.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach shared that FEMA launched a new online service letting homeowners and property owners get quotes and buy National Flood Insurance Program policies directly online, without an agent. The city reminded residents most flood policies have a 30-day waiting period before coverage starts.

## Key facts

- FEMA launched a direct online NFIP quote-and-purchase option
- Most standard homeowners policies don't cover flood damage
- Most flood policies carry a 30-day waiting period
- Help at floodsmart.gov or (877) 336-2627
- Shared via City of Daytona Beach alert
