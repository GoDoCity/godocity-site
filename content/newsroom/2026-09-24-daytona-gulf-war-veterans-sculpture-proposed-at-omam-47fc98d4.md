## Why it matters

A permanent public sculpture honoring Gulf War veterans would be a lasting new landmark at the Ormond Memorial Art Museum in the heart of downtown. Veterans memorials tend to resonate strongly with local residents and could become a point of community pride and a reason to visit.

## What happened

The Ormond Beach Arts District Board scheduled a public hearing for 4:00 PM Monday, October 5, 2026, at City Hall to consider a request from the Ormond Memorial Art Museum's executive director for a permanent public art sculpture honoring Gulf War veterans who served in Operation Desert Shield and Desert Storm. The sculpture would be placed at OMAM on East Granada Boulevard within the Downtown Community Redevelopment Area, processed under the city's public arts program.

## Key facts

- Public hearing set for 4:00 PM Monday, October 5, 2026 at Ormond Beach City Hall, Conference Room 103
- Request submitted by Stephanie Mason-Teague, OMAM Executive Director
- Sculpture would honor Gulf War veterans of Operation Desert Shield and Desert Storm
- Located at Ormond Memorial Art Museum, 78 East Granada Blvd, in the Downtown CRA
- Processed under Chapter 18 public arts program in the Downtown Overlay District
