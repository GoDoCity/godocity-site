## Why it matters

Flooding closed multiple New Smyrna Beach roads, and residents need to know which routes are reopened and where crews are still working. Road conditions and pumping operations directly affect daily commutes and safety.

## What happened

The City of New Smyrna Beach reported reopening several roads after heavy rain, including Sunset Avenue, Turnbull Bay Road, Halleck Street, Wayne Avenue, Williams Road, Pioneer Trail, Sugar Mill Drive, and South Myrtle Avenue, with Wildwood Drive expected to reopen the same day. Repairs continue at North Peninsula Avenue & Due East Street, an undermined roadway at 1328 Beacon St. is being inspected, and pumps are operating at several locations with crews seeking a pumping site in the Islesboro neighborhood.

## Key facts

- Multiple NSB roads reopened after heavy rain
- Repairs ongoing at North Peninsula Ave & Due East St
- Undermined roadway at 1328 Beacon St being addressed
- Pumps operating at Southard, Surf, and Dougherty streets
- Crews seeking pumping location in Islesboro neighborhood
