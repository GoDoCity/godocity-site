## Why it matters

E-bikes and scooters are a growing presence on NSB streets, sidewalks and paths, and the proposed rules would restrict where they can go and add helmet requirements with fines. Residents, parents and riders have a direct stake in what gets decided, and the city is opening the process to public input.

## What happened

At the Aug. 25 meeting, commissioners agreed with Mayor Fred Cleveland to postpone consideration of a new micromobility ordinance until Nov. 10 to gather more public input. The proposed rules would restrict bikes, e-bikes and scooters from certain sidewalks, paths, parking/business districts, parks and courts, and require helmets for riders 16-17 under a $150 fine. A public workshop is set for 5 p.m. Sept. 29 at the Hathaway Commission Chambers.
