## Why it matters

'Revenue strategies' is government-speak for potential new taxes, fees or rate increases that could hit DeLand residents' wallets. Any decision requiring procedural action before Jan. 1 could move fast, so residents who want input have a narrow window. This is the kind of money-in-your-pocket story worth watching.

## What happened

The DeLand City Commission scheduled a special meeting for Monday, Nov. 9, 2026 at 6 p.m. at City Hall to get a first-pass overview of potential FY 2027/28 revenue strategies, decide which options merit further study, and give direction on items needing procedural action before Jan. 1, 2027.

## Key facts

- Special meeting set for Monday, Nov. 9, 2026 at 6 p.m. at DeLand City Hall
- Purpose: first-pass overview of FY 2027/28 revenue strategies
- Some options require procedural action before Jan. 1, 2027
- Public participation encouraged via Speaker's Cards
- Agenda available at deland.org
