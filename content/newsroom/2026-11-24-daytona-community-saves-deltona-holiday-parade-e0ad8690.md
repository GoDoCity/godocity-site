## Why it matters

Deltona first canceled its beloved holiday parade amid major budget cuts, then reversed course after community groups stepped up — a feel-good civic story with a real-money backdrop. It also signals that Amendment 3's property tax changes are starting to force tough local budget decisions that could affect services across Volusia.

## What happened

Deltona announced Saturday it would cancel its annual Holiday Parade and most city events citing roughly $20 million in anticipated revenue losses from Amendment 3. Within 48 hours, several community organizations offered time and resources, and City Manager Doc Dougherty said the parade would go forward without placing the full cost on taxpayers.

## Key facts

- Parade was canceled Saturday, then reinstated after community groups offered support
- Deltona faces about $20M in reduced revenue over two years, needing ~$12M in cuts next year
- City is canceling other events except two 5K races, ending construction contracts and withdrawing vehicle orders
- City expects to cut more than $23M from its budget by 2030
- Amendment 3 passed with 62% voter approval earlier this month
