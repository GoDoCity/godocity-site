## Why it matters

Major flooding has submerged roads, cars and apartment complexes across Daytona Beach and nearby cities, displacing residents and prompting rescues. This is critical public-impact news affecting where people live, drive and work, with shelters closing and recovery resources starting Monday.

## What happened

After days of heavy rain, Daytona Beach Mayor Derrick Henry announced Friday evening that the city was shifting from storm response to recovery. The Atlantic High School shelter closed, with no Volusia shelters open over the weekend and displaced residents given hotel vouchers. Flooding submerged roads including LPGA Boulevard and Beville Road, flooded apartment parking lots and cars, and required airboat and high-water vehicle rescues. New Smyrna Beach, Edgewater, Port Orange and Holly Hill also declared local states of emergency, and NSB warned of life-threatening electrical hazards in Corbin Park from submerged transformers.
