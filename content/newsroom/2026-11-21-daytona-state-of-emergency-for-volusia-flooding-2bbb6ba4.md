## Why it matters

A state of emergency directly covering Volusia County signals serious flooding risk for Daytona-area residents, affecting safety, travel, and property. Readers need to know resources are being mobilized and what conditions to expect. This is critical public-impact news that cannot be ignored.

## What happened

On Thursday, Gov. Ron DeSantis issued a state of emergency covering 21 Florida counties, including Volusia and Flagler, amid historic flooding along the state's east coast. The order runs through the end of 2026. The state mobilized the Florida National Guard, State Guard, 200 FHSMV troopers, 235 FWC officers, and deployed high-water vehicles, pumps, boats, airboats, and 750 FDOT crews. Scrutiny has emerged over DeSantis's 2026 veto of about $18.4 million in local stormwater drainage funding.
