## Why it matters

A presidential visit to the Ocean Center means major road closures, traffic jams and a security 'lockdown' across the beachside peninsula on Friday evening. Residents, workers and visitors need to know what to avoid and when. This is a high-impact event affecting travel and daily life throughout the beachside.

## What happened

President Trump was expected to speak at the Ocean Center on Friday evening at 7 p.m. (doors 3:30 p.m.). Police vehicles, barricades and closure signs were already in place, and the adjacent six-story parking garage was closed. Sheriff Mike Chitwood said multiple agencies were assisting, warned the peninsula would 'go into lockdown' with street and road closures and traffic jams, and said airspace restrictions and a drone ban would be enforced. Specific closure locations and the traffic plan were still being finalized after a Friday afternoon planning meeting.
