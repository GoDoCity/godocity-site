## Why it matters

This is a first-in-Florida public tool that lets residents look up convicted domestic violence offenders countywide, affecting how people vet acquaintances, dates and neighbors. It's a significant county government action with broad public-safety relevance that readers will discuss and likely use.

## What happened

Volusia County launched what officials call Florida's first public database of people convicted of felony or misdemeanor domestic/dating violence crimes in the county. Built from Clerk of Court records, it covers convictions (not arrests) going back 10 years and lists names, mugshots, DOBs and identifying details. No victim info is included. The County Council approved it in July on a 6-1 vote, sponsored by Councilmember Matt Reinhart and modeled on a Tennessee statewide database.

## Key facts

- Described as Florida's first public domestic violence conviction database
- Covers convictions only, not arrests, going back 10 years
- Includes name, mugshot, DOB and identifying details; no victim info
- Built from Volusia County Clerk of Court case management records
- Approved July on a 6-1 County Council vote, sponsored by Matt Reinhart
- Modeled after a Tennessee statewide database; available on county website
