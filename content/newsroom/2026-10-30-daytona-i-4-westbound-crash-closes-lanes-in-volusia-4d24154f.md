## Why it matters

A major crash blocking two westbound I-4 lanes at DeBary Avenue affects commuters heading from Volusia into Seminole County during morning rush hour. Traffic disruptions on I-4 are a daily pain point for area drivers.

## What happened

Florida Highway Patrol reported a crash just after 5 a.m. Friday on I-4 westbound at DeBary Avenue that closed two left lanes. FHP's incident page describes it as a hit-and-run with injuries, and drivers were warned to expect significant delays entering Seminole County.

- Two left lanes closed

- Significant delays for Volusia-to-Seminole commuters
