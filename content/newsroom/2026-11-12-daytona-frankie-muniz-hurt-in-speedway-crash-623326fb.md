## What happened

On Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, actor and NASCAR driver Frankie Muniz crashed during a practice lap at Daytona International Speedway while preparing for the Ford Veterans Day Classic exhibition race. Rescue crews had to cut the roof off his Team Air Force car to extract him. His team said he was awake and alert and taken to a local medical facility. He did not compete in the evening event.
