## Why it matters

Flooding has repeatedly damaged homes and roads in Orange City, including during Hurricane Milton in 2024. A major new pump station promising 33x the current capacity is real money and real public-safety relief for residents who live with repeated flooding.

## What happened

Orange City and Volusia County are advancing a multi-million-dollar stormwater project that would connect drainage systems to a new pump station discharging to a city-owned site on West French Avenue. The new station could move more than 33 times the water of the current one. Design and permitting are complete, with three construction options ranging from about $19 million to $27 million, and work could begin as early as early 2027.

## Key facts

- New pump station would move 33x more water than current one
- Three construction options ranging ~$19M-$27M
- Discharge site is city-owned land on West French Avenue
- Final design and permitting complete; construction could begin early 2027
- Area flooded badly during Hurricane Milton in 2024
- Plan is a joint Orange City and Volusia County effort
