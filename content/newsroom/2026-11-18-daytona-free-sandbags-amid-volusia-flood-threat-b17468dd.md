## Why it matters

A prolonged heavy-rain event brings flooding risk across Volusia this week, and free sandbags are a practical, timely public-safety resource residents can act on immediately. This meets the critical-public-impact exception and gives readers actionable where-to-go info.

## What happened

Central Florida faces several days of heavy rain with flooding risk. Volusia communities are offering free sandbags: Daytona Beach at Bethune Point Park (noon-5 p.m. Nov. 17 and 8 a.m.-5 p.m. Nov. 18), Edgewater at Rotary Park and Public Works, and New Smyrna Beach at 124 Industrial Park Ave (up to 15 bags with photo ID through Friday).

## Key facts

- Prolonged heavy rain and flooding risk across Central Florida this week

- Edgewater: Rotary Park and Public Works Operations Facility

- Seniors/disabled in NSB may get up to 10 pre-filled bags
