## Why it matters

A cold front is bringing 40-45 mph wind gusts, 8-10 foot waves and high rip-current risk to Volusia beaches this weekend, with red flags flying. This is a real public-safety situation affecting anyone heading to the beach, and the king-tide timing threatens fresh beach-restoration work and raises coastal flooding/erosion concerns.

## What happened

Volusia County beach safety officials warned of dangerous swimming conditions as a cold front moves through Central Florida. Wind gusts could reach 40-45 mph beginning Saturday Oct. 17, with breaking waves of 8-10 feet. The combination with fall king tides heightens risks of rip currents, coastal flooding and beach erosion. Beach Safety planned to fly red flags and urged people to stay out of the water and follow lifeguard instructions.
