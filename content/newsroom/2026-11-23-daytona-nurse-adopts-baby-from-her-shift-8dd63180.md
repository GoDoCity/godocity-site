## Why it matters

A deeply moving human-interest story rooted at AdventHealth Fish Memorial in Orange City and finalized in a DeLand courtroom. It celebrates local healthcare workers rallying around one of their own, exactly the kind of uplifting, shareable story Volusia readers love.

## What happened

Andrea Small, a 51-year-old Orange City registered nurse and case manager at AdventHealth Fish Memorial, took home a 2-day-old baby in 2025 after his parents couldn't care for him, then fostered and ultimately adopted him. The adoption was finalized Oct. 29, 2026, in DeLand, naming the boy Westin Reid Small. Her hospital colleagues supplied baby gear, child care and support throughout the year.

## Key facts

- Andrea Small, 51, of Orange City is an RN/case manager at AdventHealth Fish Memorial
- She took the 2-day-old home after a shift in 2025; baby weighed 4 lbs 11 oz at birth
- Adoption finalized 10:27 a.m. Oct. 29, 2026, in DeLand; child named Westin Reid Small
- Hospital colleagues provided a Pack 'n Play, formula, essentials and child care
- Westin's Sept. 11 birthday falls near that of Small's firstborn Madelynn, who died at 6.5 weeks
