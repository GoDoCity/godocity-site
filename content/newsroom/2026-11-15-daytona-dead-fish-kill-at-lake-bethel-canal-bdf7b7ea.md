## Why it matters

Fish kills can signal water-quality problems that concern residents near Volusia lakes and canals. The uncertainty over the cause — ranging from natural oxygen depletion to possible dumping or spills — raises environmental questions. However, this is a localized incident near Enterprise, outside the core Daytona coverage area.

## What happened

A homeowner near a Lake Bethel canal off Enterprise Road reported dead fish, including bass, piling up over three days. FWC said heavy rain can cause localized fish kills through oxygen depletion but lacks enough information to determine the cause. The homeowner suspects dumping or runoff and is unsure how to dispose of the fish.

## Key facts

- Dead fish concentrated in one canal near Enterprise Road at Lake Bethel
- Homeowner David Schaefer noticed them three days before the report
- FWC cites heavy rain and oxygen depletion as a common cause
- Other possible causes include algal blooms, spills, disease, herbicides
- FWC says it lacks enough information to determine the cause
