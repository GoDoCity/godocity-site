## Why it matters

A city sandbag site opening signals an approaching weather threat and gives residents a free way to protect their homes from flooding. This is practical, time-sensitive public-impact info readers act on immediately.

## What happened

The City of Port Orange opened its sandbag site at 4505 Clyde Morris Blvd. on Thursday, Nov. 19 at 9 a.m., remaining open as weather permits. The site is self-service; residents must bring their own shovel and valid ID. Pre-filled bags are available for residents with disabilities and older adults.
