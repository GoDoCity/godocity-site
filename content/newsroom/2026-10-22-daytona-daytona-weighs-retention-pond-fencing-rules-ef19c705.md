## Why it matters

Two young brothers drowned in a retention pond earlier this month, prompting city officials to explore safety fencing requirements at multi-family complexes. This is a public-safety policy change that could affect apartment residents and developers across the city. It touches a tragic local loss that the community is actively following.

## What happened

At a Wednesday night meeting, Daytona Beach commissioners discussed potentially requiring fencing around retention ponds at multi-family housing complexes. The move follows the drowning deaths of brothers Kashh (3) and Elijah (5), who wandered from their apartment near Clyde Morris Boulevard. City staff will explore concepts and bring ideas back to the commission at a later meeting.

## Key facts

- Two brothers, ages 3 and 5, drowned in a retention pond near Clyde Morris Boulevard earlier in October
