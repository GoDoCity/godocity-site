## What happened

Floodwaters from the rising St. Johns River have trapped some Stone Island residents near Deltona in their homes for days. A family was rescued Tuesday night, and parts of the neighborhood remain underwater even after rain stopped. One resident, Wesley Tykeson, said he has pumped water out for four days as it reached his garage and neared his front door. Experts say the river, at historic levels, is expected to keep rising.
