## Why it matters

Beachgoers and park users at North Beach Community Park will get a cooler, splinter-free walkway made from recycled materials. It's a small but tangible improvement to a popular public access point, and beach access stays open during the two-week project.

## What happened

Crews began replacing aging, rotting wooden boardwalks at Grayce K. Barck North Beach Community Park with recycled composite decking on Monday, Oct. 12. The work is expected to take about two weeks. The new material resists splinters, stays cooler and requires less maintenance. Dune walkover beach access remains open during construction.
