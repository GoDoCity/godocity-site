## Why it matters

The fire chief leads a $21.3 million department with seven stations serving all of Daytona Beach, so the pick affects public safety citywide. The family legacy angle — Hughes following his father as chief — adds a genuinely uplifting human-interest hook that resonates locally.

## What happened

City Manager Deric Feacher promoted Chris Hughes, a 25-year department veteran and interim chief since July 2026, to Daytona Beach Fire Chief. Hughes is the department's 20th chief and follows his father Gary Hughes, who served as chief from 2008 to 2010. A swearing-in ceremony will be announced later.

## Key facts

- Chris Hughes named 20th Daytona Beach Fire Chief, promoted from interim
- Joined department in 2001; served as engineer, lieutenant, battalion chief, deputy and interim chief
- Father Gary Hughes was chief 2008-2010
- Department has seven stations, 131 budgeted positions, $21.3M budget
- Hughes is a certified Fire Officer III, paramedic and registered nurse
- Swearing-in/badge-pinning ceremony to be announced
