## Why it matters

This directly affects anyone heading to Daytona-area beaches, especially with dangerous surf and rip currents expected this weekend. The new flag system changes what beachgoers should watch for, and rip currents have driven record rescue numbers in recent years. It's practical, timely safety information with real public impact.

## What happened

Volusia Beach Safety launched a new color-coded flag warning system at lifeguard towers. Red flags signal dangerous rip currents/hazardous conditions, yellow indicates moderate surf, and purple warns of dangerous marine life. The flags replace the orange flag that previously just marked a staffed tower. Flags can change throughout the day as conditions shift, and officials rolled it out as dangerous surf was expected over the weekend.

## Key facts

- Red = dangerous rip currents/hazardous water; yellow = moderate surf; purple = dangerous marine life
