## Why it matters

Residents across Volusia hit by recent flooding now have a single in-person location to access state and local recovery help. This is practical, money-in-pocket public-impact news for anyone affected, and the notice clarifies FEMA aid is not currently available.

## What happened

The State of Florida is opening a Multi-Agency Resource Center (MARC) at the Dickerson Community Center in Daytona Beach beginning Friday, Nov. 27, to help Volusia County residents affected by recent flooding. The center offers in-person connections to state agencies and local organizations for recovery guidance and resources. It serves all Volusia residents regardless of municipality. Because there is no presidential disaster declaration, FEMA Individual Assistance is not available; the MARC connects residents to nonfederal resources instead.

## Key facts

- MARC opens Friday, Nov. 27 at Dickerson Community Center, 308 S. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd., Daytona Beach
- Open to all Volusia County residents affected by recent flooding
- Assistance provided in person only during operating hours
- No presidential disaster declaration means FEMA Individual Assistance is not available
- Established at request of City of Daytona Beach, funded/coordinated by FDEM, with county support
