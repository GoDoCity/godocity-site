## Why it matters

A fun, low-cost fitness class gives Daytona Beach residents a quirky new way to stay active. It's the kind of community activity that can appeal to readers looking for something different to try.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced Cardio Drumming fitness classes at Schnebly Recreation Center on Oct. 20, Nov. 3 and Nov. 10, from 9:30-10:30 a.m. Cost is $5-$10 per class depending on equipment, limited to 15 participants with advance reservations encouraged.

## Key facts

- Classes at Schnebly Recreation Center, 1101 N. Atlantic Ave.
- Dates: Oct. 20, Nov. 3, Nov. 10, 9:30-10:30 a.m.
- $5 with own equipment, $10 using center's
- Limited to 15 participants; reservations encouraged
- Call (386) 671-3560 to reserve
