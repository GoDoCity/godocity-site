## Why it matters

Holiday beachgoers need to know where they can and can't park on the sand. About 125 spaces near five parks will be temporarily unavailable Saturday through Monday, plus the Seabreeze ramp closure limits driving on a half-mile stretch. This is practical, actionable info for anyone heading to the coast.

## What happened

Volusia County and the Sheriff's Office will keep select on-beach parking areas clear near five beachfront parks from Saturday through Monday over Labor Day weekend as a pedestrian safety measure. Roughly 125 spaces (under 3% of the county's 5,000+ on-beach spaces) will be coned off, though vehicles can still drive through. Permanent restrictions remain in front of three other parks. The county also notes the Seabreeze Boulevard ramp remains closed, blocking beach driving on about a half mile between University and Seabreeze.

## Key facts

- Restrictions in place Saturday through Monday over Labor Day weekend
