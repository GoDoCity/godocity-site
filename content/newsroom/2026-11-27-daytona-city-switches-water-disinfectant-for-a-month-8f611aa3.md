## Why it matters

This affects every resident and business on Daytona Beach city water, plus some South Daytona customers, who may notice a stronger chlorine smell or taste for several weeks. While routine, it's the kind of practical heads-up readers want so they don't panic about their tap water.

## What happened

Starting Thursday, Nov. 26, the city temporarily switched its water disinfectant from chloramines to free chlorine, a routine month-long maintenance process. Water remains safe to drink, though some customers may notice a stronger smell or taste. The city will switch back to chloramines on Saturday, Dec. 26, and offered tips to reduce the chlorine taste.

## Key facts

- Switch from chloramines to free chlorine began Nov. 26
- Reverts to chloramines Dec. 26
- Water remains safe to drink and use
- South Daytona customers among those who may notice stronger taste/smell
- Tips: run tap, refrigerate in open pitcher, add citrus or cucumber
