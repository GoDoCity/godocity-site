## Why it matters

Rising St. Johns River flooding is a real public-impact story for western Volusia residents, with a human-interest angle of rescuing stranded livestock. It carries practical warnings for anyone living along the river to prepare their animals as conditions worsen.

## What happened

Volusia County sheriff's deputies teamed up with animal services to reach a household in Osteen cut off by rising floodwater, helping check on goats, turkeys and chickens the residents could no longer reach. Officials warned residents along the St. Johns River to plan for their animals' food, water, medication and access needs as water levels keep rising.
