## Why it matters

A beloved holiday tradition was canceled over budget fears, then revived by community organizations — a feel-good turnaround with a deeper story about how Amendment 3 budget cuts could reshape Deltona's events. Readers in western Volusia care about both the parade and what it signals for future city services.

## What happened

Deltona canceled its annual Christmas parade citing projected revenue losses tied to Florida's Amendment 3, but reversed the decision Monday, Nov. 23 after community organizations offered time and resources. The parade is now set for Saturday, Dec. 12 at 6 p.m. on Deltona Boulevard. The city projects nearly $20 million in reduced revenue over two years and says no department is off the table.
