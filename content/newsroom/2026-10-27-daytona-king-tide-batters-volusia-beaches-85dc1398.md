## Why it matters

Dangerous surf and king tides are eroding dunes, toppling a toll booth and prompting beach access and Jetty Connector closures across Volusia — a real public-safety and taxpayer-money story for anyone who lives near or visits the coast. Residents are also debating whether millions spent on dune projects were worth it.

## What happened

A king tide and rough surf created hazardous coastal conditions along Volusia County, leveling newly built dunes at 27th Avenue in New Smyrna Beach and eroding the coastline. The Dunlawton Beach approach toll booth tipped over, the Jetty Connector in Ponce Inlet was temporarily closed as a precaution, and additional beach access points closed out of caution. The county defended its dune placement projects as working as intended, saying sand moved offshore still protects the coast.
