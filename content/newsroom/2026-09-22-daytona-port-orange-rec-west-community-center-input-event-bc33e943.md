## Why it matters

A new community center is a lasting neighborhood amenity, and Port Orange residents have a direct chance to shape it. The Sept. 26 event offers free food and family activities, making it an easy, fun civic outing worth flagging before it happens.

## What happened

Port Orange is gathering resident input on the planned REC West Community Center through a community event from 10 a.m. to noon Saturday, Sept. 26, at Airport Road Park, featuring free food, kids activities and family fun. Residents unable to attend can weigh in via an online city survey.

## Key facts

- Event runs 10 a.m.–noon Saturday, Sept. 26, at Airport Road Park
- Free food (while supplies last), kids activities and family fun
- Input sought for the new REC West Community Center
- Online survey available as an alternative to attending
