## Why it matters

Rising St. Johns River flooding is a real public-impact story affecting Volusia residents' homes and safety. Officials warn the river will crest in days and urge early preparation, making this timely and actionable for readers in Deltona, DeLand, Astor and Enterprise.

## What happened

Floodwaters inundated roads and yards in the Stone Island area of Deltona, with residents sandbagging homes as the St. Johns River continues rising from runoff and last week's rain. Volusia County officials say the river will crest in a few days and warned residents in Astor, Enterprise, Lake Harney Woods and along the river to prepare early. The Jolly Roger Bait Shop in DeLand is surrounded by water and flooding has reached Hontoon Island State Park. Boaters are reminded to observe no-wake zones.
