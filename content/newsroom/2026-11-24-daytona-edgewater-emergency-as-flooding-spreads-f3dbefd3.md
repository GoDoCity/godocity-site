## Why it matters

Heavy rain has saturated eastern and southern Volusia, flooding roads and triggering an Edgewater state of emergency with more rain and St. Johns River flooding on the way. This is critical public-safety and practical news affecting where residents can drive, live and protect their homes. It qualifies as must-keep safety news despite its negative tone.

## What happened

After 6-8 inches of rain over 72 hours and 3-6 more inches expected, Edgewater declared a Local State of Emergency, advising Florida Shores residents to stay home. A Flood Watch runs through Friday, soil saturation hit ~100% in spots, and the St. Johns River is projected to reach major flood stage near Geneva, Astor and DeLand into the weekend. Officials urged sandbagging, avoiding flooded roads, and reporting damage.
