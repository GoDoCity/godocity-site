## Why it matters

A large mixed-use project with 348 apartments, a 120-room hotel and multiple restaurants would reshape a rural Volusia community at the I-4/Orange Camp Road gateway. It revives a development fight that played out in 2018 and raises familiar growth-vs-small-town questions relevant across west Volusia. Daytona-area readers who commute I-4 or follow county growth would take note.

## What happened

Property owners proposed a mixed-use development on land along I-4 and Orange Camp Road that was once eyed for a DeLand auto mall (rejected in 2018). The plan includes fast food restaurants, a sit-down restaurant, a 120-room hotel and 348 apartment units. Residents are split over traffic and small-town character, and Lake Helen leaders were expected to take up the proposal Thursday.

## Key facts
