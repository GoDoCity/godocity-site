## Why it matters

A presidential rally at the Ocean Center will trigger significant road closures and traffic snarls across the beachside, directly affecting anyone trying to drive, work, or visit the area Friday. The practical travel impact makes this critical public-information news regardless of politics. Residents need to know which roads close and when.

## What happened

The Volusia Sheriff's Office announced significant road closures and security measures for a presidential rally where President Trump is expected to speak at 7 p.m. Friday at the Ocean Center. Atlantic Avenue closes to vehicles from Oak Ridge Boulevard to Main Street 5-9 p.m.; Earl Street closes to vehicles at 8 a.m.; Peninsula Drive closes between Main and Ora streets at 4 p.m. Congestion is expected as early as 7 a.m., and authorities advise non-attendees to avoid the area.
