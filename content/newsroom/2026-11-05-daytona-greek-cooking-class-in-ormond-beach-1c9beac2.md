## Why it matters

A hands-on Greek cooking class is the kind of fun, local activity GoDoDaytona readers like to discover and share. It gives foodies something to look forward to and do in the slow winter months.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced a Greek cooking class with Chef Elaine Pitenis set for Thursday, January 21, 2027, from 11:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. The menu features traditional Greek soups like avgolemono, faki, fasoulada and youvarelakia, plus homemade pita, Greek salad and kataifi dessert.

## Key facts

- Date: Thursday, January 21, 2027, 11:30 a.m.–3 p.m.
- Led by Chef Elaine Pitenis
- Menu includes avgolemono, faki, fasoulada, youvarelakia soups
- Also pita bread, Greek salad and kataifi dessert
- Hosted by the City of Ormond Beach
