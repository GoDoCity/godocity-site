## Why it matters

The city budget sets the millage rate and funds everything from police to roads to utilities, so it directly touches every resident's wallet and services. The adoption of the rolled-back rate means tax implications are a real-money story worth explaining clearly.

## What happened

Daytona Beach adopted a $402.4 million budget for Fiscal Year 2026-27, funding operations, public safety, infrastructure, utilities and capital improvements through Sept. 30, 2027. The adopted millage rate is 5.7309, identified as the calculated rolled-back rate, with about $63.9 million in General Fund property tax revenue.

## Key facts

- Total budget: $402.4 million for FY 2026-27
- Millage rate set at 5.7309, described as the rolled-back rate
- General Fund includes about $63.9 million in property tax revenue
- Budget covers fiscal year ending Sept. 30, 2027
