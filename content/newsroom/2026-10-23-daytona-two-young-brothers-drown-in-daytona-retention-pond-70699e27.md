## Why it matters

The deaths of two young children in a neighborhood retention pond is a deeply felt tragedy that hits Daytona Beach families hard and raises safety questions about unfenced retention ponds near apartment complexes. It is major local news that the community will be talking about, and the pending State Attorney review adds an ongoing dimension.

## What happened

Daytona Beach Police Chief Jakari Young said 5-year-old Elijah Hawkins and 3-year-old Kashh Williams escaped their apartment through a child-locked door while their mother napped on Saturday, Oct. 10. A missing child alert was issued that evening, and the boys were found dead in a nearby retention pond hours later. Surveillance video showed the boys playing near the pond. Young called it an 'extremely tragic accident' with no obvious signs of foul play, but the case has been sent to the State Attorney's Office to determine whether charges are warranted.
