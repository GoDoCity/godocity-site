## Why it matters

The beachside redevelopment effort is a long-running local interest, and new tech aimed at helping tourists navigate and local businesses get noticed could shape the visitor experience. Readers care about what's changing on the beachside and whether taxpayers or the CRA would foot any bill.

## What happened

Technology company Rove iQ is set to present a proposal to Daytona Beach's Beachside Redevelopment Board to install interactive outdoor digital kiosks. The touchscreen displays would offer maps, directions, business listings, event info and advertising for visitors. The presentation does not mean approval, and key details like number, placement, cost and revenue-sharing remain unresolved.

## Key facts

- Rove iQ is pitching interactive outdoor kiosks to the Beachside Redevelopment Board
- Kiosks offer maps, directions, business listings, events and advertising
- Screens range from 55 to 75 inches, with larger options available
- Systems can use cameras and software to analyze visitor interaction, raising privacy questions
- No approval yet; cost, placement and revenue-sharing undecided
