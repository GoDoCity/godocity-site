## Why it matters

Beach driving is a signature Volusia experience, and ramp closures plus dangerous 7-11 foot surf directly affect residents and visitors planning beach trips this week. The erosion also raises pointed questions about the $25.8 million renourishment project completed just five months ago.

## What happened

Powerful surf and king tides have eroded Volusia beaches, exposing a seawall at NSB's 27th Avenue Beachfront Park and keeping beach driving ramps closed. The NWS extended a Coastal Flood Warning and High Surf Advisory through 2 a.m. Friday. County officials say they can't fully assess damage until the tidal event ends later this week.

## Key facts

- Coastal Flood Warning and High Surf Advisory extended through 2 a.m. Friday
- Breaking waves of 7-11 feet possible
- Seawall at 27th Ave Beachfront Park now exposed
- $25.8M renourishment project placed 550,000 cubic yards of sand five months ago
