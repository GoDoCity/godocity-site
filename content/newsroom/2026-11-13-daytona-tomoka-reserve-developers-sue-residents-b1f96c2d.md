## Why it matters

The former Tomoka Oaks Golf Course has been a flashpoint for nearly five years, and the lawsuit escalates a high-stakes neighborhood development battle. Residents worry about traffic on an already crash-prone Nova Road, flooding and home values if 254 homes get built. The legal escalation against private citizens is unusual and likely to spark strong community reaction.

## What happened

The developers of the proposed Tomoka Reserve subdivision filed a lawsuit against 12 Ormond Beach residents opposing the project, which would bring up to 254 homes to the 147-acre former Tomoka Oaks Golf Course. The suit accuses residents of using HOA resources to fight the development and claims their actions cost the developers money. Residents cite traffic, flooding and property-value concerns, and are separately appealing the city's approval in court.

## Key facts

- Proposed Tomoka Reserve would bring up to 254 homes to 147-acre former Tomoka Oaks Golf Course
- Developers sued 12 residents opposing the project
- Suit alleges residents misused HOA resources and cost developers money
- Residents cite Nova Road traffic (a high-crash area), flooding and property values
- Developers say more than half the site would be preserved green space with nearly 3 miles of buffering
- A separate resident appeal of the city's approval is still in the courts
