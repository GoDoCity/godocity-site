## Why it matters

This is a major, unusual crime story involving an international investigation spanning Japan and the Philippines, not a routine blotter item. The arrest of a local man in a long-unsolved disappearance will draw significant community interest in Volusia County.

## What happened

The Volusia County Sheriff's Office arrested Epifanio IX Ramos Gaminde of DeBary and charged him with first-degree murder in the 2024 death of his wife, Maria Gaminde. Her family reported her missing after no contact since January 2024; he claimed she moved overseas voluntarily. Investigators, suspicious of inauthentic texts, coordinated with federal agencies and officials in Japan and the Philippines. Deputies and the State Attorney's Office traveled to the Philippines to gather evidence of a deliberate effort to make Maria appear alive. Her body has not been recovered. Gaminde is held with no bond on an immigration detainer.
