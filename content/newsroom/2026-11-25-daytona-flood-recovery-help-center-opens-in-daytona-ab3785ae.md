## Why it matters

Historic flooding has left Volusia residents cleaning out homes, and a multi-agency resource center opening in Daytona Beach gives affected families direct access to housing help, supplies and support. This is real, actionable public-impact news with a clear local hook. It also ties in a free community Thanksgiving dinner.

## What happened

A multi-agency resource center is opening at the Dickerson Community Center in Daytona Beach on Friday, Nov. 27 at 1 p.m., running daily 7 a.m.–7 p.m. through Dec. 11. It will offer housing help, social services, supplies and emotional/spiritual support. Catholic Charities of Central Florida is also providing short-term financial aid, case management, muck-out kits and hygiene kits countywide. The city of Daytona Beach is hosting a free Thanksgiving community dinner Thursday at 1 p.m.

## Key facts

- Resource center at Dickerson Community Center, 308 S. Dr. MLK Jr. Blvd., opens Fri Nov 27 at 1 p.m.
- Open daily 7 a.m.–7 p.m. through Dec. 11
- Catholic Charities of Central Florida offers aid countywide: flooding@cflcc.org, 407-658-1818 ext. 9100
- Help includes housing, supplies, muck-out and hygiene kits, financial assistance
- Free Daytona Beach community Thanksgiving dinner Thursday at 1 p.m.
- Residents can also call 211 for disaster resources
