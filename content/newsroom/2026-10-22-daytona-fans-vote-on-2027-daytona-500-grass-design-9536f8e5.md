## Why it matters

The Daytona 500 is the area's marquee event, and this gives locals a fun, interactive way to shape a piece of race-day history. It's a shareable, feel-good item that invites reader participation before the February race.

## What happened

Daytona International Speedway opened fan voting for the infield grass design to be featured at the 69th Daytona 500 on Sunday, February 21, 2027. Three options are up: 'Star-Spangled Skies' (Thunderbirds flyover theme), 'Racing in Motion' (dual checkered flags and starry center), and 'World Center of Racing.' Voting runs October 20 through October 28 on the Speedway's official website.

## Key facts

- Voting runs Oct 20 through Oct 28, 2026
- Three designs: Star-Spangled Skies, Racing in Motion, World Center of Racing
- 2027 Daytona 500 is Sunday, Feb 21, 2027 — the 69th running
- Voting is on Daytona International Speedway's official website
- Tickets are on sale with a sellout considered probable
