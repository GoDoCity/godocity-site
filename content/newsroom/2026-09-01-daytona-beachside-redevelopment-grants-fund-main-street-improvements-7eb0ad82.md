## Why it matters

Main Street redevelopment is a long-running local interest area, and these grants show visible change coming to the beachside district — a new mural, storefront upgrades and home improvements. Readers who frequent the Main Street corridor and businesses like Neptune's Bar & Grill will notice the payoff. It also signals ongoing public investment in an area many residents want revitalized.

## What happened

The Beachside Redevelopment Board approved several grants: $50,000 in commercial improvement funding for 415 Main St. (Neptune's Bar & Grill) toward a $77,300 project including new AC units, a $10,000 grant for a 13x66-foot mural at 500 Main St. by artist Beth O'Connor, and $3,400 toward exterior painting of a home at 17 S. Oleander Ave. The city has awarded 15 grants totaling $124,836 in the Main Street area this fiscal year.

## Key facts

- $50,000 commercial grant to 415 Main St. (Neptune's Bar & Grill) for AC units within a $77,300 project
- $10,000 grant funded a 13-by-66-foot mural at 500 Main St. by artist Beth O'Connor
- $3,400 residential facade grant for a home at 17 S. Oleander Ave.
- 15 grants totaling $124,836 awarded in Main Street area this fiscal year
- Programs run through the city's Community Redevelopment Agency
