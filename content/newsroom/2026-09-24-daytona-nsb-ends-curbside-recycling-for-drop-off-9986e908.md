## Why it matters

This directly affects every New Smyrna Beach household that currently sets out a recycling bin. Residents need to know curbside pickup ends Oct. 1 and where the new 24/7 drop-off dumpsters are located. The return of glass recycling after a five-year gap is a genuine reader win.

## What happened

City Manager Kevin Cowper told the City Commission on Sept. 22 that curbside recycling pickup will end Oct. 1, replaced by three single-stream drop-off sites open 24/7 at 27th Avenue Beachfront Park (3701 S. Atlantic Ave.), the Sugar Mill trailhead (433 Sugar Mill Dr.), and NSB Public Works (124 Industrial Park Ave.). Glass will return to the recyclables list after being removed in November 2021. Assistant City Manager Ron Neibert also announced his Oct. 2 retirement after 40-plus years of service.

## Key facts

- Curbside recycling pickup ends Oct. 1, 2026
- Three 24/7 single-stream drop-off sites open at Beachfront Park, Sugar Mill trailhead, and Public Works
- Glass returns to recyclables after removal on Nov. 9, 2021
- Assistant City Manager Ron Neibert retires Oct. 2 after 40+ years
