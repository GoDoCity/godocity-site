## Why it matters

A proposed amendment to Florida's property tax system could directly affect how much homeowners pay and how city services are funded. With the November election approaching, residents have real money and local services at stake, making this more than routine red tape.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach launched a 'Know Before' educational campaign to explain how property tax dollars fund services like public safety, infrastructure and parks, and how a proposed state amendment could impact the community. The city is directing residents to its social media and DaytonaBeach.gov ahead of the November election.

## Key facts

- City launched a 'Know Before' property tax education campaign
- Focuses on how tax dollars fund public safety, infrastructure, parks and recreation
- Tied to a proposed amendment to Florida's property tax system
- Timed ahead of the November 2026 election
- Residents directed to city social media and DaytonaBeach.gov
