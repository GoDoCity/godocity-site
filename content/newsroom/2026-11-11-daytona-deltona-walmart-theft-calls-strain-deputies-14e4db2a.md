## Why it matters

This is a structural public-safety and taxpayer-cost story, not a routine blotter item. It shows how repeat retail theft at a specific Deltona store ties up sheriff's deputies that residents fund, which could spark local conversation about policing resources and retail crime.

## What happened

News 6 reviewed Volusia County Sheriff's Office dispatch records and found deputies were called to the Walmart on Howland Boulevard in Deltona roughly 465 times between August 2025 and August 2026, many for shoplifting and minor theft. A UF Loss Prevention Research Council criminologist said repeat offenders drive much of the problem, and retailers spend millions on security while still losing billions. Walmart said it is committed to working with law enforcement.

## Key facts

- About 465 deputy calls to the Howland Blvd Walmart in Deltona, Aug 2025-Aug 2026
- A comparable Orlando Walmart logged nearly 480 dispatches
- Many calls involved shoplifting and minor theft
- UF Loss Prevention Research Council works with 191 retailers including Walmart
- Walmart loses an estimated $3 billion annually to theft against $713B revenue
