## Why it matters

Volusia County says it created the first searchable domestic-violence offender registry in Florida, a free public tool residents can use to check whether someone has a DV conviction. It's a countywide first with real practical use for dating, hiring in-home caregivers, and protecting elderly residents, and could expand statewide.

## What happened

Volusia County launched an online, searchable database of people convicted of domestic or dating violence in county courts over the past 10 years. It was proposed by County Councilman Matt Reinhart and is connected to the court system, updating live with each new conviction. The registry shows names, booking photos and related court cases but no victim information, and includes only convictions, not arrests.

## Key facts

- Described as Florida's first online DV offender database
- Includes convictions in Volusia courts over past 10 years
- Shows names, booking photos, court cases; no victim info
- Idea of County Councilman Matt Reinhart
- Convictions only, not arrests; one conviction qualifies
- County working with state leaders to potentially expand statewide within a year
