## Why it matters

The plan shapes where bike lanes, sidewalks and trails get built over the next decade, affecting how residents walk, bike and get around Ormond Beach. A public meeting gives readers a direct chance to influence local infrastructure.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach is updating its long-range multimodal plan covering bicycle, pedestrian and trail improvements for 2026-2036. A public input meeting is scheduled for December 9, 2026 at 5:30pm.

## Key facts

- Ormond Beach is updating its 2026-2036 Multimodal Plan
- Covers bike, pedestrian, trail and other mobility improvements
- Public meeting set for December 9, 2026 at 5:30pm
- City is soliciting resident input
