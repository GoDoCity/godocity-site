## Why it matters

Flooding and heavy rain are expected this week, and residents in Port Orange and Edgewater need to know where to get sandbags to protect their homes. This is practical, time-sensitive public-safety information with real money and property impact.

## What happened

Volusia County opened sandbag stations ahead of expected flooding and heavy rain. Edgewater opened two sites at Rotary Park and the Public Works Operation Facility, allowing up to 20 sandbags per household. Port Orange opened a station at 4057 Bruner Road, open Monday until dark, with staff pre-filling and loading up to 10 bags per vehicle with ID.

## Key facts

- Edgewater: Rotary Park (902 W. Park Ave.) and Public Works (1898 Air Park Road), up to 20 bags
- Port Orange: 4057 Bruner Road, open Monday until dark, up to 10 bags per vehicle, ID required
- Port Orange staff pre-fill and load bags
- Driven by flooding and heavy rain concerns this week
- Brevard County sites also opening (less relevant to Daytona area)
