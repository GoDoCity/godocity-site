## Why it matters

A precautionary boil water notice is direct public-safety info for affected residents, who need to know their water may be unsafe to drink without boiling. Even a small, short outage qualifies under the critical-public-impact exception because it affects what people put in their bodies.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach interrupted water service at 432 Airport Road from 8:30am to 11:00am on Saturday, November 7, 2026, to install a fire hydrant after operational issues. Affected customers were placed on a precautionary boil water notice until further notice.

## Key facts

- Boil water notice tied to fire hydrant installation at 432 Airport Road
- Water interrupted 8:30am-11:00am Saturday, November 7, 2026
- Precautionary notice remains until city rescinds it
