## Why it matters

A feel-good local triumph: eight DeLand High dancers earned spots in the nationally televised Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade on its centennial. It's exactly the kind of community-pride story readers love to share, and it's timed to Thanksgiving week.

## What happened

Eight girls from DeLand High School's Avant Garde dance team were invited to perform in the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City on Nov. 26. Several also dance at Arts Alive Dance studio in Orange City. They auditioned via a recorded piece in January, were selected by Spirit of America Productions, and will spend days rehearsing and sightseeing in NYC.

## Key facts

- Eight DeLand High Avant Garde dancers invited to the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade
- Dancers: Brianna North, Alexa & Addison Schairer, Emma Benge, Audrey Smith, Avery Tidmarsh, Sophia LeFils, Melanie Brown
- Several also dance at Arts Alive Dance studio in Orange City
- Parade airs Nov. 26; last year drew record 34.3M viewers
- Spirit of America Productions recruited the talent
