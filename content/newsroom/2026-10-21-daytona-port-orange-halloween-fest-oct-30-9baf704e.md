## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly Halloween event is exactly the kind of local fun GoDoDaytona readers plan their weekends around. With costume contests, trunk-or-treat, food trucks and a kids zone, it gives Port Orange families a safe and festive outing right before Halloween.

## What happened

The City of Port Orange announced its annual Halloween Fest, set for Oct. 30 from 5 to 8 p.m. at City Center. The event features a costume contest, trunk-or-treat, kids zone, games, activities, local vendors and food trucks.

## Key facts

- Annual Halloween Fest on Oct. 30, 5-8 p.m.
- Location: Port Orange City Center
- Features costume contest, trunk-or-treat, kids zone, games and activities
- Local vendors and food trucks on site
- Family-friendly and open to the community
