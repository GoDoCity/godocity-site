## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly waterfront festival in its 17th year is exactly the kind of weekend outing GoDoDaytona readers want to plan. It draws families, foodies and boating fans to Ormond's riverfront parks, and gives locals a shareable event to put on the calendar.

## What happened

Ormond Mainstreet announced the 17th Annual Ormond Beach Riverfest on Nov. 21-22, 2026, at Rockefeller Gardens and Fortunato Park. The weekend festival features live music, local food, a kids' zone, a boat show, a petting zoo, an artisan market and waterfront views.

## Key facts

- 17th annual event hosted by Ormond Mainstreet
- Dates: Sat Nov 21 (10am-6pm) and Sun Nov 22 (10am-4pm)
- Location: Rockefeller Gardens & Fortunato Park, 26 Riverside Drive
- Features live music, food, kids' zone, boat show, petting zoo, artisan market
- Vendor and volunteer opportunities available
