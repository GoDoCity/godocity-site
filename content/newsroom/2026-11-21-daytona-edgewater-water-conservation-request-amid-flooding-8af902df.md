## Why it matters

A citywide request tied to flooding and strained wastewater systems directly affects how Edgewater residents use water at home. It signals current weather impacts people may be experiencing right now, making it practical public-impact news despite not being 'fun.'

## What happened

Edgewater's Public Works & Utilities Department asked all residents and businesses to voluntarily conserve water until further notice because significant rainfall and flooding have raised flows at the Wastewater Treatment Plant. Residents were urged to postpone laundry and dishwasher use and limit nonessential water usage. The city said it continues to monitor conditions and will provide updates.
