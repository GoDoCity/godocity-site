## Why it matters

A festive city-hosted holiday event for residents 55 and older gives an active local demographic something to do and mark on the calendar. It's a small, upbeat community item useful to seniors and their families in Daytona Beach.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced a Winter Wonderland holiday event for Senior Oasis members ages 55+, set for 6-9 p.m. Friday, Dec. 4 at the Cherry Cultural & Educational Center. Hosted by Zone 6 Commissioner Paula R. Reed, it will feature a DJ, food, photo booth, cider and cocoa. Admission is $5, reservations required by Nov. 13, with Family Health Source as presenting sponsor.

## Key facts

- Presenting sponsor: Family Health Source
