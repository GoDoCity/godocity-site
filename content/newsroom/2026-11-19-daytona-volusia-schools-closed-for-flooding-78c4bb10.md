## Why it matters

School closures directly affect thousands of local families who must arrange childcare and adjust plans. Flooding guidance from Emergency Management signals real public-safety conditions on area roads heading into the Thanksgiving break.

## What happened

Volusia County Schools announced that all schools and district offices would be closed Thursday, Nov. 19, and Friday, Nov. 20, due to heavy rain and flooding. The closures include Extended Day programs, athletics, extracurriculars and all school events, which will be rescheduled after Thanksgiving. Students return Monday, Nov. 30.

## Key facts

- All Volusia schools and district offices closed Nov. 19-20
- Closures include athletics, extracurriculars and Extended Day programs
- Decision made with Volusia County Emergency Management guidance
- Students scheduled to return Monday, Nov. 30 after Thanksgiving break
- Officials urged avoiding flooded roadways
