## Why it matters

A new city app gives Ormond Beach residents a direct, practical tool to report potholes, damaged signs and other non-emergency issues. It's a useful, actionable civic tool that readers can download and use right away.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach launched OB Connect, an official app (powered by SeeClickFix) that lets residents report non-emergency concerns like potholes and damaged signs. It's available on the Apple App Store, Google Play, and via a web browser version.

## Key facts

- App is called OB Connect, launched late September 2026
- Powered by SeeClickFix platform
- Available on Apple App Store, Google Play, and web browser
- Used to report non-emergency issues: potholes, damaged signs, etc.
