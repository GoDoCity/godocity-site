## Why it matters

Flooding that lingers days after a storm directly affects where Port Orange residents can drive and live, and raises questions about drainage capacity. Families in Oak Hill are gutting homes, a real public-impact story for Volusia residents.

## What happened

Days after heavy rain flooded parts of Volusia County, roads in a Port Orange neighborhood remained shin-deep in water. Two connected retention ponds are over capacity and the city installed a pump, but saturated ground makes it hard to move water. In Oak Hill, several homes suffered extensive damage and Southeast Volusia Habitat for Humanity plans to help rebuild. Edgewater's mayor said the city pre-pumped retention areas before the storm and water had receded by Saturday.
