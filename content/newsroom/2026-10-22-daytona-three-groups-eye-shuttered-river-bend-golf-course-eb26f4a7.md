## Why it matters

River Bend has been a decaying eyesore for six years, frustrating neighbors who want action. Now three competing proposals — two to reopen the golf course, one to expand the airport business park with hangars — put the 172-acre property's future in play. The outcome will reshape a large chunk of Ormond Beach land and affect nearby residents directly.

## What happened

River Bend Golf Course in Ormond Beach closed in 2020 after its operator filed for bankruptcy. Three groups have now submitted letters of intent to buy the 172-acre property; two want to reopen/revamp the course, one wants to expand the airport business park and add hangars. Because the land is part of Ormond Beach Municipal Airport, any use change needs FAA approval, and the city must update its airport master plan. The property is not currently for sale and faces wetland and aeronautical constraints.
