## Why it matters

The Christmas parade is a beloved annual tradition, and its cancellation plus a freeze on nearly all city events will disappoint many Deltona families during the holidays. It also signals deep budget pain ahead tied to Amendment 3, which could mean cuts to other services residents rely on.

## What happened

Deltona's city manager announced Sunday the cancellation of the Deltona Christmas Parade, citing budget pressure from Amendment 3. The city says it must cut $12 million next year and $5 million the following year, eventually more than $23 million total. All city events except two upcoming 5K runs are being cut, with funds redirected to essential services. The city manager warned more difficult budget decisions are coming.

- Deltona Christmas Parade canceled per city manager Doc Dougherty
- Press release issued Sunday citing Amendment 3
