## Why it matters

The fate of the former Tomoka Oaks golf course is one of Ormond Beach's most contentious development fights, and a developer suing a dozen residents escalates a yearslong battle that neighbors have fought on traffic and hurricane-evacuation grounds. This affects whether 250+ homes get built and sets a notable precedent for residents who organize against development.

## What happened

Triumph Oaks of Ormond Beach I LLC, developer of the former Tomoka Oaks Golf and Country Club, filed suit against 12 people connected to the Tomoka Oaks HOA, alleging breach of fiduciary duty and improper use of HOA resources to oppose its redevelopment. The company seeks more than $50,000 in damages. The 148-acre property, bought in 2021, is slated for more than 250 homes; commissioners approved the plan in March 2026 and residents appealed.

## Key facts

- Triumph Oaks bought the ~148-acre former golf course in 2021
- Plan calls for 250+ homes; approved by commissioners March 2026
- Lawsuit targets 12 people tied to the HOA, seeks $50,000+
- Claims improper use of nonprofit HOA assets for litigation
- Residents cite traffic and single-road hurricane evacuation concerns
- Golf course closed in 2018
