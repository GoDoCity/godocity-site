## Why it matters

This changes how every New Smyrna Beach property owner pays for fire protection, moving the cost off property-value-based taxes to a separate assessment based on property type and size. It affects real money on residents' tax bills and will phase in over four years starting Oct. 1, 2027.

## What happened

On Sept. 8, 2026, the NSB City Commission voted to fund fire protection through a dedicated fire assessment rather than general property taxes. The rate, based on property type and building size, phases in over four years beginning Oct. 1, 2027, and will appear as a separate line on tax bills. It funds firefighter wages, operations and Basic Life Support; ambulance/ALS stay separately funded. Low-income homestead residents at or below 60% AMI can apply for financial assistance.

## Key facts

- Commission voted Sept. 8, 2026 to adopt fire assessment
- Takes effect Oct. 1, 2027, phased over four years
- Based on property type and building size, not assessed value
- Shown as separate line item on tax bills
- Funds firefighter wages, operations and Basic Life Support
- Assistance available for homestead owners at/below 60% AMI
