## Why it matters

A local consumer-fraud saga has an unusual, attention-grabbing hook that readers will talk about: a Port Orange couple accused of defying a court ban on selling defective hot tubs, allegedly running covert sales from a warehouse before fleeing the state. More than 30 consumers were affected and nearly half a million in restitution is at stake.

## What happened

Florida's Attorney General filed an Oct. 13 motion asking a Volusia County judge to find Dylan and Chelsie Placker in contempt and reinstate a $471,096 judgment. The couple had signed a 2025 settlement banning them from any Florida hot tub business, but were allegedly caught operating from a Port Orange warehouse and selling via Facebook Marketplace under aliases. They are also accused of missing restitution payments and hiding ownership of a boat, trailer and truck before moving to Oklahoma.
