## Why it matters

A large coordinated operation involving the Sheriff's Office, Daytona Beach police and the FBI is a bigger public-safety story than a routine blotter item, and Sheriff Chitwood's blunt messaging tends to generate community conversation. ## What happened

In October 2026, the Volusia County Sheriff's Office partnered with Daytona Beach police and the FBI on a month-long series of operations that resulted in more than 120 arrests. The effort targeted fugitives, suspected drug dealers, repeat offenders and people accused of sex crimes, prompted by a seasonal rise in fall violent crime.
