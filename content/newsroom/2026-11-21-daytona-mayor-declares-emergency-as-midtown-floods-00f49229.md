## What happened

The mayor of Daytona Beach declared a local state of emergency after severe storms flooded Midtown Daytona Beach, turning roads into streams and lawns into ponds. At a Nova Road apartment building, floodwaters submerged the parking area and entered some first-floor units, while others used sandbags as barriers. City response teams were working to manage the weather and maintain operations.
