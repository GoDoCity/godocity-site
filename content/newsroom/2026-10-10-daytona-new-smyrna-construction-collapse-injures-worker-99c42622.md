## Why it matters

A construction worker was seriously injured when a home under construction collapsed in a New Smyrna Beach neighborhood, raising questions about jobsite safety and the cause. ## What happened

Marisela Gonzalez, a mother of two, was working on a home under construction in New Smyrna Beach about two weeks ago when the structure collapsed, trapping her under debris. Bystanders and firefighters freed her; she underwent surgery for a spinal injury, broken femur and body trauma, and was told she had a 10% chance of walking again. The cause remains under investigation and a verified GoFundMe has been set up.
