## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly festival at a landmark venue gives readers a concrete weekend plan and supports a local charity. Beer tastings, live music and food trucks are exactly the kind of upbeat, shareable event GoDoDaytona readers act on. It's a recurring, well-established community draw.

## What happened

The Ormond Beach Rotary Club announced its 7th Annual Beer Festival for Saturday, October 10, 2026, from 1 to 4 p.m. at The Casements. Admission is free, with an optional tasting ticket for unlimited samples from more than 35 breweries and 100-plus beer styles. The event features live music, food trucks and a Kids Zone, with proceeds benefiting Rotary charities.
