## What happened

President Donald Trump is scheduled to hold a midterm campaign rally in Daytona Beach on Friday, Oct. 30, 2026. Doors open at 3:30 p.m., the event begins at 5:30 p.m., and Trump is expected to speak at 7 p.m. The venue has not been announced, and guests must register in advance via the RNC. It would be his second Florida midterm rally in a week, following one in Fort Myers.
