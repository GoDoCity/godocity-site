## Why it matters

The annual holiday parade is a beloved family tradition and a big downtown draw. Readers will want to mark their calendars, and community groups have a free chance to enter a float or performance.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced its annual Christmas Parade will step off at 6 p.m. Saturday, Dec. 12, starting at Dr. Mary McLeod Bethune Boulevard and traveling south on Beach Street to Jackie Robinson Parkway. It will feature local bands, floats and community performances. The city is accepting free parade entries and has posted route, staging, road-closure and free parking maps.

## Key facts

- Parade at 6 p.m. Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026
- Route: Dr. Mary McLeod Bethune Blvd south on Beach Street to Jackie Robinson Parkway
- No application or entry fee to participate
- Features local bands, floats and community organizations
- Entries handled by Samantha Gottlieb, Parks & Recreation, (386) 671-5551
- Free public parking map and road-closure map provided
