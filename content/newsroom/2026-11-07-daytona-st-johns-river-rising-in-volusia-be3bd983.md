## Why it matters

Rising river levels from Brevard runoff threaten low-lying neighborhoods like Deltona's Stone Island, and residents remember flood damage from Ian and Milton. A free sandbag site and a crest expected early next week make this practical, actionable public-impact news.

## What happened

Flood runoff from Brevard County is flowing north into the St. Johns River, raising water levels in Volusia County. The river is expected to crest Monday or Tuesday. Volusia County opened a free self-service sandbag site in Osteen as a precaution and says it is monitoring but not yet sounding alarms.

## Key facts

- River expected to crest Monday or Tuesday
- Free sandbag site at Fire Rescue Station 36, 180 N. State Road 415, Osteen, open daily 8 a.m.-5 p.m.
- Levels highest since Hurricane Milton, per residents
- County added generators for pumps/lift stations after Ian sewage backups
- Officials monitoring but not yet sounding alarm
