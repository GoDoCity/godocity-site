## Why it matters

I-95 is a main artery for Port Orange, New Smyrna and southern Volusia commuters, and full overnight closures with detours affect anyone traveling late at night. This is real, timely public-impact information readers can act on.

## What happened

FDOT announced overnight closures of I-95 between Taylor Road and State Road 44 from 9 p.m. to 6:30 a.m. on select nights to set beams for a new bridge. Northbound closes Sept. 23, 24 and 27; southbound closes Sept. 28, 29 and 30. Detour signs and message boards will guide drivers, and dates may shift with weather.

## Key facts

- Closures between Taylor Road and SR 44, 9 p.m.–6:30 a.m.
- Northbound closed Sept. 23, 24, 27
- Southbound closed Sept. 28, 29, 30
- Work involves setting beams for a new bridge
- Detour signs and message boards to be in place
- Dates/times may change with weather
