## Why it matters

A fatal crash involving multiple children from Ormond Beach is a genuine community tragedy that local residents will discuss, and S.R. 40 was blocked, affecting travel. While not an uplifting story, the loss of a local child and multiple injured children carries real community weight.

## What happened

On Saturday afternoon around 2:13 p.m., a Tesla Model Y traveling west on S.R. 40 west of Still Road veered right, overcorrected, and collided head-on with an eastbound Chevy Silverado. A 12-year-old Ormond Beach boy riding in the Tesla died; four others, including three more children, were seriously injured. The road was blocked and FHP is investigating.
