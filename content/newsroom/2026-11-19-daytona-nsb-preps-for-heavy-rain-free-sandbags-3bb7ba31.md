## Why it matters

Several days of potentially heavy rain (3-6 inches or more) are forecast, and the city is offering free sandbags and sand piles to help residents protect their homes. This is practical, time-sensitive public-impact info for NSB residents. It also folds in multiple upcoming community updates worth flagging.

## What happened

City Manager Kevin Cowper told the City Commission that NSB is preparing for days of potentially heavy rainfall, with Public Works and public safety staging equipment and personnel. Residents can get up to 15 free sandbags with photo ID at 124 Industrial Park Ave., 8 a.m.-5 p.m. through Friday; seniors and residents with disabilities can get up to 10 pre-filled bags. Sand piles are available 24/7 at the Sports Complex football stadium lot at 2335 Sunset Dr. He also recapped a North Causeway/3rd Avenue visioning workshop, noted Thanksgiving trash pickup moves to Nov. 28, and confirmed the Dec. 5 Christmas Parade.

## Key facts

- Forecast of 3-6 inches of rain, possibly more intense
- Up to 15 free sandbags per resident with photo ID at 124 Industrial Park Ave., 8 a.m.-5 p.m. through Friday

- Thanksgiving trash pickup moves from Nov. 26 to Sat., Nov. 28
