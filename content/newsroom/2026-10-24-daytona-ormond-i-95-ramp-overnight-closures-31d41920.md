## Why it matters

The ramp from U.S. 1 to southbound I-95 is a key Ormond Beach connection, and overnight closures affect commuters, truckers and late-night travelers for nearly a week. Readers can plan around the detour, which adds roughly 13 minutes.

## What happened

FDOT will close the ramp from U.S. 1 in Ormond Beach to southbound I-95 overnight Nov. 1-5 for bridge repairs, from as early as 9 p.m. to about 6:30 a.m., with the final closure ending Friday, Nov. 6. A signed detour routes drivers via Nova Road and West Granada Boulevard.

## Key facts

- Ramp from U.S. 1 to southbound I-95 in Ormond Beach closes overnight Nov. 1-5
- Closures start as early as 9 p.m., reopen by about 6:30 a.m.
- Final closure ends Friday, Nov. 6
- Detour via Nova Road and West Granada Boulevard, about 4 miles
- News 6 drove the detour in 12 min 58 sec
- Closures for FDOT bridge repairs; schedule may change for weather
