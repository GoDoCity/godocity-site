## Why it matters

Razzle's was a Daytona Beach institution and nightlife landmark near Seabreeze Boulevard for over four decades, tied to the area's spring break and party culture. Its closing marks the end of an era that many residents and visitors have personal memories of, and signals broader pressures on beachside nightlife. A closure this significant will generate strong community conversation.

## What happened

Razzle's Nightclub, open 41 years near the corner of Seabreeze Boulevard and SR-A1A, is closing. Mayor Derrick Henry acknowledged the closure in a Facebook post, calling it a genuine loss, and pushed back on claims that city leadership or beachside conditions caused it, citing broader economic pressures on nightlife. The club had also been the site of violent incidents, including a July 2023 shooting that injured four.
