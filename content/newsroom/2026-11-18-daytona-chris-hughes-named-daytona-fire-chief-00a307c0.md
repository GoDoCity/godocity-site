## Why it matters

The fire chief leads a department that touches public safety for every resident and visitor. Hughes is a homegrown pick who rose through the ranks over 25 years, a local-success-story angle readers appreciate. It also marks a leadership transition after Dru Driscoll's retirement.

## What happened

Daytona Beach officially named longtime firefighter Chris Hughes, 47, as its new fire chief after he served as interim chief since July. He becomes the department's 20th chief, having joined in 2001 and worked up through firefighter, driver/engineer, lieutenant, battalion chief and deputy chief. He succeeds former Deputy City Manager/Fire Chief Dru Driscoll, who retired. A swearing-in and badge-pinning date will be announced later.
