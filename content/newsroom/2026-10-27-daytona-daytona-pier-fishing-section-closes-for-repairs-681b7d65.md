## Why it matters

The Daytona Beach Pier is an iconic oceanfront landmark, and anglers and visitors who use it regularly will want to know it's off-limits for the next couple of months. It's a practical heads-up that affects a popular free local activity.

## What happened

The fishing portion of the Daytona Beach Pier closed to pedestrians and fishing starting Monday, Oct. 26, for scheduled repair and maintenance work. The city says the closure is for safety and the pier is expected to reopen by the end of the year.

## Key facts

- Fishing section of Daytona Beach Pier closed starting Mon., Oct. 26, 2026
- Closure covers pedestrians and fishing
- Scheduled repair and maintenance work cited for safety
- Anticipated reopening by end of year
