## Why it matters

Working parents in Daytona Beach need affordable childcare when school is out, and these city camps run $20/day. It's practical, useful information families will share and act on ahead of the school year's break days.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach opened registration for its K-5 school break camps at Schnebly Recreation Center. Teacher Duty Day camps run on specific dates for $20/day or $90 for all six, and a Thanksgiving Break Camp (Nov. 23-25) costs $30. Winter and Spring Break camps are planned for later. Spots are first-come, first-served.

## Key facts

- K-5 camps at Schnebly Recreation Center, 1101 N. Atlantic Ave., 7:30 a.m.-5:30 p.m.
- Teacher Duty Days: Oct. 12, Nov. 11, 2026; Jan. 4, Feb. 15, March 22, 2027
- Cost $20/child/day or $90 for all six days
- Thanksgiving Break Camp Nov. 23-25, $30 for all three days
- Winter Break Camp at Scarlett-Golden Center and Schnebly; Spring Break at Schnebly
- First-come, first-served; parents provide meals
