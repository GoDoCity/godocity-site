## Why it matters

Scammers are impersonating Daytona Beach staff and using real permit data to trick property owners and developers into wiring bogus 'review and approval' fees. Anyone with a planning or zoning case could be a target, so this is a genuine money-protection alert for residents and local businesses.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach issued a fraud alert about scam emails falsely appearing to come from city staff, requesting wire-transfer payment for planning and zoning review/approval fees. The city says the emails are fake and may use real permit info, addresses, case numbers and official names to look legitimate. The FBI issued a nationwide warning about this scam type earlier in 2026.

## Key facts

- Scam emails impersonate Daytona Beach city staff
- Fraudsters request wire-transfer payment for planning/zoning review and approval fees
- Emails may include real permit info, property addresses, case numbers and official names
- City confirms the emails are not from the city
