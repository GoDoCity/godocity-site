## Why it matters

Heavy rains have flooded Daytona Beach neighborhoods, prompting airboat rescues and evacuations — a critical public-safety situation directly affecting residents. The story also carries a genuinely uplifting local-hero angle with two Embry-Riddle students helping a mother and three young children to safety.

## What happened

Days of heavy rain left Volusia neighborhoods underwater, forcing emergency rescues and evacuations. In Daytona Beach, two Embry-Riddle sophomores used an air mattress to help a mother and her three children escape knee- to neck-deep water near Orange Avenue, Morley Street and South Adams Street. Volusia rescue crews and FWC used airboats to reach stranded residents, and the sheriff visited affected neighborhoods.

## Key facts

- Flooding hit Volusia, Brevard and Seminole counties after days of heavy rain
- Two Embry-Riddle sophomores rescued a Daytona mother and three children (ages 5, 4, 3) using a Walmart air mattress
- Floodwaters near Orange Avenue, Morley Street and South Adams Street remained above knee level
- Volusia rescue crews and FWC used airboats to reach stranded residents
- Volusia County sheriff visited affected neighborhoods; some homes flooded for a fourth time
