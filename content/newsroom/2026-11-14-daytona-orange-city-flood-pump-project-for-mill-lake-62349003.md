## Why it matters

Flooding on U.S. 17-92 and around Mill Lake has hit homes, churches and businesses in Orange City, and this multimillion-dollar pump project aims to fix a recurring problem. Residents care about both the flood relief and the sizable price tag tied to grant funding.

## What happened

Orange City is planning a new pump station on the opposite side of Mill Lake with more than 30 times the capacity of the current pump, to move floodwater faster before and after storms. Design and permitting are complete, and the city is awaiting grant decisions before construction, anticipated to begin in early 2027. Three options range from $19.37M to $26.58M depending on capacity.

## Key facts

- New pump station planned on opposite side of Mill Lake
- More than 30x capacity of current pump
- Design and permitting complete; awaiting grant decisions
- Three options: $26.58M (50cfs), $22.34M (35cfs), $19.37M (25cfs)
- Construction anticipated early 2027
- Would connect other city pumps, sending water to a borrow pit
