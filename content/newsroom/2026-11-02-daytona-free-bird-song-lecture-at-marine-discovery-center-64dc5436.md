## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly nature talk is the kind of low-cost local outing GoDoDaytona readers like to discover. The Marine Discovery Center is a known New Smyrna Beach institution, and nature/birding topics have a loyal local following.

## What happened

The Marine Discovery Center's monthly Discovery Lecture Series will host 'Music Lessons: The Science of How Birds Sing' on Thursday, Nov. 5 at 6 p.m. in its Learning Lab. Local birder Joan Tague of Halifax River Audubon presents. The event is free but requires registration.
