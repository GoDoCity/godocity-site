## Why it matters

A fatal hit-and-run on a busy Daytona Beach thoroughfare is a genuine public-safety matter, and police are actively asking residents for help finding the driver. Locals who travel LPGA Boulevard or know of a damaged vehicle could directly aid the investigation.

## What happened

A 43-year-old woman was struck and killed while crossing LPGA Boulevard around 4:30 a.m. Saturday. The driver stopped, turned around, then fled, last seen heading north on Derbyshire Road. Surveillance video is too grainy to identify the vehicle, so police are analyzing debris and checking auto body shops while seeking tips.

## Key facts

- Victim was a 43-year-old woman crossing LPGA Boulevard
- Crash occurred around 4:30 a.m. Saturday
- Driver stopped, turned around, then fled north on Derbyshire Road
- Police seek a four-door sedan or small crossover with front-end/windshield damage
- Tips to Detective Patterson, (386) 671-5366
