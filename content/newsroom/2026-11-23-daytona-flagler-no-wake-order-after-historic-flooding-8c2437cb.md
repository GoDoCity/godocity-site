## Why it matters

Flagler borders Volusia and many Daytona-area residents travel, boat and have ties there; the flooding and emergency order signal regional severe-weather impacts that could affect nearby roads and waterways. It's a legitimate public-safety item under the critical-impact exception.

## What happened

Flagler County enacted Emergency Administrative Order No. 2026-01 on Saturday after historic rainfall overwhelmed drainage infrastructure. It imposes an Idle Speed/No Wake restriction on all county waters and a 5 MPH limit for vehicles on flooded roadways, effective until the local state of emergency ends or the order is rescinded.

## Key facts

- Emergency Administrative Order No. 2026-01 enacted Saturday
- Idle Speed/No Wake on all navigable Flagler waters
- 5 MPH limit on roads with standing or flowing water
- Order remains until rescinded or state of emergency expires
- Follows historic rainfall that overwhelmed retention infrastructure
