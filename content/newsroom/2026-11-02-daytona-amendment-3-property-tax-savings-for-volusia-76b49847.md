## Why it matters

Amendment 3 goes before Florida voters tomorrow and could eliminate most non-school property taxes for permanent-resident homeowners — real money in local pockets. The article includes specific annual savings estimates for every Volusia community, from $1,151/year in Daytona Beach to $1,331 in South Daytona. It also carries a warning that local governments could lose funding for police and libraries.

## What happened

A proposed constitutional amendment on the Nov. 3 ballot would create a new homestead exemption for non-school property taxes, exempting the first $150,000 of assessed value in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028 onward for permanent Florida residents. Florida TaxWatch released an online tool estimating savings by city. Volusia estimates include Daytona Beach ($1,151/$2,319), Port Orange ($1,057/$2,130), Ormond Beach ($993/$2,000) and others. The amendment needs 60% voter approval.

## Key facts

- Amendment 3 on Nov. 3 ballot; needs 60% to pass
- Exempts first $150,000 of assessed value from non-school taxes in 2027, $250,000 in 2028+
- Only for permanent FL residents before Jan. 1, 2027; newcomers phase in over 5 years
- School property taxes still apply
- Daytona Beach est. savings: $1,151 (2027) / $2,319 (2028); Port Orange $1,057/$2,130; Ormond Beach $993/$2,000
- Critics warn of lost funding for police and libraries; Florida Realtors spent $18M to pass it
