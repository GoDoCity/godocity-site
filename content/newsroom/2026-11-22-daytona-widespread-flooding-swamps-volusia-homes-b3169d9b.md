## Why it matters

Days of heavy rain have flooded homes and closed major roads across Volusia County, directly affecting where residents live and drive. This is critical public-impact news with real safety and property consequences, and impacted families will need community support.

## What happened

On Saturday, Nov. 21, Chopper 2 flew over flooded Volusia and Brevard communities after days of heavy rain caused widespread flooding, road closures and dangerous driving. Meteorologist Eric Burris described extensive flooding over South Daytona and reported many inundated homes. Parts of Brevard saw 28 inches of rain; some major roads in both counties were closed. Sunday was forecast to be mostly sunny.
