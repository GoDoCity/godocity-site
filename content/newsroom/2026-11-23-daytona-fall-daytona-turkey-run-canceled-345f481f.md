## Why it matters

The Fall Turkey Run is a major Thanksgiving-weekend draw at Daytona International Speedway, pulling in car enthusiasts, vendors and visitors. Its cancellation affects holiday plans, ticket holders and local businesses that count on the crowds.

## What happened

Organizers canceled the 2026 Fall Daytona Turkey Run, scheduled Nov. 25-29 at Daytona International Speedway, citing rising water levels that created unsafe conditions. Pre-purchased spectator tickets will be refunded to original payment cards by Friday, Nov. 27. Vendor spaces and payments will transfer automatically to the 2027 event, set for Nov. 24-28, 2027.

## Key facts

- Event was scheduled Nov. 25-29, 2026 at Daytona International Speedway
- Canceled due to unsafe rising water levels, decided with the speedway
- Spectator tickets refunded to original card by Friday, Nov. 27
- Vendor spaces/payments transfer to 2027 event
- Next Fall Turkey Run set for Nov. 24-28, 2027
