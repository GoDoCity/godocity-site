## Why it matters

The free 55+ social program is a useful, feel-good community resource for Daytona Beach seniors and their families. Residents looking for connection and activities now have open membership slots at three recreation centers.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced membership openings in its free Senior Oasis program for adults 55 and older at Schnebly, Sunnyland, and Dickerson centers, each with specific meeting days and times. Two other locations are currently full.

## Key facts

- Free program for adults 55+
- Open at Schnebly (3rd Wed, 11am-1pm), Sunnyland (Thursdays 10am-2pm), Dickerson (Wednesdays 10am-2pm)
- Cherry and Yvonne Scarlett-Golden centers are full
- Contact numbers provided for each center
