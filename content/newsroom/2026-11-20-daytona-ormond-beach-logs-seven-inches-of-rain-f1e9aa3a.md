## Why it matters

A multi-day rain event dumped over 7 inches on Ormond Beach, and the city is actively managing stormwater and asking residents to reduce wastewater use. This is practical public-impact information about flooding risk, sandbag availability and utility strain that affects daily life.

## What happened

From Monday through 11 a.m. Thursday, Ormond Beach Public Works recorded 7.04 inches of rain. The city pre-pumped ponds to create storage and reports no road closures or flooding so far. Self-service sandbags are available at the Nova Community Center, and the Utility Department is asking residents to limit wastewater usage to ease the load on the Water Reclamation Facility.

## Key facts

- 7.04 inches of rain recorded Monday through 11 a.m. Thursday
- No road closures or flooding reported as of the update
- Ponds pre-pumped Monday to create storage
- Self-service sandbags at Nova Community Center, 440 North Nova Road
- City asking residents to restrict wastewater usage
