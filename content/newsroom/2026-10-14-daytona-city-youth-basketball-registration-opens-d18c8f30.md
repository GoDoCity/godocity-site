## Why it matters

Parents with kids ages 6-17 have a new recreation option through the city's rec centers. It's an actionable, family-friendly item that readers can sign up for right away.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach opened registration for its youth basketball program for players ages 6 to 17. The season features skills clinics, practices and games at city recreation centers. Registration opened Oct. 5.

## Key facts

- Open to players ages 6 to 17
- Registration opened Oct. 5
- Includes skills clinics, practices and games
- Held at city recreation centers
