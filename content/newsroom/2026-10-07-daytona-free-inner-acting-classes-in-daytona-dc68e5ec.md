## Why it matters

A free, confidence-building program for kids, teens and adults is the kind of accessible community offering families appreciate knowing about. It's positive, actionable, and squarely local.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach is running a free Inner Acting program for children 8-12, teens and adults at two city recreation facilities through Dec. 18. Classes are held at Dickerson Community Center and the Yvonne Scarlett-Golden Cultural and Educational Center, with registration via email to Leroy Meadows.

## Key facts

- Free program offered by City of Daytona Beach
- Open to kids 8-12, teens and adults
- Runs through Dec. 18
- Held at Dickerson Community Center and Yvonne Scarlett-Golden Cultural and Educational Center
- Register by emailing Leroy Meadows at MeadowsLeroy@daytonabeach.gov
