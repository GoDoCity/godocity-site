## Why it matters

Thousands of Daytona-area residents are still recovering from mid-November flooding that dumped over 16 inches of rain, and this story combines a heartwarming community response with practical, actionable recovery resources. The newly opening Multi-Agency Resource Center gives flood victims countywide a place to get housing, financial and emergency help. It's both uplifting and genuinely useful public-impact news.

## What happened

On Thanksgiving Day, Mayor Derrick Henry hosted a free community dinner at the Cherry Cultural & Educational Center for residents affected by recent flooding, serving 300 donated meals. Standing floodwater still lingered in parts of Midtown. A Multi-Agency Resource Center (MARC) is opening at the John H. Dickerson Community Center to connect Volusia County flood victims with recovery services.

## Key facts

- Mayor Derrick Henry hosted a free Thanksgiving dinner; 300 meals provided at Cherry Cultural & Educational Center
- Meals donated by Wildcat Dining/SodexoMagic with sponsors including Gary Yeomans Ford, CW Roberts Contracting and Glenn and Connie Ritchey
- NWS reported about 16.11 inches of storm rainfall at Daytona Beach International Airport as of Nov. 20
- MARC opening at John H. Dickerson Community Center, 308 S. Dr. MLK Jr. Blvd.
- County lists Nov. 27 opening (1-7 p.m.) but state site lists Nov. 28; hours 7 a.m.-7 p.m. daily through Dec. 11
- Services include housing, financial assistance, emergency supplies, social services and referrals
