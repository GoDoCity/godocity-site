## Why it matters

A locally built WWII vessel returning to within 600 feet of where it was constructed is a feel-good, shareable piece of hidden local history. The planned $500,000 memorial gives readers a cause to follow and support. It connects Volusia residents to an untold wartime heritage story.

## What happened

The Tiger, a 140-ton WWII tugboat built in DeLand in 1944, has returned to Lake Beresford after decades abroad, including time in Sweden. DeLand Historic Trust president Dan Friend brought it back to the US three years ago; volunteers moved it to the lake on Oct. 31, 2026. The Trust now aims to raise $500,000 to build a memorial with a bronze plaque and mural honoring DeLand's WWII tugboats. A fundraising event was held Nov. 11 at American Legion Post 259 in DeBary.
