## Why it matters

The Halifax Art Festival is one of Daytona's signature downtown events, drawing crowds to Beach Street for juried art. Readers will want to know both that it's happening and how the road closures affect their weekend travel. It's a fun, go-see-it event with a practical driving-impact angle.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced the Halifax Art Festival returns to downtown this weekend, Nov. 6-8, with temporary road closures along Beach Street and Magnolia Avenue. Beach Street will be closed from ISB south to Orange Avenue and north to Bay Street, and Magnolia Avenue between Beach Street and Palmetto. ISB stays open to through traffic.

## Key facts

- Halifax Art Festival runs Fri Nov 6 through Sun Nov 8, 2026
- Beach Street closed from ISB to Orange Ave and north to Bay Street
- Magnolia Ave closed between Beach Street and Palmetto Ave
- ISB remains open eastbound and westbound
