## What happened

Epifanio IX Ramos Gaminde of DeBary was arrested and charged with first-degree murder in the death of his wife, Maria Gaminde, a University High School teacher reported missing in January 2024. Investigators found no record she left the U.S. despite her husband's claims she went abroad, and a niece in the Philippines admitted to faking communications and purchases at his direction. Maria's body has not been recovered; Gaminde is held without bond with an ICE detainer.
