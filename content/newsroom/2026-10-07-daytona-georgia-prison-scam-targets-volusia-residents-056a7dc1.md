## Why it matters

Impersonation scams are hitting local residents and this one cost a DeLand woman $15,000. A clear public-safety warning with a prominent local voice (Sheriff Chitwood) gives readers practical protection advice.

## What happened

Volusia County Sheriff's Office arrested Vera Faust, 43, of Athens, Georgia, in connection with a scam in which callers impersonated sheriff's deputies and told a DeLand woman to pay to avoid arrest. She sent $15,000; detectives recovered most of the funds. Investigators believe Georgia prison inmates are running the operation using outside associates. It's at least the third arrest tied to the scheme.

## Key facts

- Vera Faust, 43, of Athens, GA, arrested and extradited to Volusia County on fraud charges
- DeLand woman sent scammers $15,000; most funds recovered
- Scammers impersonated Volusia County Sheriff's Office members
- At least third arrest tied to Georgia prison inmate scam operation
- Prior arrest: Annie Ashley, 61, in a fake jury duty scam about a week earlier
