## Why it matters

Fee and tax changes hit residents' wallets directly, meeting the real-money exception to the fun filter. NSB homeowners would want to know about a stormwater fee jump and a debt-service millage increase, plus how to weigh in.

## What happened

The New Smyrna Beach City Commission set its first of two budget hearings for 5:30 p.m. Wednesday, Sept. 9, to review a proposed $200.7M city budget and $75.1M utilities budget. Proposals include raising the annual stormwater fee from $125 to $150, holding the operating millage at 4.673 mills, and increasing the debt-service millage (for buying environmentally sensitive land in the Turnbull Creek watershed) from 0.1115 to 0.1695 mills. A second hearing is set for Sept. 23.

## Key facts

- First budget hearing 5:30 p.m. Wed., Sept. 9; final hearing Sept. 23
- Stormwater fee proposed to rise $125 to $150/year
- Operating millage held at 4.673 mills
- Debt-service millage up from 0.1115 to 0.1695 mills for Turnbull Creek watershed land
- Proposed city budget $200.7M; utilities budget $75.1M
- Public can speak up to 3 minutes at 214 Sams Ave.
