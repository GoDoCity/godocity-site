## Why it matters

The Tiger is a genuine piece of DeLand's WWII homefront history, built on Lake Beresford in 1944 and now back within 600 feet of its original site. It's a feel-good heritage story with real local color, plus an urgent race to save the vessel before it sinks and a fundraising push residents can act on.

## What happened

Volunteers with the DeLand Historic Trust moved the 140-ton tugboat Tiger, built on Lake Beresford in 1944, to its original lake last week after it was brought back from Sweden in 2022 and spent time stuck in St. Johns River mud. Historian Dan Friend warns the 82-year-old vessel could sink at any time and the group is raising $500,000 to buy land, pull it from the water, and create a memorial museum honoring WWII-era tugboats.

## Key facts

- Tiger is a 140-ton tugboat built on Lake Beresford, DeLand in 1944
- Built by ~500 workers on day/night shifts after Pearl Harbor, served Allied ops in Europe
- Returned from Sweden in 2022, moved to Lake Beresford last week
- Now within 600 feet of its original build site, still has original engine
- DeLand Historic Trust seeks $500,000 to buy land and remove it from water
- Some items kept at DeLand Memorial Hospital and Veterans Museum
