## Why it matters

This is a critical public-impact story: ongoing flooding, multiple high-water rescues and FEMA-provided facilities affecting a Volusia neighborhood just before Thanksgiving. Residents face weeks of hardship with warnings about alligators and wastewater backups. It qualifies under the safety exception despite its negative tone.

## What happened

Stone Island, a ~182-home Volusia neighborhood near the St. Johns River, has endured two high-water rescues in two days, including a rescue of an 8-year-old boy in a wheelchair and an infant. Fire Rescue used high-water vehicles to reach stranded couples. FEMA brought portable showers and bathrooms, and residents have been told not to use home drains due to wastewater backup risk. The river has not crested, and more flooding is expected Wednesday and Thursday.
