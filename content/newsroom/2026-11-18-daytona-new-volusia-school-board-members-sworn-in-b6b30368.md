## Why it matters

The Volusia County School Board sets policy and budget for every public school in the area, so new leadership and two ousted incumbents signal potential shifts affecting families across the region. New chair Krista Goodrich takes over leadership from Ruben Colón.

## What happened

On Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, three members were sworn in to the Volusia County School Board. Brenda Beery unseated incumbent Jamie Haynes in District 1, Grace Kellermeier defeated incumbent Jessie Thompson in District 3, and Ruben Colón won a third term in District 5. Krista Goodrich was named board chair, with Donna Brosemer as vice chair.

## Key facts

- Beery beat incumbent Haynes 59% in District 1 (DeLand, DeBary, Orange City area)
- Kellermeier beat incumbent Thompson in District 3 (NSB, Edgewater, Port Orange area)
- Colón won third term in District 5 (Deltona area)
- Krista Goodrich chosen as new board chair; Donna Brosemer vice chair
- Sworn in Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026
