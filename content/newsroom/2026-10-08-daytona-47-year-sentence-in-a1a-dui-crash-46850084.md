## What happened

Scott March, 64, was sentenced Wednesday to 47 years in prison after pleading no contest to vehicular homicide and DUI manslaughter in the Nov. 1, 2025 deaths of Thomas and Julia Lauck on State Road A1A. Prosecutors said he reached 147 mph and had a 0.13% BAC before slamming into the couple's car in a no-passing zone.
