## Why it matters

Significant same-day road closures on Atlantic Avenue, Peninsula Drive and Earl Street will snarl beachside traffic and affect anyone driving, working or heading out tonight. Multiple agencies are assisting, signaling a high-profile visitor. Readers need this to plan their Friday.

## What happened

Daytona Beach Police announced significant traffic impacts Friday, Oct. 30 for a scheduled dignitary visit. Atlantic Avenue closes between Oakridge Blvd and Main Street 5-9 p.m.; Earl Street closes starting 8 a.m.; Peninsula Drive closes between Main and Ora streets starting 4 p.m. Additional undisclosed route closures will occur, and traffic impacts begin as early as 7 a.m.

## Key facts

- Atlantic Ave closed Oakridge Blvd to Main St, 5-9 p.m.
- Earl St closed to vehicles from 8 a.m.
- Peninsula Dr closed Main St to Ora St from 4 p.m.
- Traffic impacts begin as early as 7 a.m.
- Multiple law enforcement agencies assisting
- Travel route itself not disclosed
