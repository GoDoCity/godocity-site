## Why it matters

A local high school band earning an invitation-only spot in London's New Year's Day Parade is a genuine community point of pride, the kind of feel-good hometown-kids-do-good story that resonates and gets shared. Families can watch on PBS or livestream, giving readers a reason to tune in.

## What happened

University High School's band, the Titan Regiment, is traveling to London to march in the New Year's Day Parade on Jan. 1, 2027. The group of 155 (including 90 students) departs Orlando Dec. 27 and returns Jan. 3, performing Elton John's 'Saturday Night's Alright' along a route passing Buckingham Palace and ending at 10 Downing Street. It's the school's second trip; the band also performed in 2024.

## Key facts

- 155 travelers including 90 students depart Orlando Dec. 27, return Jan. 3
- Band performs Elton John's 'Saturday Night's Alright' along the parade route
- Invitation-only event; second UHS trip, director Koskoski's third
- Cost roughly $4,000 per traveler
- Parade starts 1 p.m. London time (6 a.m. ET); viewable on PBS and londonparade.com
