## Why it matters

Neighborhood traffic safety is a relatable, local-impact issue many Volusia residents face, and this story shows newly-elected Deltona leaders acting on resident complaints. It also features a notable back-and-forth with Sheriff Chitwood, which could drive conversation.

## What happened

A Staten Drive resident, Jeff Ensminger, complained of chronic speeding and near-misses in his Deltona neighborhood, including a close call last Friday. After he emailed city and county leaders, newly-elected Mayor Maritza Avila-Vazquez asked the city manager on Monday to begin the speed-bump process, which typically involves a traffic study and coordination with the Sheriff's Office. New Commissioner Brandy Lee White called it a top priority, while Sheriff Mike Chitwood pushed back on criticism of enforcement.
