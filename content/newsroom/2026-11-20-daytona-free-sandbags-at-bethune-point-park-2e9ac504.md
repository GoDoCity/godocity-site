## Why it matters

Flooding is a recurring worry in low-lying Daytona Beach neighborhoods, and free sandbags are a tangible, time-sensitive resource residents can act on today. This meets the critical public-impact exception — real protection for homes ahead of possible localized flooding.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach is distributing free sandbags to residents to prepare for heavy rainfall and possible flooding. Since Wednesday, more than 4,000 sandbags have been filled. Distribution runs Thursday Nov. 19 and Friday Nov. 20 until 5 p.m. each day at Bethune Point Park, 11 Bellevue Ave., with a limit of 10 sandbags per vehicle. Residents must bring their own shovels.

## Key facts

- More than 4,000 sandbags filled since Wednesday
- Available Thursday Nov. 19 and Friday Nov. 20 until 5 p.m.
- Location: Bethune Point Park, 11 Bellevue Ave., enter from Bellevue Avenue
- Limit 10 sandbags per vehicle; bring your own shovel
- Limited city staff available to assist
