## Why it matters

A vacant 11.6-acre beachside parcel could become a public park instead of 30-40 new homes, a meaningful land-use decision residents clearly care about. It touches development pressure, green space, and $2.5M in city spending.

## What happened

Ormond Beach commissioners are considering a $2.5M purchase of the former Osceola Elementary School site, closed since 2021, to preserve the 11.6 acres as public use rather than let it be sold for residential development. The contract was on Wednesday night's commission agenda. The parks master plan flagged a need for more beachside recreation space; park design and timeline are undetermined.

## Key facts

- Former Osceola Elementary, closed 2021, sits on 11.6 acres
- Purchase price is $2.5 million
- Site could otherwise yield 30-40 homes
- Parks master plan cites need for beachside recreation space
- Contract was on Wednesday Nov. 4 commission agenda
- Park design and timeline not yet determined
