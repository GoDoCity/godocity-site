## Why it matters

New Smyrna voters approved up to $15 million to preserve land, and now the city is considering opening those 251.6 acres to the public with trails and paddle launches. That's a tangible outdoor-recreation payoff for residents and visitors, and a public workshop lets residents shape the plans.

## What happened

Since 2018, NSB has acquired 251.6 acres in the Turnbull Creek watershed using voter-approved conservation funds. The land preservation committee expressed interest in adding passive public access like hiking trails and kayak/canoe launches. A public workshop is set for 3 p.m. Oct. 21 at the Hathaway Commission Chambers, featuring early concepts like a Bay Drive launch.

## Key facts

- 251.6 acres acquired in Turnbull Creek watershed since 2018
- 2018 referendum: 75%+ approved up to $15M for conservation land
- Concepts include hiking trails, canoe/kayak launches, passive recreation
- Public workshop 3 p.m. Wed., Oct. 21 at 214 Sams Ave.
- Presentation by Natural Resources Manager Tony Miller
