## Why it matters

Free sandbag distribution signals a flooding threat and offers residents practical, time-sensitive help protecting their homes. This is exactly the kind of public-impact info readers act on quickly.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach is distributing free sandbags to residents from noon to 5 p.m. Tuesday, Nov. 17, and 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Wednesday, Nov. 18, at Bethune Point Park, 11 Bellevue Ave. Residents must bring their own shovels, with a limit of 10 sandbags per vehicle and limited city staff to assist.

## Key facts

- Free sandbags at Bethune Point Park, 11 Bellevue Ave.
- Available noon-5 p.m. Nov. 17 and 8 a.m.-5 p.m. Nov. 18
- Limit of 10 sandbags per vehicle
- Residents must bring their own shovels
- Limited city personnel on hand to assist
