## Why it matters

The story exposes how Florida's owner-based lien rules can rope innocent property owners into six-figure debts and foreclosure threats—a cautionary tale for any Volusia homebuyer. It involves veterans, a jailed attorney, and a city declining to resolve, giving it strong community-interest and watchdog appeal.

## What happened

Several veterans in Deltona are fighting the city over $125,000 in decade-old code enforcement liens tied to a former owner's properties on Eden Drive and Tulip Street. Because Florida liens attach to the owner rather than just the violating property, current owners with no connection to the original minor violations now face foreclosure. The situation is further tangled by attorney Marc Brown, now jailed for money laundering and grand theft, who allegedly stole over $260,000 of the buyers' closing funds, leaving money stuck in the court registry. The city declined to comment, citing ongoing litigation.

## Key facts

- $125,000 in liens originated from minor violations charged at a daily rate over a decade
