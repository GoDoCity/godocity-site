## Why it matters

Flooding making roads impassable is exactly the kind of critical public-impact news that affects where residents can safely drive. New Smyrna Beach is within GoDoDaytona's coverage area, and readers need to know which roads are compromised and how to get help.

## What happened

New Smyrna Beach Police urged residents to stay home as flooding created dangerous, potentially impassable road conditions across the city. Officials warned that roads may look safe but aren't, told drivers never to cross standing water or bypass barricades, and said emergency crews were monitoring conditions. Residents whose homes are threatened can call the non-emergency line at 386-424-2000, or 911 for immediate danger.
