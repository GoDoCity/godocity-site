## Why it matters

A coastal flood warning tied to rough surf and strong winds is flooding neighborhood streets in Flagler Beach through early Thursday — the same weather pattern could affect Volusia's coastal communities to the south. Residents care about rip currents, beach conditions and street flooding even just north of Ormond.

## What happened

A coastal flood warning remains in effect for parts of Flagler Beach through early Thursday morning due to high pressure to the north and low pressure to the south producing rough surf and strong winds. Flooding left some neighborhood streets (South 23rd Street, South Flagler Avenue) impassable, with barricades placed and some residents unable to drive home. The city had not responded to WESH's inquiry.
