## Why it matters

The attack happened at the Daytona Beach Boardwalk during Daytona 500 weekend, one of the area's biggest tourist draws, raising questions about safety at a signature local destination. The family's unanswered questions about police handling of the suspect beforehand add a public-accountability angle that resonates with residents and visitors.

## What happened

Jermaine Long was sentenced Thursday to 45 years in prison after entering no contest pleas to eight charges across four cases, including attempted second-degree murder and aggravated battery. The lead case stemmed from the Feb. 14, 2026 slashing of 13-year-old Sullivan Clarke near the Daytona Beach Boardwalk, which required 13 stitches. The family negotiated a plea to spare the boy from testifying and said they are satisfied with the sentence but still have questions about police conduct prior to the attack.
