## Why it matters

A hands-on nature program for elementary-age kids gives Ormond Beach families a low-cost, educational outing during fall. These seasonal, kid-friendly events are exactly the kind of local 'things to do' GoDoDaytona readers with children look for.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach is hosting 'Owls: Eyes of the Night,' an animal ambassador program for children in grades 1-5, on Friday, October 9, with sessions at 10:00am or 3:30pm. Registration is required.

- Program: 'Owls: Eyes of the Night' animal ambassador day
- For children in grades 1st-5th
- Friday, October 9, sessions at 10:00am and 3:30pm
- Registration required
- Hosted by the City of Ormond Beach
