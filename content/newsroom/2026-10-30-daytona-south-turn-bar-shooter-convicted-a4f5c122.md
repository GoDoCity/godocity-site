## Why it matters

The June 2025 shooting at a Daytona Beach bar wounded three people and shook a local establishment, making the conviction a notable public-safety resolution. ## What happened

Anthony Kaznakoff, 29, was convicted of two counts of attempted second-degree murder with a firearm after a three-day trial before Circuit Judge Elizabeth Blackburn. He fired 17 rounds at South Turn Sports Bar & Grill on June 20, 2025, hitting three people, none fatally. He now faces up to life in prison.
