## Why it matters

The St. Johns River is rising toward its crest and already flooding walkways, boat ramps and threatening homes in western Volusia. This is critical public-impact news: readers need to know where to get sandbags and how to report damage. Flooding risk and evacuation concerns directly affect residents' safety and property.

## What happened

As the St. Johns River nears its crest, Volusia County opened sand and sandbag sites for residents protecting their homes. Water has submerged walkways and boat ramps, and at least one resident near Lake Daisy reported water within inches of entering his home. Lake County also set up a sandbag site at Fire Station No. 10 in Astor, available to Volusia residents. Daytona Beach is urging people to report storm damage through the Volusia County damage portal.
