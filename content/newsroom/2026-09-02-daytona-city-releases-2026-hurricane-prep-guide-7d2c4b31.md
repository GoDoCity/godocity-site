## Why it matters

Hurricane season directly threatens the Daytona area, and residents have faced serious storm impacts in recent years. A city guide on evacuation zones, emergency alerts and supply checklists is genuinely useful public-safety information readers can act on.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach published its 2026 Hurricane Preparedness Guide, a booklet covering emergency notification sign-ups, safety tips, evacuation zone details and a removable supply checklist with key phone numbers. It is available online at the city's StormGuide page.

## Key facts

- Guide published Aug. 21, 2026 for the current Atlantic hurricane season
- Covers emergency notifications, evacuation zones, safety tips and a supply checklist
- Available at www.DaytonaBeach.gov/StormGuide
- Includes removable checklist with important phone numbers
