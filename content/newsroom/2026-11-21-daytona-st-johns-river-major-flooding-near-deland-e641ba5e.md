## Why it matters

Major to near-record flooding on the St. Johns River threatens riverfront communities in west Volusia for weeks, a genuine public-safety and property concern. This is critical-impact news readers along the river and in DeLand/Astor need regardless of its downbeat tone.

## What happened

A Nov. 20 SJRWMD briefing warned the St. Johns River near DeLand will reach major to near-record flood levels, cresting about one to one-and-a-half weeks after Nov. 19 and staying at or above moderate flood stage for roughly a month. Astor faces major flooding potentially lasting one to two months. About 60 billion gallons of floodwater are being stored upstream, and the river's gradual slope keeps levels rising for days after rain ends.
