## Why it matters

Flooding has repeatedly devastated Edgewater homes, and this project aims to protect vulnerable neighborhoods. Residents who lost everything are personally invested, giving the funding decision real human stakes and practical impact on where people live.

## What happened

Edgewater is pursuing Volusia County Transform 386 funding for the 27th Street Stormwater Drainage Improvement Project, which would connect the E-9 Canal to the Silver Palm Canal with two 42-inch culverts. City Council was set to consider a $2.5 million local funding commitment and a budget amendment at its Monday meeting. Affected residents who had their homes flooded voiced support.

## Key facts

- Project connects E-9 Canal to Silver Palm Canal along 27th Street right-of-way
- Includes two 42-inch culverts
- Agenda lists $2.5 million in local match/leveraged funds
- Funding sought through Volusia County's Transform 386 program
- No grant award announced yet; funding details still being confirmed
- Resident Tommy Smith reported 18 inches of water inside his home
