## Why it matters

The loss of the annual Christmas parade and most city events touches residents directly during the holidays and signals a serious fiscal crunch facing Deltona. The $23 million in planned cuts, terminated contracts and canceled vehicle orders point to a broader squeeze on city services that affects thousands of Volusia residents.

## What happened

Deltona announced Saturday it is canceling its annual Christmas parade and most other city events (except two 5K races) to stay fiscally responsible amid expected funding reductions from the newly passed Amendment 3. City Manager Doc Dougherty said the city must cut roughly $12 million next year and another $5 million the following year, totaling more than $23 million by 2030, and is also terminating construction contracts and withdrawing vehicle orders.

## Key facts

- Deltona canceled its annual Christmas parade and most city events except two 5K races
- City must cut about $12M next year, $5M the following, over $23M by 2030
- City Manager Doc Dougherty cited Amendment 3's funding impact
- City is terminating construction contracts and withdrawing vehicle orders
- Amendment 3 passed with 62% voter approval earlier this month
