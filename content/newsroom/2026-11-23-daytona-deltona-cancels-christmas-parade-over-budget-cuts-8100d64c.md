## Why it matters

A canceled Christmas parade is a tangible, emotional hit for Deltona families and a vivid early sign of how Amendment 3's tax changes will squeeze local services countywide. It signals deeper cuts coming and sets a precedent other Volusia cities may follow. Residents who registered or planned to attend need to know.

## What happened

Deltona's city manager Doc Dougherty announced Saturday that the city's Christmas parade, set for Dec. 12, is canceled, citing drastic revenue reductions from the recently passed statewide Amendment 3. All other city-organized events are 'effectively canceled' except two self-supporting 5K runs. Dougherty estimates $12 million in 2027 cuts, $5 million more in 2028, and over $23 million total by 2030, blaming Deltona's reliance on low property values and little commercial development.
