## Why it matters

The Florida Tennis Center is a city-owned recreation facility used by local players, and new leadership could signal changes to programming, tournaments and memberships. It's a modest local-interest item for tennis enthusiasts.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced George T. Henry as the new Tennis Manager at the city-owned Florida Tennis Center. He brings 30-plus years in tennis, recreation and facility management, previously serving as Director of Tennis for Play Tennis Gainesville. He will oversee programming, tournaments, memberships, facility planning and community engagement.
