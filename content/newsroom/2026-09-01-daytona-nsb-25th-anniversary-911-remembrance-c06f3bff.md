## Why it matters

The 25th anniversary of 9/11 is a significant milestone, and this community ceremony offers residents a meaningful way to gather and honor those lost. It brings together local officials, first responders and community groups at the Brannon Center.

## What happened

The City of New Smyrna Beach, along with its fire and police departments, announced a 9/11 remembrance ceremony on Friday, Sept. 11 at the Brannon Center. Doors open at 8 a.m., with the ceremony starting at 8:46 a.m. It will feature remarks from Mayor Fred Cleveland and Fire Chief John Flynn, an honor guard presentation, choir performances, a bell ceremony and moment of silence, plus bagpipe and Taps performances.

## Key facts

- Ceremony is Friday, Sept. 11, 2026 at the Brannon Center
- Doors open 8 a.m.; ceremony begins 8:46 a.m.
- Marks the 25th anniversary of 9/11
- Features Mayor Fred Cleveland, Fire Chief John Flynn, Volusia Sheriff's Honor Guard
- Includes Chisholm High School Alumni Legacy Choir, bell ceremony, moment of silence
