## Why it matters

A nearly 40-year fixture across from Daytona International Speedway has closed for good, leaving Volusia County without a single Hooters. Longtime locals and race fans will notice the loss of a landmark tied to the Speedway's business ecosystem and NASCAR history.

## What happened

The Hooters on International Speedway Boulevard in Daytona Beach permanently closed, the last Hooters in Volusia County. The closure follows Hooters of America's March 2025 Chapter 11 bankruptcy and a wave of national and regional closures. The nearest remaining location is now in Orlando.

## Key facts

- Located on International Speedway Blvd, across from Daytona International Speedway
- Open nearly 40 years; now the last Hooters in Volusia County
- Hooters of America filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy in March 2025
- Hooters sponsored NASCAR entries at Daytona from the 1980s to 2020s
- Nearest remaining location is in Orlando
- Facebook page promoted October specials days before closing
