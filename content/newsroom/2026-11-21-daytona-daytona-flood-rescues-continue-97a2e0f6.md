## Why it matters

Major flooding is directly impacting Daytona Beach residents, with first responders conducting hours of water rescues and officials warning high water could linger for about a week. This is critical public-safety and public-impact news readers need, and it affects where people live, drive and seek shelter.

## What happened

First responders returned to flooded Daytona Beach neighborhoods Saturday morning to rescue stranded residents after rising water continued to threaten the community. Police spent nearly 10 hours Friday conducting waist-deep water rescues, pulling people from homes and cars, with FWC airboats assisting. In Midtown, high water forced officers to help EMS transport patients to Halifax Hospital. Officials expect the high water to remain roughly a week because the bowl-shaped area does not drain.

## Key facts

- FWC airboats assisted pulling residents from homes and cars
- Midtown flooding forced patient transport to Halifax Hospital

- Rescues resumed Saturday morning despite rain stopping
