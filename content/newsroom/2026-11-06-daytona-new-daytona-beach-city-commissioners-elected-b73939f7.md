## Why it matters

City commission makeup shapes decisions on development, budgets and services affecting every Daytona Beach resident. New faces in Zones 3 and 5 signal potential shifts in city priorities, and readers want to know who now represents them.

## What happened

In Tuesday's general election, incumbent Zone 1 Commissioner Monica Paris won reelection and Steve Miller won the Zone 3 seat, succeeding Quanita May. Willie Williams had already won the Zone 5 seat in the August primary, replacing Dannette Henry, who did not seek reelection. The new commissioners will be sworn in at 6 p.m. Wednesday, Nov. 18, at Daytona Beach City Hall.

## Key facts

- Monica Paris reelected in Zone 1
- Steve Miller elected to Zone 3, succeeding Quanita May
- Willie Williams won Zone 5 in August primary, replacing Dannette Henry
- Oath of office set for 6 p.m. Nov. 18 at Daytona Beach City Hall
