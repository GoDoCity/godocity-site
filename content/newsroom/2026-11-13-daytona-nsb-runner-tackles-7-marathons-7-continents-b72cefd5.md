## Why it matters

A local resident is undertaking an extraordinary physical feat to raise $100,000 for a Volusia/Flagler nonprofit mentoring fatherless boys. It's the kind of inspiring hometown story readers love to share and rally behind.

## What happened

New Smyrna Beach resident Casey Sparks is running seven marathons on seven continents in seven days, 184 miles total, to raise $100,000 for No Longer Fatherless, which mentors boys without father figures in Volusia and Flagler counties. He spoke to WESH 2 from Cape Town, South Africa, and tied the cause to losing his own father at 18.
