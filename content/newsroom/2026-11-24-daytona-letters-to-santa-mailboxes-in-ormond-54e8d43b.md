## Why it matters

A charming, family-friendly holiday tradition that gives Ormond Beach kids a free, tangible activity during the season. Parents and grandparents love these low-effort, high-delight seasonal offerings, and it's the kind of wholesome item that performs well on social media.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach set up official North Pole Mailboxes where children can drop letters to Santa and receive a reply, provided they include a wish list and full mailing address. Mailboxes are located at the Nova Community Center and The Casements, open November 25 through December 16, 2026.

## Key facts

- Two mailbox locations: Nova Community Center (440 N. Nova Rd) and The Casements (25 Riverside Dr)
- Open November 25 to December 16, 2026
- Kids must include a wish list and full mailing address to get a reply
- Run by the City of Ormond Beach
