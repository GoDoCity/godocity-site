## Why it matters

A coordinated federal-local crackdown resulting in 120 arrests is a major, unusual public-safety story that affects community safety across the county, not a routine blotter item. It involves prominent officials (Sheriff Chitwood, Chief Young, FBI) and touches Biketoberfest crowds. Residents will care about drug, trafficking and child-predator enforcement in their area.

## What happened

Law enforcement officials announced the results of Operation Fall Harvest, a 30-day crackdown that led to 120 arrests. The FBI, Volusia Sheriff's Office, Daytona Beach PD and Homeland Security partners targeted fugitives, drugs, prostitution and human trafficking. Daytona Beach police ran three operations this fall, including a child-predator sting (six arrests), a drug operation (about nine) and a prostitution operation (about six), some timed around Biketoberfest. Officials said the partnership will continue and include cold-case review with FBI resources.
