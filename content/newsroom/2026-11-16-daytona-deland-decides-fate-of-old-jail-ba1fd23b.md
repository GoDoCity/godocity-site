## Why it matters

The vacant old Volusia County jail has been a debated eyesore in downtown DeLand for decades, and Monday's vote could finally determine whether it becomes a mixed-use community space or gets demolished for a clean slate. Residents, nearby business owners and history buffs all have a stake. The decision carries real stakes because commissioners were told there will be no second vote.

## What happened

DeLand city commissioners were scheduled to vote at 7 p.m. Monday on whether to sell the old jail property as-is for redevelopment or demolish the building and sell the land. The jail has sat vacant since 1987, and the city took possession in 2018, selecting GlassHouse Square for redevelopment. A 2023 rezoning rejection led to a developer lawsuit, and a court order now requires reconsideration.
