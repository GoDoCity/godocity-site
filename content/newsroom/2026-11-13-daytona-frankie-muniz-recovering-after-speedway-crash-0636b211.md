## Why it matters

A celebrity actor-turned-racer crashing at Daytona International Speedway is a high-interest story that ties national attention to the local track. Readers follow Speedway happenings closely, and a dramatic crash with a happy ending (no major injuries) is both buzzy and shareable.

## What happened

Actor and racecar driver Frankie Muniz crashed Wednesday while practicing at Daytona International Speedway for the Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic. He was hospitalized but said in a video on X that he escaped major injury, is sore, and plans to return home to Phoenix before racing again. A spectator described the crash as one of the hardest hits he'd seen, with crews cutting the roof off the car to extract Muniz.

## Key facts

- Crews cut the roof off the Ford Mustang Dark Horse R to extract him
- Muniz credited the car's build for protecting him
- He plans to return home to Phoenix and race again soon
