## Why it matters

Truck traffic and road safety on a narrow county road touches residents who live and drive in the DeLand area. The county is now studying possible improvements, which could lead to real changes. It's a relatable neighborhood-vs-infrastructure story many Volusia residents understand.

## What happened

A Marsh Road resident, Mary Dickinson, says heavy trucks and 18-wheelers diverted onto the narrow two-lane road have damaged her property, caused ground movement, created constant noise, and made the tight curves dangerous. Volusia County said Marsh Road is a classified collector road connecting SR 11 and SR 92 and that it does not restrict truck traffic on county thoroughfares. The county also disputed claims of prior weight-limit signs, saying limits apply only to bridges. County engineers say they are examining potential corridor improvements.

## Key facts

- Marsh Road is a two-lane county collector road connecting SR 11 and SR 92
- Resident Mary Dickinson reports property damage and year-long noise/traffic issues
- County says it does not restrict truck traffic on thoroughfare-classified roads
- County disputes that weight-limit signs ever existed on the road itself
- County Engineer and traffic staff are examining corridor improvements
