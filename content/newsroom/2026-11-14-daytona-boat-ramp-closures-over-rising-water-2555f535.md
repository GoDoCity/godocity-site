## Why it matters

Boating and fishing are huge recreational draws across the Daytona area, and these closures directly affect where residents can get on the water. The safety angle—submerged ramps, hidden debris and deceptive tide swings—gives readers a practical reason to check before heading out. No-wake warnings also matter to waterfront homeowners worried about flooding.

## What happened

Volusia County temporarily closed several boat ramps along the St. Johns River and Intracoastal Waterway due to rising water and hazardous conditions. St. Johns closures stem from heavy rainfall runoff flowing north into the county, with no firm reopening date. Intracoastal closures are tied to the recent king tide cycle, with ramps needing inspection before reopening. Officials urged boaters to follow no-wake restrictions and check ramp status online.
