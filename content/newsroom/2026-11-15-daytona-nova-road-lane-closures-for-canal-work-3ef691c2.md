## Why it matters

Nova Road is a major Port Orange artery, and daytime lane closures for a full week will affect commuters and local business access. This is practical, time-sensitive info drivers can use to avoid delays.

## What happened

FDOT scheduled temporary daytime lane closures on northbound Nova Road between Herbert Street and Big Tree Road from November 16-20, closing the inside and middle lanes 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. for canal maintenance. The outside lane stays open and business/driveway access is maintained.

## Key facts

- Northbound Nova Road between Herbert Street and Big Tree Road
- Closures Nov 16-20, daily 9 a.m.-5 p.m.
- Inside and middle northbound lanes closed; outside lane stays open
- Work is for canal maintenance by FDOT
- Business and driveway access maintained
