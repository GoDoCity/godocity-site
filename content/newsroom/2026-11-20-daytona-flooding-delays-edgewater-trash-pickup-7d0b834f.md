## Why it matters

Flooding and road closures are affecting trash pickup for Edgewater residents, a practical service impact tied to ongoing weather. It signals broader flooding conditions in southern Volusia that readers should know about.

## What happened

The City of Edgewater announced its Refuse Division will continue collection only where roads are safely accessible, meaning residents on flooded or closed roads may see delays due to recent inclement weather and flooding.

## Key facts

- Edgewater Refuse Division collection delayed on flooded/closed roads
- Trucks only travel safely accessible roads
- Cause cited as recent inclement weather and flooding
- Issued by Edgewater Public Works & Utilities Department
