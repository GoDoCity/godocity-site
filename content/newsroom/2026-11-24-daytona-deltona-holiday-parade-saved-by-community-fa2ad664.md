## Why it matters

A beloved holiday tradition was canceled, then revived when residents and groups stepped up — a feel-good community win. But the backstory is serious: Amendment 3 is projected to slash Deltona's property tax revenue by tens of millions, with more cuts coming that could affect services and jobs across the city.

## What happened

Deltona canceled its 2026 Christmas parade citing anticipated revenue losses from voter-approved Amendment 3, but reversed course Monday after community groups offered time and resources to cut costs. The parade will roll along Deltona Boulevard on Saturday, Dec. 12 at 6 p.m. The city warned all other events are effectively canceled except two self-supporting 5Ks, and that significant budget reductions are coming as Amendment 3 expands homestead exemptions starting Jan. 1, 2027.

## Key facts

- 2026 parade was canceled then reinstated after community groups offered help
- Parade set for Saturday, Dec. 12 at 6 p.m. along Deltona Boulevard
- All other city events canceled except two self-supporting 5K races
- County estimates revenue drops of ~$12M (30.3%) at $150K exemption or ~$17.6M (44.5%) at $250K exemption
- About 63% of Deltona's 42,836 parcels have homestead exemptions
- Amendment 3 changes take effect Jan. 1, 2027, first applying to 2027 tax roll
