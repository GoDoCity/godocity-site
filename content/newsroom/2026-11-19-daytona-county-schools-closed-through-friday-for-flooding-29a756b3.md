## Why it matters

School closures and flood risk directly affect thousands of Volusia families planning their week, their commutes and their kids' schedules. With 3-6 inches of rain forecast and potential major flood stage on the St. Johns River, this is critical public-impact news that overrides the fun filter.

## What happened

Volusia County Schools were closed through Friday, Nov. 20 due to flooding. The NWS forecast 3-6 inches of rain, with localized totals of 8-12 inches possible, especially on the coast. A flood watch is in effect until Friday evening. Southern Volusia and areas near Lake Harney and the St. Johns River face the greatest risk, with the river potentially reaching major flood stage over the weekend. Soil saturation in southern Volusia is estimated at 75-85%. Sandbags are available via the county.
