## Why it matters

These are practical, free, time-sensitive community service events happening within days in two core coverage areas. Residents can safely dispose of paint, batteries, pool chemicals and other hazardous items they can't put in regular trash. It's genuinely useful information readers can act on immediately.

## What happened

Volusia County is hosting two free household hazardous waste collection events: Friday, Oct. 9 at Ormond Beach Public Works and Saturday, Oct. 10 at New Smyrna Beach Public Works, both 8 a.m. to noon. Accepted items include paint, batteries, pool chemicals, pesticides, motor oil and up to 10 fluorescent lamps. Proof of Volusia residency required; residential use only.

## Key facts

- Friday Oct. 9, 8 a.m.-noon at Ormond Beach Public Works, 501 N. Orchard St.
- Saturday Oct. 10, 8 a.m.-noon at NSB Public Works, 124 Industrial Park Ave.
- Accepts paint, batteries, pool chemicals, pesticides, motor oil, gasoline, up to 10 fluorescent lamps
- Proof of Volusia County residency required; residential only
- Year-round free disposal at Tomoka Landfill (Port Orange) and West Volusia Transfer Station (DeLand)
