## What happened

Michael Hightower, 40, of Daytona Beach, was arrested after allegedly attacking a 79-year-old man and 74-year-old woman outside Daytona College on Sunday, September 20, hitting the woman with his vehicle and beating both, leaving them in critical condition on life support. Around 3:20 a.m. Sept. 21, while under guard at a hospital, he allegedly fought an Ormond Beach officer, grabbed the officer's gun, and shot him in the leg. Hightower faces two counts of attempted felony murder plus battery and assault on law enforcement charges. Officer Austin Long is expected to recover.
