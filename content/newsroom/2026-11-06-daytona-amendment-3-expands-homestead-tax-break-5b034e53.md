## Why it matters

This is real money in homeowners' pockets — the homestead exemption triples to $150,000 in 2027 and reaches $250,000 by 2028, cutting property tax bills for Volusia-area homeowners. It also raises real concerns about funding for local police, fire and services in Daytona Beach and surrounding cities. The money-in-pocket plus public-safety-funding angle makes this cross the local-impact exception despite being a statewide measure.

## What happened

On Tuesday, Florida voters approved Amendment 3, clearing the 60% threshold with 62.0% of the vote (99% counted per AP). It expands the homestead exemption on non-school taxes from $50,000 to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028. New residents who didn't establish residency by end of 2026 face a five-year waiting period. Opponents warn of lost revenue for local governments and public safety agencies.
