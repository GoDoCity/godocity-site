## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly cultural evening in downtown Ormond Beach that gives residents and visitors a reason to explore local galleries, studios and the historic Casements. It's an easy 'go do' event with a complimentary shuttle, exactly the kind of outing GoDoDaytona readers enjoy.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced the return of the Historic Ormond Beach Art Walk on Saturday, November 7, 2026, from 3-7 p.m., in partnership with the Ormond Beach Arts District. Galleries, studios, museums and businesses will showcase artwork, live demonstrations and music. The Casements will offer complimentary guided tours of John D. Rockefeller's winter home at 4:30 and 5:30 p.m., and a free shuttle will loop between participating locations.

## Key facts

- Saturday, November 7, 2026, 3-7 p.m. in downtown Historic Ormond Beach
- Partnership with the Ormond Beach Arts District
- Galleries, studios, museums and businesses feature art, live demos and music
- Free guided Casements (Rockefeller winter home) tours at 4:30 and 5:30 p.m.
- Complimentary AdventHealth shuttle loops every 25-30 minutes, 3-7 p.m.
