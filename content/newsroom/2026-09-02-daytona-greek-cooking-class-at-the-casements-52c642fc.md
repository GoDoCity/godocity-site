## Why it matters

The Casements is a beloved Ormond Beach landmark, and a hands-on Greek cooking class is exactly the kind of local activity residents might book or share. It offers a fun, tangible thing to do on a specific date.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced a hands-on Greek cooking class led by Chef Elaine Pitenis at The Casements on Thursday, September 24, from 4 to 7:30 p.m. Participants will prepare and enjoy a full Greek-inspired dinner including chicken souvlaki, rice pilaf, spanakopita, Greek salad and baklava. Registration is required.

## Key facts

- Class is Thursday, September 24, 4 to 7:30 p.m.
- Held at The Casements in Ormond Beach
- Led by Chef Elaine Pitenis
- Menu: chicken souvlaki, rice pilaf, spanakopita, Greek salad, baklava
- Registration required via city link
