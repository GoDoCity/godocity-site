## Why it matters

Boating and fishing are big parts of life along the Halifax River, and improved public launch access is a tangible amenity for Port Orange residents and visitors. The project was almost entirely grant-funded, sparing local taxpayers most of the cost. Storm-resilient docks matter in a hurricane-prone region.

## What happened

The Frank Scofield Boat Ramps at Causeway Park in Port Orange reopened after a $1.8 million upgrade, celebrated with a ribbon-cutting. The project rebuilt docks to be more storm-resilient and improved the ramp for easier launching. Funding came from $1.6 million in grants (ECHO, FIND, CDBG), reducing the taxpayer burden.

## Key facts

- Frank Scofield Boat Ramps at Causeway Park reopened after upgrades
- Total project cost $1.8 million
- $1.6 million covered by ECHO, FIND and CDBG grants
- Docks rebuilt to better withstand storms
- Ribbon-cutting held with local, state and federal officials
