## Why it matters

A fun, low-key community happening that gives residents a reason to swing by City Hall and grab a bite before the commission meeting. It's the kind of light, shareable local event GoDoDaytona readers enjoy and can act on.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced 'City Hall Eats,' bringing food trucks to City Hall from 4-8 p.m. Wednesday, Sept. 23. Two vendors, Cool Sal's Pizza Co and Charm City Treats Hun, were selected via random drawing announced Sept. 3. Future dates will be scheduled, and interested vendors can apply to enter future drawings.

## Key facts

- Event runs 4-8 p.m. Wednesday, Sept. 23 at City Hall, 301 S. Ridgewood Ave.
- Vendors selected: Cool Sal's Pizza Co and Charm City Treats Hun
- Vendors chosen via random drawing announced Sept. 3
- City Commission meeting is at 6 p.m. same day
- Vendors pay a $75 permit fee; future dates to be announced
