## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly evening event with local food trucks is exactly the kind of weekend plan GoDoDaytona readers like to grab. It's hyper-local to Daytona Beach and easy to act on.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced Food Truck Friday on Friday, Oct. 9, from 5 to 8 p.m. at the Florida Tennis Center, 1 Deuce Court. Confirmed trucks include Fizzy Flamingo, Tropisoul, JT's Wiener Wagon, Side of the Road Jerk Chicken and Hangry Eats.

## Key facts

- Friday, Oct. 9, 5-8 p.m.
- Location: Florida Tennis Center, 1 Deuce Court
- Confirmed trucks: Fizzy Flamingo, Tropisoul, JT's Wiener Wagon, Side of the Road Jerk Chicken, Hangry Eats
- Hosted by the City of Daytona Beach
