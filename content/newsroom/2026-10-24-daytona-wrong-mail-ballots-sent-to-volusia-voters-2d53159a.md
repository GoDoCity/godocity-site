## Why it matters

About 350 Volusia County voters received incorrect vote-by-mail ballots ahead of the Nov. 3 general election, raising concerns about whether affected residents' votes will count correctly. This is real public-impact news that readers in north Volusia need to act on immediately. Election integrity and the mechanics of casting a valid vote affect a broad swath of residents.

## What happened

Volusia Supervisor of Elections Lisa Lewis said a vendor error caused roughly 350 voters in the Congressional District 6 area (north Volusia, east and west side) to receive mail ballots containing contests for the wrong city, tied to a single mailing delivered Oct. 7. Of those, only two had been completed and counted, and those votes will stand as cast. Replacement ballots were to be sent Friday, and affected voters are urged to call the elections office at 386-736-5930 or vote in person during early voting or on Election Day, Nov. 3.
