## Why it matters

A free, convenient way for residents to safely get rid of paints, chemicals and other items they can't put in the trash. It's practical, useful civic information with an environmental payoff that locals can act on.

## What happened

The City of Daytona Beach announced a free Household Hazardous Waste Collection Day from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 7, at the Public Works facility, 950 Bellevue Ave. Proof of Daytona Beach residency is required and there is no cost to drop off accepted items.

## Key facts

- Saturday, Nov. 7, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.
- Public Works facility, 950 Bellevue Ave.
- Free, but proof of Daytona Beach residency required
- Covers items that can't go in regular trash or recycling
