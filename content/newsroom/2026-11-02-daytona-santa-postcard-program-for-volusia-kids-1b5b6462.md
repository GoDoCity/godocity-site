## Why it matters

A charming, free seasonal program parents can act on, exactly the kind of feel-good holiday item that drives newsletter shares. It's practical, time-sensitive, and county-wide so it reaches all of GoDoDaytona's core communities.

## What happened

Volusia County's Parks, Recreation and Culture Division is again running its annual holiday postcard program, where kids 12 and younger can receive a personalized postcard signed by Santa. Parents email the child's full name and mailing address to elves@volusia.gov by Dec. 11.

## Key facts

- Program run by Volusia County Parks, Recreation and Culture Division
- Open to children ages 12 and younger
- Email child's full name and mailing address to elves@volusia.gov
- Deadline to sign up is Dec. 11
- Postcards are personalized and signed by Santa
