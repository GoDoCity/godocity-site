## Why it matters

A free, family-friendly downtown event gives Ormond Beach residents and visitors a fun weekend outing showcasing local galleries, live art, music and history. The complimentary shuttle and free Casements tours make it accessible and easy to enjoy. It's exactly the kind of community celebration GoDoDaytona readers love to plan around.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced the Historic Ormond Beach Art Walk will take place Saturday, October 3, from 3-7 p.m. in partnership with the Ormond Beach Arts District. Local galleries, studios, museums and businesses will showcase artwork, live demonstrations and music. The Casements will offer complimentary guided tours of John D. Rockefeller's winter home at 4:30 and 5:30 p.m., and a free AdventHealth shuttle will loop between participating locations every 25-30 minutes.

## Key facts

- Saturday, October 3, 2026, 3-7 p.m.
- Held in Historic downtown Ormond Beach with the Ormond Beach Arts District
- Free guided tours of The Casements (Rockefeller's winter home) at 4:30 and 5:30 p.m.
- Complimentary AdventHealth shuttle loops every 25-30 minutes, 3-7 p.m.
- Features galleries, studios, museums, live demos and music
