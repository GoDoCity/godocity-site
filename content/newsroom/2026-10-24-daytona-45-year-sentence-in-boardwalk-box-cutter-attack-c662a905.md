## Why it matters

The Daytona Beach Boardwalk is a central tourist and community spot, and a violent attack on a 13-year-old there drew significant local concern. The conclusion of the case with a lengthy sentence is a notable public-safety resolution tied to a prominent location.

## What happened

Jermaine Long, 44, of Daytona Beach was sentenced Thursday to 45 years in prison after a no contest plea to aggravated battery and attempted second-degree murder. He was accused of slashing 13-year-old Sullivan Clarke across the neck with a box cutter near the Daytona Beach Boardwalk on February 14. The boy required 13 stitches. Long also pleaded in three other cases and received a prison releasee reoffender designation on 30 years of the sentence.
