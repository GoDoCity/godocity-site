## Why it matters

Flooding near Spruce Creek and Dunlawton is a recurring hazard that affects a major Port Orange corridor and could threaten drivers. A first-person warning to 'turn around, don't drown' has real public-safety value during wet conditions.

## What happened

Port Orange resident Michael Krum said his Kia Soul stalled in floodwater near Spruce Creek and Dunlawton while running errands, with water reaching nearly to his windows. Flooding was also reported along Tomoka Farms Road and near Carousel Acres.

## Key facts

- Car stalled in floodwater near Spruce Creek and Dunlawton in Port Orange
- Driver Michael Krum said water rose nearly to the windows
- Additional flooding reported on Tomoka Farms Road and near Carousel Acres
- Driver's warning: 'Go back home'
