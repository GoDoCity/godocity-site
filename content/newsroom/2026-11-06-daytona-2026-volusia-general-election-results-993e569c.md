## Why it matters

These results decide who leads Volusia County, its school board and nearly every city commission in the Daytona area, plus charter amendments and tax-related referendums that affect residents directly. Readers across Daytona Beach, Ormond Beach, Port Orange and beyond will want to know who won and which measures passed.

## What happened

Volusia County released results of the 2026 general election, with 238,164 of 401,708 registered voters casting ballots for 59% turnout. Winners include Nancy Miller (County Council At-Large), Wendy Anderson (District 1), Danny Robins (District 3) and J. David Sosa (District 5). In Daytona Beach, incumbent Monica Paris held Zone 1 and Steve Miller won Zone 3. New Smyrna Beach re-elected Mayor Fred Cleveland; Deltona chose Maritza Vazquez as mayor. Multiple county and city charter amendments and referendums also passed or failed.

## Key facts

- 59% turnout: 238,164 of 401,708 registered voters
- J. David Sosa unseated incumbent David Santiago in County Council District 5 by a narrow margin
- Daytona Beach: Monica Paris held Zone 1, Steve Miller won Zone 3
- Ormond Beach: Jennifer Bright (Zone 2) and Amber Bobak (Zone 4, beat incumbent Briley) won
- New Smyrna Beach re-elected Mayor Fred Cleveland; Deltona elected Maritza Vazquez mayor
- Port Orange amendments passed overwhelmingly (88-90%); several county amendments passed or failed
