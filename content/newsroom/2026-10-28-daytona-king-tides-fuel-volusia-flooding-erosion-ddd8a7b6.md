## Why it matters

King tides and strong winds are driving beach erosion and coastal flooding along Volusia's shoreline, exposing seawalls and raising safety concerns at beach access points. This affects beachgoers, waterfront residents and anyone heading to the coast during the holiday weekend. It's real public-impact weather news with practical takeaways like sandbag locations.

## What happened

A combination of king tides and strong winds caused flooding and beach erosion across Central Florida's coastal counties. In Volusia, the tides stripped sand from the shoreline and exposed the seawall at places like 27th Avenue Beachfront Park in New Smyrna Beach. County officials are monitoring but won't know the full extent until the tidal event ends later in the week. Flooding also pushed inland along the St. Johns River in Astor, where a self-service sandbag site is available. Conditions are expected to improve into the holiday weekend.
