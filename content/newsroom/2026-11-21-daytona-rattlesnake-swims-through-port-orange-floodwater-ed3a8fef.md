## Why it matters

The viral clip is a vivid reminder of the dangers lurking in this week's historic flooding across Volusia County, where multiple communities declared local states of emergency. Residents facing knee-deep floodwaters need to know wildlife hazards are real. With the St. Johns River forecast to reach major or record crest, flood risks continue.

## What happened

A Port Orange resident, Tricia Dugan, recorded video of a rattlesnake swimming through floodwater right outside her home as she tried to leave for work in knee-deep water. Port Orange was among several Volusia communities under local states of emergency after days of heavy rain. Rain totals reached nearly 20 inches in parts of the county, and the St. Johns River near DeLand is forecast to hit major crest around Tuesday.
