## Why it matters

A state of emergency and forecasted multi-week St. Johns River flooding is critical public-impact news that directly affects Volusia residents' safety, property and travel. This meets the public-impact exception despite not being 'fun' news.

## What happened

On Wednesday, Nov. 4, Gov. Ron DeSantis expanded a flooding state of emergency originally issued Monday for seven counties to also include Seminole, Lake and Volusia Counties. The order cites widespread heavy rainfall and consequential flooding, and notes NWS forecasts that the St. Johns River is expected to flood, possibly lasting several weeks.

## Key facts

- DeSantis expanded the emergency order to Volusia on Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026
- Original order Monday covered seven counties including Brevard
- St. Johns River expected to flood for potentially several weeks per NWS
- Added counties: Seminole, Lake, Volusia
