## Why it matters

The Loop is one of Ormond Beach's most beloved scenic natural areas, and these free classes and plant sales give residents a hands-on way to green their own yards with native species. It's a low-cost, feel-good community activity with clear calendar hooks readers can act on.

## What happened

The City of Ormond Beach announced its fall ReGrow the Loop native plant class and sale schedule at the EDC, with sessions on Saturday 10/10, 11/14 and 12/5, each from 10am to 12pm.

## Key facts

- Three fall sessions: 10/10, 11/14, 12/5
- Each runs 10am-12pm
- Held at the EDC in Ormond Beach
- Part of the ReGrow the Loop native plant program
