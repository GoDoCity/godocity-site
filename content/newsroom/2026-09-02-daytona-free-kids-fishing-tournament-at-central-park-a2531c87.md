## Why it matters

Family-friendly free events are exactly the kind of local activity GoDoDaytona readers with kids love to plan around. A catch-and-release tournament at Central Park is a wholesome, shareable community event.

## What happened

Ormond Beach Parks and Recreation announced the 'Reel in the Fun' free catch-and-release kids fishing tournament, set for Saturday, February 13, 2027 at Central Park. Check-in starts at 8 AM, fishing at 9 AM, and registration is required.

## Key facts

- Free catch-and-release kids fishing tournament
- Saturday, February 13, 2027 at Central Park, Ormond Beach
- Check-in 8 AM, fishing begins 9 AM
- Registration required; info at (386) 610-0790
